# Master Digital Marketing Taxonomy — Overview

## Design Rules

- Hierarchy: Level 1 → Level 2 → Level 3 → Level 4 (Level 5 rare)
- Lifecycle coverage: Business → Market → Customer → Strategy → Brand → Offer → GTM → Acquisition → Conversion → Retention → Advocacy → Measurement → Optimization → Automation → AI → Scale
- First-class international dimensions
- Channel families kept separate where industry data and ops differ (search, social, video, commerce media, programmatic, creators)
- AI Search / AEO / GEO treated as distinct from classic SEO
