# Vyapari / Entrepreneur Centre Model

## Customer segments
Micro trader → small business → SME → growth company → larger enterprise.

## Centre role
Local acquisition, business diagnosis, digital setup, marketing coordination, service fulfilment, specialist referrals and relationship management.

## Network
Merchant ↔ creator ↔ marketer ↔ supplier ↔ employer ↔ customer ↔ larger company/brand ↔ qualified finance/compliance partner.

## Revenue
Service fees, retainers, project margins, referrals/commissions where permitted, premium packages and partner economics.
