# Vyapari / Trade / Entrepreneur Business Solutions Network

## Scope
Small traders, merchants, SMEs and larger entrepreneurs.

Possible services:
- digital presence and marketing
- branding/content
- lead generation
- CRM/digital systems
- bookkeeping/tax/compliance coordination through qualified providers
- business planning
- marketplace/tender support where permitted
- vendor/customer connections
- hiring and training
- funding/investor readiness and introductions through compliant professionals/partners

## Model
Business diagnosis → small solution/demo → paid pilot → recurring growth/services → premium solutions → partner/network integration.

## Boundary
Do not present unlicensed financial/investment/tax/legal advice as Nivy advice. Use qualified/licensed professionals and clear role boundaries.
