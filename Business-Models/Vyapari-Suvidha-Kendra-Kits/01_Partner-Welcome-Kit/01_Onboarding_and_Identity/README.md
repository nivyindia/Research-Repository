# 01 — Onboarding and Identity

## Required Markdown/document inventory
- Company Profile and approved credentials
- Welcome Letter
- Franchise/Retailer Application Form
- Partner Details Form
- Partner KYC Checklist
- Eligibility Criteria
- Partner Agreement / Terms reference
- Onboarding SOP
- Activation Checklist
- Training and access handover checklist

## Validation
Confirm required fields, authorized reviewer, data-handling method, approval steps and current version. Keep blank templates in the repository; keep completed KYC documents in an access-controlled system, not GitHub.
