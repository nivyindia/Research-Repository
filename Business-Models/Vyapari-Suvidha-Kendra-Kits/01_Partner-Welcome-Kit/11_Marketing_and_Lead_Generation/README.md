# 11 — Marketing and Lead Generation

## Required content
- Local marketing plan
- Approved digital/print creatives
- Digital profile setup guidance
- Lead-generation support policy
- Lead assignment and acceptance process
- Partner follow-up responsibility
- Campaign reporting and attribution
- Support scope, eligibility and any fees
- Marketing claim approval process

Explicitly separate confirmed company commitments from optional, conditional or proposed support. Do not promise leads, customer volume or campaign results without an approved written policy.
