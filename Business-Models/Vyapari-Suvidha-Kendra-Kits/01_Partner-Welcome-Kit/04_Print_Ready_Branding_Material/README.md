# 04 — Print-Ready Branding Material

## Required assets
- Approved logo and usage guide
- Visiting card
- Shop board/signage guidance
- Service brochure and flyers
- Posters, banners and standees
- WhatsApp/social creatives
- Franchise/retailer promotion materials
- Print specifications and source/editable files

Use only current approved brand assets. Record version, owner, dimensions and approval status. Do not place unapproved claims or unverified prices on creatives.
