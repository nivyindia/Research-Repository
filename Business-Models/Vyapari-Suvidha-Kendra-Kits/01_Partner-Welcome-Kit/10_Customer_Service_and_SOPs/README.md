# 10 — Customer Service and SOPs

## Required SOPs
- Enquiry and customer qualification
- Quote and price approval
- Consent and document collection
- Document completeness/quality check
- Payment and receipt
- Order entry and handoff
- Status updates and delivery
- Missing documents and delay handling
- Complaint, cancellation and refund
- Escalation and service recovery

Each SOP should state owner, trigger, inputs, steps, expected output, record to update and exception route.
