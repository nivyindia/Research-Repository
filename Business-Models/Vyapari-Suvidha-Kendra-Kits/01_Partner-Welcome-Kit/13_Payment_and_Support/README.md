# 13 — Payment and Support

## Required documents
- Customer payment SOP
- Authorized payment channels and receipt process
- Commission settlement rules
- Refund/cancellation policy
- Reconciliation/dispute process
- Support directory
- Escalation matrix and response ownership
- Service incident/complaint form

Payment methods, settlement timing and refund conditions must be approved and consistent with the agreement. Do not publish personal bank/UPI details until verified and authorized.
