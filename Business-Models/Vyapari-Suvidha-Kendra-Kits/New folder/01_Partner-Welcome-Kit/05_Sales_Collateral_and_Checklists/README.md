# 05 — Sales Collateral and Checklists

## Required assets
- Complete service catalogue
- Short service/pricing snapshot
- Link to full pricing/commission matrix
- Service-wise document checklists
- Customer discovery questions
- Approved sales pitch
- Customer FAQ
- Quotation template
- Customer order/intake form
- Service exclusions and escalation notes

Prioritize completeness and currentness of pricing and service document checklists. Service-specific regulatory requirements must be verified from current authoritative sources.
