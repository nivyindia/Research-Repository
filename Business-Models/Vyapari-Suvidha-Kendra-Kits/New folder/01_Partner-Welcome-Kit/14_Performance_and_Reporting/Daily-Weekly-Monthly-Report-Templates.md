# Daily, Weekly and Monthly Report Templates / दैनिक, साप्ताहिक और मासिक report templates

**स्थिति:** प्रारूप / **Status:** Skeleton — reporting field outline

## Daily / दैनिक
Enquiries, leads, cases opened/closed, pending cases, exceptions, next actions. / Enquiries, leads, खुले/बंद cases, pending cases, exceptions, अगले कदम.

## Weekly / साप्ताहिक
Lead stages, follow-up completion, conversion fields, service bottlenecks, complaints and training needs. / Lead stages, follow-up completion, conversion fields, service bottlenecks, complaints और training needs.

## Monthly / मासिक
Volume trends, service mix, quality issues, case ageing, customer feedback and action register. / Volume trends, service mix, quality issues, case ageing, customer feedback और action register.

## Data rules / डेटा नियम
हर metric की definition, reporting owner, reporting period और source दर्ज करें। / Record each metric definition, reporting owner, reporting period, and source.
