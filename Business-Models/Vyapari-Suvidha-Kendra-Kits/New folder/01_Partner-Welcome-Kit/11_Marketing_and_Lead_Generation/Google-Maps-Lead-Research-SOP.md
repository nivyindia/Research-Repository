# Google Maps Lead Research SOP / Google Maps से lead research SOP

**स्थिति:** प्रारूप / **Status:** Skeleton — research outline, not an approved outreach policy

## Steps / चरण
1. Approved geography and business segments / स्वीकृत geography और business segments
2. Search terms and permitted sources / Search terms और अनुमत sources
3. Business name, public listing URL and research date / Business name, public listing URL और research date
4. Fit criteria, evidence note and confidence / Fit criteria, evidence note और confidence
5. Duplicate check and CRM entry / Duplicate check और CRM entry
6. Contact only through permitted channels and applicable consent rules / केवल अनुमत channels और लागू consent rules के अनुसार संपर्क
7. Opt-out, correction and retention process / Opt-out, correction और retention process

## Pending / लंबित
Applicable platform terms, privacy rules and outreach policy must be reviewed before use. / उपयोग से पहले platform terms, privacy rules और outreach policy की समीक्षा जरूरी है।
