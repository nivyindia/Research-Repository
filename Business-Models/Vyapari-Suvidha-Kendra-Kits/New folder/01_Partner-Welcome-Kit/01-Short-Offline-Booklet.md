**Audience:** Franchisee / Retailer  
**Target length:** 8–10 pages  
**Status:** Content framework / working draft; commercial facts require source verification.

## Page 1 — Welcome and Company
Welcome to Vyapari Suvidha Kendra. This booklet is a quick guide to the partner model, available services, customer handling and support. Insert approved company introduction, logo, official contact details and verified credentials. **[SOURCE/APPROVAL REQUIRED]**

## Page 2 — How the Partner Model Works
Explain the customer journey:
1. Customer enquiry.
2. Understand the service needed.
3. Share the approved price and document checklist.
4. Record the lead/order using the approved process.
5. Collect and check documents securely.
6. Submit the order to the authorized processing team.
7. Track progress and update the customer.
8. Confirm delivery and record feedback.

Clearly distinguish the Retailer/Franchisee's tasks from the company's processing and support responsibilities. **[VERIFY OPERATING MODEL]**

## Page 3 — Getting Started
Summarize application, eligibility, KYC, agreement, any approved fees, training, activation and first-day checklist. Do not state a fee or approval timeline until confirmed. **[LINK APPROVED FORMS]**

Show the approved catalogue. Possible categories to verify include GST, ITR, MSME/Udyam, FSSAI, Trademark, DSC and other services actually offered by the company. ## Page 5 — Pricing and Earnings
Use the current approved price and commission matrix. Show customer price, applicable external/statutory costs, partner share and any other deductions separately. Add 2–3 illustrative scenarios only after rates are verified. No guaranteed-income language. **[MASTER MATRIX REQUIRED]**

## Page 6 — Find and Convert Customers
Quick steps: identify local businesses, ask permission to understand their needs, recommend only relevant services, use approved collateral, follow up, record every lead and never promise an unconfirmed outcome. Include approved WhatsApp and sales scripts by reference. **[LINK SALES KIT]**

## Page 7 — Documents and Order Processing
Use the service-specific checklist before accepting an order. Confirm document legibility and completeness, use approved secure channels, record consent where required, and never retain identity documents longer than authorized. Refer to the detailed SOP. **[LINK CHECKLISTS/SOP]**

## Page 8 — Marketing and Company Support
Describe only confirmed support: approved creatives, training, online lead allocation, digital profile/marketing help or campaign support. Specify eligibility, scope, response expectations and partner responsibilities only when documented. **[VERIFY WRITTEN COMMITMENTS]**

## Page 9 — Payments, Conduct and Support
Summarize approved customer payment, settlement, cancellation/refund, complaint, discount and brand-use rules. Include official support contacts and escalation route. **[APPROVED POLICIES/CONTACTS REQUIRED]**
