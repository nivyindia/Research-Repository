# 07 — Business Model and Commercials

## Required content
- Franchise/Retailer business model
- Company vs partner responsibilities
- Initial fee/deposit/recurring charges, if applicable
- Territory or exclusivity terms, if applicable
- Commission eligibility and exclusions
- Renewals, suspension and termination
- Discount/negotiation authority
- Partner obligations and service standards

All numbers and binding terms must come from the approved commercial plan and agreement. Unknown terms remain `[VERIFY WITH AUTHORIZED OWNER]`.
