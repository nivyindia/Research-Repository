# Partner Welcome Kit — Franchisee / Retailer

## Audience and purpose
For a new or existing Vyapari Suvidha Kendra Franchisee/Retailer. It explains how the business works, what services can be offered, how to handle customers, where to find pricing and checklists, how support works and which rules must be followed.

## Deliverables
- `01-Short-Offline-Booklet.md` — target 8–10 printed pages.
- `02-Long-Format-Master-Guide.md` — detailed digital reference.
- Numbered subfolders contain content checklists and placement guidance.

Only approved facts and current contact details may appear in the final booklet. Link detailed policies and changing price data rather than hard-coding unverified figures.
