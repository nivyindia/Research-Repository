# Vyapari Suvidha Kendra Kits — Project Planning Hub / परियोजना योजना केंद्र

## Mandatory format / अनिवार्य प्रारूप

**हिंदी:** इस प्रोजेक्ट की हर file हिंदी और English दोनों में बनाई जाएगी। हर section में पहले देवनागरी लिपि में हिंदी और उसके तुरंत बाद उसी सामग्री का English translation होगा।

**English:** Every file in this project must be written in both Hindi and English. In every section, write the Hindi in Devanagari script first, followed immediately by the English translation of the same content.

**हिंदी उदाहरण (Hindi Sample)**

> **"वसुधैव कुटुम्बकम् का अर्थ है कि पूरी धरती ही हमारा परिवार है।"**

**English translation:** “Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam means that the entire Earth is one family.”

Technical filenames, paths, URLs and code may remain in English, but their explanatory content must follow the Hindi-first bilingual format.

## Purpose / उद्देश्य
Keep every planning artifact for the Partner Welcome Kit and Distributor Field Meeting Kit together before writing or migrating final content.

**English:** Centralize all planning artifacts for the Partner Welcome Kit and Distributor Field Meeting Kit before final content writing or migration begins.

## Operating rules / कार्य नियम
- Plan first; a folder or placeholder is not completed content.
- Do not invent prices, commissions, incentives, lead guarantees, legal terms or service availability.
- Mark content as Verified, Needs confirmation, Missing source, or Approved.
- Keep Distributor economics separate from Franchisee/Retailer economics.
- Never put customer KYC data, passwords or confidential personal information in GitHub.
- Preserve source provenance and archive superseded versions.
- Derive short offline booklets from approved long-format masters.
- Update the progress tracker whenever a phase or deliverable changes.
- **All content must be Hindi-first and immediately followed by its English translation.**
