# Distributor Field Meeting Kit — Long-Format Field Playbook
**Audience:** Distributor | **Status:** Working draft pending source verification and approval.

## 1. Role and authority
Define recruitment territory, activities, reporting, lead ownership, permitted representations and escalation limits. Clarify who approves partners and who may contract or collect documents/payments. [VERIFY AGREEMENT]

## 2. Market and prospect planning
Map business clusters and likely partner profiles. Record source/date for market claims and contact prospects respectfully.

## 3. Recruitment funnel
Prospect → initial contact → discovery → qualification → meeting/demo → commercial explanation → follow-up → application → KYC/verification → agreement/payment if applicable → training → activation. Assign an owner and exit criteria to each stage.

## 4. Opening script
“Namaste, main Vyapari Suvidha Kendra ke business-partner model ke baare mein baat karna chahta/chahti hoon. Kya abhi 5–10 minute baat karna suvidhajanak hai?” Stop if the person declines. Do not imply government affiliation unless authorized and evidenced.

## 5. Discovery questions
What customers do you serve? Which business services are requested? Can you manage enquiries and follow-ups? What arrangement are you considering? Who decides? What must be clear before you proceed?

## 6. Franchise model pitch
Explain customer segments, active services, partner tasks, company processing/support, pricing transparency and customer-service expectations. Use approved presentation and agreement.

## 7. Franchisee earnings
Use approved matrix; distinguish customer price, statutory/third-party costs, company share, partner share, deductions and settlement. Scenarios must show assumptions and remain illustrative, not guaranteed.

## 8. Distributor earnings
Use only the approved plan: eligible activity, commission basis, exclusions, payment trigger/cycle, reversals and conditions. Never infer Distributor share from Franchisee commissions.

## 9. Online lead generation and marketing
Document lead sources, allocation, acceptance/follow-up ownership, duplicate handling, CRM and reporting. For marketing define deliverables, ad-spend treatment, geography, duration, eligibility and exclusions. No guaranteed results unless formally approved.

## 10. Meeting agenda
Introduction/permission → discovery → relevant opportunity → model and responsibilities → verified pricing/earnings → confirmed support → objections → qualification/next step → notes and follow-up.

## 11. Qualification
Hot: meets criteria, decision authority and specific next step. Warm: potential fit but questions/timing remain. Cold: poor fit, no readiness or declined. Record evidence; do not pressure.

## 12. Objection handling
Listen, acknowledge, clarify, answer from approved evidence, disclose uncertainty and agree next step. Escalate unanswered questions.

## 13. Closing and negotiation
Summarize need, fit, verified terms and unresolved items. No unauthorized discounts or side agreements. Send the approved proposal and log version/date.

## 14. Application and handoff
Share official checklist and secure channel. Explain verification, agreement, payment if applicable, training and activation. Final approval belongs to authorized team.

## 15. Follow-up
Use approved sequence: recap, requested information, answer open questions, decision check-in, close-loop. Respect contact preferences and opt-outs.

## 16. Reporting and KPIs
Track visits, qualified leads, meetings, proposals, applications, approvals, activations, conversion, lost reasons, complaints and next actions. Targets require management approval.

## 17. Regional fees/compliance
Use dated authoritative sources; separate statutory fees from company charges; escalate uncertain questions.

## 18. Training and QA
Practice scripts and role-plays, test service knowledge, pricing accuracy, CRM use and ethical conduct.

## 19. Support and escalation
Use approved support directory, issue owner, case reference and approved response targets.

## 20. Data protection and field conduct
Use approved systems, protect prospect information, avoid false urgency/income guarantees and never store KYC files in GitHub or unapproved personal accounts.

## Approval gates
Commercial: franchise/distributor economics. Operations: service scope/onboarding. Marketing: leads/campaign commitments. Legal/compliance: agreements/regulatory claims. Brand: collateral.
