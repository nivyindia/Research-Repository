# Master Plan — Vyapari Suvidha Kendra Kits

## Objective
Prepare both kits in short offline and long digital formats, grounded in existing Google Drive materials: Partner Welcome Kit, Distributor Field Kit and Training Folder.

## Scope
### Partner Welcome Kit
Audience: Franchisee / Retailer.
Purpose: explain the model, onboarding, services, pricing/earnings, customer handling, sales/marketing, SOPs, payments and support.

### Distributor Field Meeting Kit
Audience: Distributor.
Purpose: recruit Franchisees/Retailers, run field meetings, qualify prospects, explain economics and company support, handle objections, close and hand off for onboarding.

## Phases
1. **Structure** — create folders and Markdown index files.
2. **Inventory** — list existing Drive files and map each to a destination; do not recreate available assets.
3. **Evidence check** — verify services, pricing, fees, commissions, incentives, lead/marketing commitments, agreements and policies.
4. **Draft long versions** — write full reference content and link shared master sources.
5. **Derive short booklets** — compress approved content to 8–10 and 9–12 pages.
6. **Review** — commercial, operational, legal/compliance and brand approval.
7. **Print readiness** — pagination, contact details, current version/date and QR/links to digital references.

## Rules
- No unsupported income claims or guarantees.
- Do not present government fees, statutory requirements or service timelines as fixed unless verified for the specific service and date.
- Avoid storing sensitive customer KYC data in this repository.
- Keep old files archived; never delete source files during migration.
- A price/commission change must update the master source first, then trigger a review of both kits.
