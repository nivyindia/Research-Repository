# Regional Compliance Alert Register / क्षेत्रीय compliance alert register

**स्थिति:** प्रारूप / **Status:** Skeleton — source-driven register

## Fields / फ़ील्ड
1. Jurisdiction/region and affected service / Jurisdiction/region और प्रभावित service
2. Alert topic and required action / Alert topic और required action
3. Official source URL and publication date / Official source URL और publication date
4. Applicability/effective date / Applicability/effective date
5. Last verified date and next review / Last verified date और next review
6. Responsible owner and escalation / Responsible owner और escalation
7. Status and superseded alert reference / Status और superseded alert reference

## Rule / नियम
केवल official sources से verify की गई alerts रखें; legal advice का दावा न करें। / Use only alerts verified from official sources; do not represent them as legal advice.
