# Master Implementation Plan

## 1. Objective
Create a complete, source-backed documentation system for:
- **Partner Welcome Kit** — for Franchisees/Retailers joining and operating the service centre.
- **Distributor Field Meeting Kit** — for Distributors recruiting, qualifying and onboarding Franchisees/Retailers.
- **Shared Master References** — common service catalogue, approved pricing/commission matrix, approvals, version control and audit records.

The immediate milestone is to finalize the outline of every planned file and the phased implementation plan. Detailed content production follows only after the outline and source inventory are reviewed.

## 2. Final deliverables
1. Planning hub containing all plans and the progress tracker.
2. File-by-file outline register for every proposed document.
3. Source inventory and gap analysis against Google Drive Partner Welcome Kit, Distributor Field Kit, Training Folder and relevant approved business documents.
4. Migrated/organized source materials with provenance and duplicate handling.
5. Verified shared master references and commercial matrix.
6. Long-format master guides for each audience.
7. Compact offline booklets: Partner Welcome Kit (target 8–10 pages) and Distributor Field Meeting Kit (target 9–12 pages).
8. Supporting sales, onboarding, training, communications, marketing, reporting and SOP assets.
9. Approval and release record for print/offline versions.

## 3. Audience and content boundary
### Partner Welcome Kit
Answers: What is the business? What services can I offer? What documents do I need? How do I onboard, sell, serve customers, receive payments, get support and track performance? What are the verified charges and earnings?

### Distributor Field Meeting Kit
Answers: Which prospects are suitable? How should I conduct a field meeting? How do I explain the model, handle objections, qualify prospects, follow up and hand over onboarding? What are the distributor's verified earnings and responsibilities? What support does the company actually commit to?

### Shared references
Maintain one approved source of truth for service availability, customer pricing, statutory/third-party charges, company/partner/distributor shares, settlement rules, effective dates, approvals and policy changes.

## 4. Non-negotiable rules
- No fabricated or assumed commercial terms.
- No earnings guarantees unless explicitly supported by approved written policy.
- No claim of company-provided leads, online marketing, conversion support or territory protection until confirmed by source/owner.
- Franchisee/Retailer share and Distributor share must be explained independently.
- Mark unknowns visibly and route them to the decision register.
- Keep customer personal data and completed KYC documents outside this repository.
- Keep an archive of superseded versions; do not silently overwrite source files.
- Every migrated file should record original name/location, date if available, destination and status.
- Print-ready content must be derived from approved master content.

## 5. Definition of done
The project is complete only when:
- Every planned file has a defined purpose and outline.
- Relevant source folders have been inventoried and compared against the outline.
- Duplicates, conflicting versions and missing information are recorded.
- Pricing, commission, incentives, settlement, lead/marketing support, service scope and onboarding requirements are verified and approved.
- Both long-format guides are complete and reviewed.
- Both offline booklets meet their page targets and pass factual/compliance review.
- Required supporting assets are linked and version-controlled.
- Progress tracker has no unresolved release blockers.
- Final files are clearly marked Draft, Review, Approved or Archived.

## 6. Status vocabulary
- **Not started** — no work performed.
- **In progress** — work underway.
- **Needs source audit** — outline exists, but source material has not been checked.
- **Needs confirmation** — evidence is incomplete or owner decision is required.
- **In review** — content is prepared and awaiting review.
- **Approved** — authorized reviewer has approved it.
- **Blocked** — cannot proceed without a dependency or decision.
- **Complete** — acceptance criteria met; not equivalent to commercial approval unless separately stated.

## 7. Governance
The Progress Tracker is the live execution record. The File Outline Register defines intended coverage. The Source Audit Plan controls source inspection and migration. The Decisions and Open Questions file records unresolved facts. If scope or sequencing changes, update these records and explain why before proceeding.
