## Operating rules
- Plan first; do not treat a folder structure or placeholder as completed content.
- Source documents are the authority for existing commercial terms. Do not invent prices, commissions, incentives, lead guarantees, legal terms or service availability.
- Mark content as **Verified**, **Needs confirmation**, **Missing source**, or **Approved**.
- Keep Distributor economics separate from Franchisee/Retailer economics.
- Do not publish customer/private KYC data, passwords, identity documents or confidential account details in GitHub.
- Preserve source provenance and version/date when migrating material.
- Short offline booklets are derived from approved long-format masters; they must not introduce new claims.
- Update the progress tracker whenever a phase or deliverable changes.
