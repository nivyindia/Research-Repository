**हिंदी:** ये templates प्रारंभिक operational frameworks हैं। इन्हें वास्तविक policy, target, retention rule या approved system का विकल्प न माना जाए। इनमें कोई price या commission figure शामिल नहीं है।

**English:** These templates are initial operational frameworks. They do not replace actual policy, targets, retention rules, or an approved system. No price or commission figures are included.

## 4. Status vocabulary / स्थिति के मानक

- **New / नया:** Received but not yet reviewed / प्राप्त हुआ, समीक्षा बाकी।
- **In progress / जारी:** An owner is working on the case / जिम्मेदार व्यक्ति काम कर रहा है।
- **Awaiting customer / ग्राहक की प्रतीक्षा:** A required response or document is pending / जरूरी उत्तर या document बाकी है।
- **Awaiting internal team / Internal team की प्रतीक्षा:** Handoff sent; response pending / handoff भेजा गया; जवाब बाकी है।
- **Escalated / Escalated:** Sent to an authorized decision-maker / अधिकृत decision-maker को भेजा गया।
- **Completed / पूरा:** Completion evidence exists / पूरा होने का प्रमाण उपलब्ध है।
- **Cancelled or declined / रद्द या अस्वीकृत:** Reason recorded without unnecessary sensitive details / कारण दर्ज है; अनावश्यक sensitive details नहीं।

## 5. Data protection rules / Data protection नियम

**हिंदी:** Reports में aggregate counts और non-sensitive case IDs को प्राथमिकता दें। Aadhaar/PAN numbers, bank account details, passwords, identity scans, tax returns या अन्य वास्तविक customer documents GitHub में न डालें। Reporting access और retention period को authorized owner से confirm करें।

**English:** Prefer aggregate counts and non-sensitive case IDs in reports. Do not put Aadhaar/PAN numbers, bank account details, passwords, identity scans, tax returns, or other real customer documents in GitHub. Confirm reporting access and retention periods with an authorized owner.

## 6. Pending owner confirmations / Owner confirmations बाकी

- **हिंदी:** Reporting frequency, authorized system, official recipients, escalation deadlines, KPI definitions and targets अभी confirm करने हैं। / **English:** Reporting frequency, authorized system, official recipients, escalation deadlines, KPI definitions and targets still need confirmation.
- **हिंदी:** ये templates कोई sales quota, guaranteed result, service-level commitment या commercial term तय नहीं करते। / **English:** These templates do not set any sales quota, guaranteed result, service-level commitment, or commercial term.
