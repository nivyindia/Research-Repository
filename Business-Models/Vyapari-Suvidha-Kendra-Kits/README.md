# Vyapari Suvidha Kendra — Partner & Distributor Kits

## Purpose
This project organizes the complete content for two related but distinct kits:
1. **Partner Welcome Kit** — for Franchisee/Retailer onboarding and day-to-day operations.
2. **Distributor Field Meeting Kit** — for field meetings, franchise/retailer recruitment, qualification, objection handling and onboarding.

## Deliverables
- Short offline booklet: Partner Welcome Kit (target 8–10 pages).
- Long digital reference: Partner Welcome Kit.
- Short offline booklet: Distributor Field Meeting Kit (target 9–12 pages).
- Long digital field-sales playbook: Distributor Field Meeting Kit.
- Shared source-of-truth templates for pricing, commissions, service checklists and approved company commitments.
- File register, gap audit and progress tracker.

## Structure
- `01_Partner-Welcome-Kit/`
- `02_Distributor-Field-Meeting-Kit/`
- `03_Shared-Master-References/`

## Content integrity rules
- Do not invent franchise fees, service prices, commission percentages, distributor incentives, guaranteed income, refund terms or marketing/lead commitments.
- Mark unconfirmed items `[VERIFY WITH SOURCE]` and keep them out of final print-ready claims until approved.
- Reuse existing Drive/training content before drafting duplicates.
- Every business claim should be labelled as verified, proposed, or pending approval.
- Use the sequence: **REUSE → ADAPT → INTEGRATE → BUILD ONLY WHAT IS MISSING**.
