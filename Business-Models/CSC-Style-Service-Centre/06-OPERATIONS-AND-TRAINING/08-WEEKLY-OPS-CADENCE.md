**Attendees:** HQ ops, partner success, L2 (pilot district), optional IT  

| Agenda item | Time |
|-------------|------|
| Critical incidents since last week | 5 min |
| LIVE centre count + new activations | 5 min |
| Ticket SLA breaches | 5 min |
| Mystery audits planned / results | 5 min |
| Training / certification backlog | 5 min |
| Recruitment funnel (stages, not income talk) | 5 min |
| Blockers (portal, term sheet, counsel) | 5 min |
| Actions with owners | 5 min |

**Do not** set vanity GMV targets as the main agenda in pilot.
