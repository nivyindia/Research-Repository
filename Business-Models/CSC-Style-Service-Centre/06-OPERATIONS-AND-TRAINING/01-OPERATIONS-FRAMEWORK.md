## 1. Centre setup

---

## 2. Daily opening / closing

1. Power, devices, biometric, printer test
2. Login to authorised portals; check announcements
3. Cash / wallet float reconciliation start-of-day
4. Display rate list and grievance number
5. End-of-day: transaction register, receipts, failed txn log, logout, backup, secure devices

---

## 3. Service delivery standards

| Step | Standard |
|------|----------|
| Identity | Verify customer eligibility; never store Aadhaar/PAN full numbers in local notes beyond lawful need |
| Consent | OTP/consent handled by customer; staff do not record OTPs |
| Documents | Scan, name files consistently, return originals |
| Payment | Official channel only; issue receipt with reference |
| Status | Give acknowledgement number and expected timeline |
| Failure | Log, escalate, no “fix” unofficial workaround |

Core operator skills (from Academy training matrix): eligibility check, scanning/PDF, form accuracy, payment/receipt, status tracking, privacy, device troubleshooting, daily register.

---

## 4. Payments, receipts, float

- Wallet top-up may incur PG charges (DSP wallet page — mode-specific %)
- Separate cash vs digital reconciliation
- No third-party credit cards for wallet top-up if provider forbids (CSC wallet warning analogue)
- Prescribed / published fees only

---

## 5. Customer support & complaints

| Level | Action |
|-------|--------|
| L1 Centre | Resolve process/status queries; re-print receipt |
| L2 District / Distributor | Portal access, device, commission disputes |
| L3 Platform | Provider / legal / fraud |
| External | Official grievance channels for G2C (e.g. 14599 for CSC network — Nivy must publish its own number) |

---

## 6. QC, audit, fraud controls

- Mystery checks / sample transaction review
- CCTV or process logs where lawful and disclosed
- Biometric device custody
- No fee overcharging vs published list
- Immediate suspension path for fraud / data abuse

---

## 7. MIS / reporting

Daily: txn count, value, failures, complaints  
Weekly: top services, training gaps  
Monthly: P&L input, partner scorecard
