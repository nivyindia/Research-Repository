## 1. Reference architecture (historical / guideline)

Early CSC scheme and many secondary summaries describe a **3-tier PPP structure**:

| Tier | Entity | Typical described role |
|------|--------|------------------------|
| Central | CSC e-Governance Services India Ltd (CSC SPV) | Platform, service aggregation, national standards, monitoring, partnerships |
| State | State Designated Agency (SDA) + District e-Governance Society (DeGS) | State implementation, coordination, VLE selection/support, local monitoring |
| Network / cluster | Service Centre Agency (SCA) | Build/operate/manage clusters of CSCs (often cited 500–1000) |
| Local | Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE) | Physical centre, citizen service delivery |

**Source notes:**
- CSC 2.0 Implementation Guidelines and secondary policy summaries still reference SDA / DeGS.
- SCA language is common in historical and textbook-style descriptions; **current operational use of SCA as a mandatory tier must be verified** — many public VLE registration flows now appear direct via CSC SPV registration portals.
- Reuse: `Nivy Academy/CSC Training/08-BUSINESS-MODEL-FRANCHISE/02-FRANCHISE-CHANNEL-HIERARCHY/CHANNEL-LEVEL-BUSINESS-MODEL-MAP.md`

**Critical distinction for Nivy:** A commercial “CSC-style” franchise programme is **not** an official CSC franchise unless authorised. Nivy programme (if built) needs its own brand, agreements and compliance framework.

## 4. VLE / local franchise-style role

### 4.1 Who is a VLE (official framing)

Village Level Entrepreneur who delivers government and non-government services from a Common Service Centre outlet (mostly owned/operated by the VLE).

Public portal language (csc.gov.in / FAQs):
- Key stakeholder of the scheme.
- Entrepreneurial capability sustains the scheme.
- Network scale cited in public material in the multi-lakh range (exact figure must be taken from dashboard on verification date).

### 4.2 Minimum requirements (public FAQ — csc.gov.in/faqs)

As published on official FAQ page (capture date 2026-10-04 research):

- Valid Aadhaar number.
- Village youth above 18 years of age.
- Passed 10th level examination from a recognised board (minimum educational qualification).
- Fluent in reading and writing the local dialect; basic knowledge of English.
- Prior knowledge of basic computer skills preferred.
- Motivated to drive social change; dedication and honesty.

**Registration fee (FAQ):** “There is no fee for registration as a VLE. It is absolutely free of cost.”

**Training (FAQ):** “No. You don’t need any specific training to become a VLE. Meeting the minimum criteria as mentioned above is enough to become a VLE.”  
*(Note: Registration portal also references TEC Certificate for certain application types — see below. Treat FAQ and portal rules together; VERIFY current mandatory training path.)*

### 4.3 Registration portal pre-requisites (register.csc.gov.in FAQ)

Public FAQ on registration portal (research capture 2026-10-04):

1. Registration allowed only for persons 18+ who are citizens of India.
2. Should either have a valid **Telecentre Entrepreneur Course (TEC) Certificate** or register under specific schemes (e.g. SHG or RDD).
3. For application type **CSC VLE**, it is mandatory to have a **TEC Certificate Number**. Applicant can register for TEC at http://www.cscentrepreneur.in/register .
4. For schemes such as SHG: provide name, mobile, state, district for white-listing and request DMs to add them.

Documents commonly listed in registration flow descriptions:
- Applicant photo
- Proof of Identity
- Proof of Address
- Cancelled cheque / passbook

### 4.4 System / infrastructure requirements (portal FAQ — older listed specs)

Portal FAQ lists (note: these may be outdated relative to current hardware expectations; mark VERIFY):

- At least 120 GB HDD
- At least 512 MB RAM
- CD/DVD Drive
- UPS PC with licensed Windows XP-SP2 or above
- 4 hrs battery backup / portable genset
- Printer / colour printer
- Webcam / digital camera
- Scanner
- Internet connection at least 128 kbps

**Action:** Prefer any newer official VLE kit / infrastructure checklist if published; treat the above as historical portal text until re-verified.

### 4.5 Responsibilities (FAQ)

- Public place with safety and convenience.
- Deliver services smoothly to citizens as available on Digital Seva Portal.
