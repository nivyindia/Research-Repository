## Policy notes (sourced)

1. **Prescribed rate list mandatory to display** at every CSC (PIB 5 Aug 2026, PRID 2295103). Overcharging complaints handled via grievance mechanisms; toll-free 14599.
2. **Registration as VLE:** free (official FAQ).
3. Most commission schedules are **partner-specific** and change; treat any number as dated snapshot only.
4. G2C service charges may be fixed by department/state; do not assume national uniform rates.
