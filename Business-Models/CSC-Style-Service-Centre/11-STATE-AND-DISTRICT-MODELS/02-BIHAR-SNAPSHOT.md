## 2. State delivery patterns (evidence summary)

| Theme | Observation | Implication for Nivy |
|-------|-------------|----------------------|
| Revenue / land services via CSC | Rajya Revenue & Land Reforms Dept training for VLEs; anchal-office CSC counters for online revenue services (2025 press) | Strong G2C facilitation demand; any Nivy role only via lawful portal/agent path |
| Local inaugurations | Block-level CSC counters for revenue services (e.g. Araria/Palasi coverage in local press) | District presence of government CSC channel is visible |
| Education / skill via VLE | CSC Education / Sarkari Pariksha style activity in rural blocks (CSC official social) | Education/skill is a viable assisted-service family |
| Registration | National CSC registration path (cscregister / digital seva) still primary public narrative | Do not invent parallel “Bihar CSC franchise fee” |

**SCA/SDA/DSP detail:** State-specific formal DSP-per-district structure is **not** as clearly documented in the same way as UP CSC 3.0 in the sources reviewed for this snapshot. Mark **VERIFY** for Bihar district agency contracts before modelling L2 as a government analogue.

## 3. Nivy expansion notes for Bihar

1. High CSC transaction activity → competition on **service quality, honesty, support**, not density claims.  
2. Revenue/land online services are a citizen pain point — only offer if lawful authorisation exists.  
3. Pilot logic: pick districts with documented service demand and manageable support radius from any L2.  
4. Never market as government CSC or revenue-department franchise.
