# CSC-Style Service Centre — Master Research & Implementation Index

## Purpose
Single navigation layer for the complete CSC-style business-model research and Nivy pilot design. Academy training material remains a separate reusable source.

## Operating principle
**REUSE → ADAPT → INTEGRATE → BUILD ONLY WHAT IS MISSING**

**Safety:** Not a CSC franchise programme unless real authorisation exists. No invented fees. No income guarantees.

## Evidence levels

- **A** — Official/current primary source  
- **B** — Provider/partner agreement or official commercial schedule  
- **C** — Credible secondary source  
- **D** — Market claim/unverified; research only  

Never convert C/D into implementation fact without verification.

---

## Implementation gates

1. Service legality/authorisation verified  
2. Customer pricing verified  
3. Unit economics verified  
4. Partner economics verified (term sheet)  
5. SOP + training ready  
6. Technology/MIS ready  
7. QC/audit ready  
8. Pilot completed  
9. Partner graduation criteria met  
10. Scale approval
