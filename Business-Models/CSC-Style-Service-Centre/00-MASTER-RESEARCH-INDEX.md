# CSC-Style Service Centre — Master Research & Implementation Index

## Purpose
Single navigation layer for the complete CSC-style / Jan Suvidha Kendra business-model research. This is the implementation source of truth; Academy training material remains a separate reusable source.

## Operating principle
**REUSE → ADAPT → INTEGRATE → BUILD ONLY WHAT IS MISSING**

## Mandatory data fields for service research
Service | State | Provider | Customer fee | Tax/charges | VLE/centre share | Distributor share | State/territory share | Nivy/central share | Settlement cycle | Wallet/prepaid requirement | Eligibility | Official source | Effective date | Evidence status.

## Evidence levels
- **A — Official/current primary source**
- **B — Provider/partner agreement or official commercial schedule**
- **C — Credible secondary source**
- **D — Market claim/unverified; research only**

Never convert C/D evidence into an implementation fact without verification.

## Implementation gates
1. Service legality/authorization verified
2. Customer pricing verified
3. Unit economics verified
4. Partner economics verified
5. SOP + training ready
6. Technology/MIS ready
7. QC/audit ready
8. Pilot completed
9. Partner graduation criteria met
10. Scale approval
