## 2. Clause subjects (checklist for counsel)

### Commercial
- [ ] Definitions (territory, services, brand, net revenue)
- [ ] Appointment & non-exclusivity / exclusivity
- [ ] Term, renewal, lock-in
- [ ] Fees, deposits, refunds, taxes (GST)
- [ ] Revenue share / commission calculation & audit rights
- [ ] Targets / minimum performance (if any)
- [ ] Working capital / float responsibility

### Brand & IP
- [ ] Licence to use Nivy marks (scope, quality control)
- [ ] **Prohibition** on using CSC / government marks without licence
- [ ] Marketing approval process
- [ ] IP ownership of training materials and software

### Operations & compliance
- [ ] Service standards & published price list
- [ ] KYC / AML where applicable
- [ ] Prohibited conduct (overcharging, fraud, data misuse)
- [ ] Audit, inspection, mystery shopping
- [ ] Training & certification requirements
- [ ] Sub-contracting / agents

### Data & privacy
- [ ] Roles (controller / processor) under DPDP / applicable law
- [ ] Permitted processing purposes
- [ ] Retention, deletion, breach notification
- [ ] Cross-border transfer (if any)
- [ ] Ban on storing OTPs, biometrics, full Aadhaar beyond lawful need

### Liability & risk
- [ ] Indemnity (partner vs platform vs citizen claims)
- [ ] Limitation of liability
- [ ] Insurance requirements
- [ ] Force majeure
- [ ] Business continuity

### Exit
- [ ] Termination for cause / convenience
- [ ] Effects of termination (de-branding, data return, final accounts)
- [ ] Non-solicit / non-compete (reasonableness — counsel)
- [ ] Dispute resolution (seat, arbitration/mediation)
- [ ] Governing law

### Government / regulated services
- [ ] Partner must hold **own** lawful authorisation for G2C/BC/IRCTC etc.
- [ ] Nivy does not warrant government appointment
- [ ] Immediate suspension if authorisation lost
- [ ] No representation as government franchise
