## 2. Suggested sections (citizen / walk-in)

1. **Who we are** — Nivy Centre Partner identity (centre name + Nivy programme reference); clarify **not** a government CSC office unless separately authorised.  
2. **What data we may process** — only what is required for the requested service (examples: name, contact, document scans for portal upload).  
3. **Purpose** — to submit/assist applications on authorised portals; receipts; complaint handling.  
4. **Third-party portals** — government/bank/provider sites process data under **their** terms; centre is facilitation point.  
5. **What we do not do** — no selling personal data; no storing OTPs; no keeping full Aadhaar/PAN in local notes beyond lawful need.  
6. **Retention** — as required for service completion, law, and dispute resolution; then delete/return.  
7. **Rights** — access, correction, withdrawal of consent where applicable — point to contact channel.  
8. **Grievance / contact** — centre + Nivy published contact (pilot number TBD).  
9. **Updates** — version date on notice.
