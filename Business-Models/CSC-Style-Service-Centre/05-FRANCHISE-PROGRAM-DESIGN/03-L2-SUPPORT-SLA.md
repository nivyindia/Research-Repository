# L2 District / Distributor — Support SLA (One-Pager)

**Folder:** `05-FRANCHISE-PROGRAM-DESIGN/`  
**Created:** 2026-10-04  
**Status:** Draft SLA for pilot / scale discussion  
**Not** a government DSP service-level document.

---

## 1. Scope

Applies to Nivy **L2 District / Distributor** support of **L3 Centre Partners** in an assigned territory cluster.

Out of scope: government CSC portal SLAs; bank BC network SLAs unless separately contracted.

---

## 2. Service levels (draft targets)

| Service | Target | Measure |
|---------|--------|---------|
| First response on L3 ticket (ops) | ≤1 business day | Ticket timestamps |
| Critical incident acknowledge (fraud / data / brand) | ≤4 business hours | Escalation form |
| Onboarding support for new L3 | Activation plan within 5 business days of agreement | CRM stage dates |
| Fee-list / brand compliance nudge | Within 2 business days of fail finding | Mystery / audit log |
| Monthly MIS chase | Partners ≥90% on-time submission support | MIS dictionary |
| Scheduled check-in sample | ≥20% of LIVE centres / month in pilot | Visit or call log |

---

## 3. L2 responsibilities

- First-line process support (not legal advice)  
- Relay platform announcements  
- Flag overcharge / brand misuse upward same day when known  
- Cooperate with mystery audits  
- No promise of CSC/government status to L3s

---

## 4. Platform (HQ) responsibilities

- Ticketing tool + escalation path  
- Training content / certification rules  
- Catalogue and fee-list version control  
- Final suspension / termination decisions per agreement

---

## 5. Exclusions

- Provider portal outages outside Nivy control  
- L3 hardware failure (partner-owned unless kit programme exists)  
- Disputes requiring bank/UIDAI/state portal owner resolution

---

## 6. Review

SLA reviewed at end of pilot; adjust staffing before scale.
