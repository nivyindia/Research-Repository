# Nivy Channel Programme Structure — Original Design (Draft)

**Folder:** `05-FRANCHISE-PROGRAM-DESIGN/`  
**Created:** 2026-10-04  
**Status:** Draft structure — not a live offer  
**Critical:** This is an **original Nivy** commercial programme concept. It is **not** an official CSC franchise and must never be marketed as CSC or government-authorised unless a real authorisation exists.

## 1. Design principles (from research)

| Principle | Source of learning |
|-----------|-------------------|
| REUSE → ADAPT → INTEGRATE → BUILD ONLY WHAT IS MISSING | Master prompt |
| Clear separation of research evidence vs Nivy IP | Legal safety |
| No invented CSC fees/commissions presented as Nivy fact | Phase 4 |
| Multi-tier optional, not mandatory copy of SCA/SDA | Phase 2 |
| Self-sustaining, transaction-oriented local centres | CSC 2.0 framing (conceptual) |
| Prescribed rate transparency for citizen-facing fees | PIB rate-list mandate (analogue: Nivy price list display) |

## 2. Proposed channel tiers (Nivy-branded)

| Tier | Working name | Role | Territory idea | Economics (to be set by Nivy policy) |
|------|--------------|------|----------------|--------------------------------------|
| L0 | Nivy Platform / HQ | Catalogue, tech, training, brand, QC, partner contracts | National | Platform fee / revenue share |
| L1 | State / Master Partner | State expansion, DSP-style coordination, recruitment support | State or multi-district | Master fee + override |
| L2 | District / Distributor | Local recruitment, stock (if any), first-line support | District / cluster | Distributor margin |
| L3 | Centre Partner / Franchisee | Physical centre, citizen service delivery | Local catchment | Centre margin + service fees |
| L4 | Service Agent (optional) | Single-service or mobile agent under a centre | Micro | Agent share |

**UP learning:** UP CSC 3.0 uses **two District Service Providers (DSP) per district** under DeGS, prepaid wallet model, PPP. Nivy may adapt a **lighter** L1/L2 design without claiming government DSP status.

## 3. Centre Partner (L3) — package outline

### Eligibility (draft — align with practical ops, not copy CSC FAQ verbatim)
- Adult Indian resident; identity & address KYC
- Minimum education / digital literacy (define in final policy)
- Suitable public-facing location
- Device + connectivity readiness checklist
- No conflicting exclusive agency that blocks Nivy services (legal review)

### What Nivy provides (to design)
- Brand guidelines and allowed signage
- Service catalogue access (Nivy-authorised services only)
- Training / certification path (reuse Academy CSC training where legal)
- Partner portal / CRM / ticket system (Phase 7)
- Marketing templates (original — Phase 8)
- Escalation and QC framework

### What partner provides
- Capex (hardware, space, fit-out)
- Working capital / float where payment services require it
- Daily ops, KYC handling, customer service
- Compliance with Nivy + applicable law + any upstream provider rules

### Fees (placeholders — set only after legal/finance)
| Item | Placeholder |
|------|-------------|
| Joining / franchise fee | TBD — transparent, written |
| Security deposit | TBD — refundable rules |
| Monthly / annual platform fee | TBD |
| Revenue share / commission | Service-wise schedule |
| Training fee | TBD or included |

**Do not publish numbers until approved.** CSC research showed registration can be free at national VLE level; Nivy may choose free or paid onboarding based on unit economics.

## 4. Appointment workflow (draft)

1. Lead capture (web / referral / camp)
2. Application + KYC documents
3. Due diligence / territory check
4. Agreement (legal-reviewed)
5. Training + practical assessment
6. Soft launch / probation KPIs
7. Full activation
8. Ongoing MIS, audit, refresher
9. Renewal / exit / replacement process

---

## 5. Territory & exclusivity (policy choices)

Options to decide later:
- Non-exclusive density model (like dense CSC markets — UP >1 lakh centres)
- Soft exclusivity (radius / pincode with performance clauses)
- Category exclusivity (e.g. only one education partner per block)

Dense markets favour **performance + support quality** over hard exclusivity.

---

## 6. Explicit non-claims

- Nivy is not CSC SPV, not MeitY, not a State Designated Agency.
- Operating “CSC-style” services does not equal CSC authorisation.
- G2C services require lawful access path (state portal agent, provider agreement, etc.).
- Branding must not use CSC logo, “CSC franchise”, or government emblems without licence.
