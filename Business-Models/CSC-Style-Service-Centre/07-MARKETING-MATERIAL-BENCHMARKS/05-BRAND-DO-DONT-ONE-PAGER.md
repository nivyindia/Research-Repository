# Brand Do / Don't — One-Pager (Centres & Recruiters)

**Folder:** `07-MARKETING-MATERIAL-BENCHMARKS/`  
**Created:** 2026-10-04  
**Status:** Pilot brand rules  
**Canonical claims text:** `08-SALES-PROPOSALS-AND-PITCHES/00-CANONICAL-DISCLAIMER.md`

---

## DO

| Do | Why |
|----|-----|
| Use **Nivy** name and approved trade dress only | Clear commercial identity |
| Show **published fee list** at counter | Compliance + trust |
| Say “independent Nivy Centre Partner” | Accurate |
| Use approved FAQ / WhatsApp scripts | Claim control |
| Direct income questions to term sheet (no oral ₹) | Legal safety |
| Remove any unofficial poster immediately | Brand integrity |

---

## DON'T

| Don't | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Use **CSC** name, logo, or “CSC franchise” | Misrepresentation |
| Use **government emblems**, Ashoka, MeitY logos | Unlawful / misleading |
| Claim MeitY / CSC SPV / SDA / DSP appointment | False unless proven |
| Promise **guaranteed income** or “earn ₹X/month” | Forbidden |
| Copy competitor franchise ads as Nivy ads | Legal + quality |
| Display rates not on current Annex A / fee list | Overcharge risk |
| Store customer OTPs or full Aadhaar in notebooks | Data abuse |

---

## Counter display minimum

1. Nivy centre identification (name + centre_id)  
2. Fee list (version + date)  
3. Grievance / support contact  
4. Short disclaimer (canonical short form)

---

## Recruiter rule

First message and decks must carry short disclaimer. Track A/B/C materials never lead with “CSC”.

---

## Violation handling

1. Photo + incident form  
2. Immediate removal order  
3. Retrain or suspend per severity  
4. Repeat → exit path
