**Goal:** Lead capture for Track A (L3 Centre Partner) without false CSC claims

## Page sections (top → bottom)

### 1. Hero
- Headline: original value prop (assisted local digital services / livelihood)  
- Sub: Independent Nivy Centre Partner programme  
- Primary CTA: **Apply** / **Book discovery call**  
- Trust strip: process transparency (not fake gov logos)

### 2. Disclaimer bar (always visible or sticky)
Short form of mandatory sales disclaimer: *Not a CSC franchise; no income guarantee; authorised services only.*

### 3. Problem / opportunity
3 bullets: citizen access gap; operator livelihood; structured support vs ad-hoc centres.

### 4. How it works (3–4 steps)
Apply → Qualify & DD → Train & certify → Launch with checklist.

### 5. What you get / what you bring
Two-column comparison (Nivy vs Partner responsibilities).

### 6. Service principles
- Only authorised catalogue  
- Published fee list  
- No CSC/government emblem use  
Link to high-level categories only (not fake authorisation claims).

### 7. Economics teaser
“Investment categories — see current term sheet.” **No ₹ figures** until approved.

### 8. FAQ accordion
- Is this CSC? → No  
- Income guaranteed? → No  
- What documents? → Align onboarding Stage A/B  
- Territory? → Policy summary

### 9. Form / CTA
Fields: name, phone, state, district, pincode, current activity, space type, source.  
Consent + disclaimer checkbox required.

### 10. Footer
Legal entity, contact, full disclaimer, privacy link.
