# Recruitment Campaign Brief — Pilot (Claim-Safe)

## 1. Objective

Generate **qualified L3 (and optional L2) applications** in chosen pilot district(s) without CSC impersonation or income guarantees.

**Target (example placeholders):** ___ applications / ___ discovery calls / ___ signed in 90 days.

---

## 2. Audience

| Track | Who |
|-------|-----|
| A — Centre Partner | Local entrepreneurs, existing shop owners, digitally literate adults |
| B — Distributor | Multi-centre support capacity (if pilot uses L2) |

---

## 3. Channels (pick what you will run)

| Channel | Use | Notes |
|---------|-----|-------|
| WhatsApp outreach | Primary | Approved HI/EN scripts only |
| Referral | Warm | Same disclaimer |
| Local meetup / camp | Optional | No CSC banners |
| Landing page | Optional | Wireframe + disclaimer |
| Paid social | Optional | Legal review of creatives |

---

## 4. Message pillars (allowed)

1. Independent **Nivy** Centre Partner programme  
2. Assisted digital services for local citizens  
3. Training + support + clear written terms  
4. Authorised catalogue only  

## 5. Message bans (hard)

- CSC franchise / government scheme / MeitY appointment  
- “Earn ₹X/month” / guaranteed income  
- CSC or national emblems  
- Competitor ad copy paste  
- Unapproved fee tables  

---

## 6. Creative checklist before publish

- [ ] Short or full disclaimer present  
- [ ] No ₹ income figures  
- [ ] CTA = apply / discovery call only  
- [ ] Hindi/English match in meaning  
- [ ] Owner sign-off recorded  

---

## 7. Measurement

| Metric | Track in |
|--------|----------|
| Leads by source | CRM |
| Stage conversion | CRM |
| LOST reasons | CRM |
| Disclaimer complaints / confusion | Ops log |

---

## 8. Crisis

If any ad is accused of looking like CSC: **pause creative**, incident form, legal notify, replace with canonical disclaimer version.
