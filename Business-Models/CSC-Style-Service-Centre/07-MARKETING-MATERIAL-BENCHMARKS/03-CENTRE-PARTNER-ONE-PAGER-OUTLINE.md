## Layout (single A4 / digital equivalent)

### Header
- Nivy logo + “Centre Partner Programme”  
- Tagline (original, non-government): e.g. *Local assisted digital services, run by local partners*

### Mandatory disclaimer (footer or sidebar — always on)
> Nivy Centre Partner is an independent commercial programme. It is **not** a Common Service Centre (CSC) franchise, not a MeitY/CSC SPV appointment, and not a State Designated Agency or bank BC appointment unless separate written authorisation exists. No income is guaranteed. Fees and services only as per current written term sheet.

### Body blocks (suggested order)

1. **Who it is for**  
   Local entrepreneur / shop owner / assisted-service operator seeking a structured centre model with training and support.

2. **What you do (L3 role)**  
   Operate a public-facing centre delivering **only authorised** services from the Nivy catalogue; follow ops checklist, published fees, brand rules.

3. **What Nivy provides**  
   - Onboarding & certification path  
   - Partner portal / ticket support (per tech blueprint)  
   - Brand guidelines (no CSC/gov marks)  
   - QC & escalation framework  
   - Training path (reuse Academy where licensed)

4. **What you provide**  
   Space, power, connectivity, approved hardware, compliant operations, local customer service.

5. **Economics (placeholder only)**  
   Investment categories listed; **no invented rupee figures**. “See current term sheet.”

6. **Territory**  
   Density / soft-exclusivity policy in one line (policy choice — not promised exclusivity unless agreement says so).

7. **How to apply**  
   Single CTA: Apply / Discovery call + link or QR to application form fields aligned with onboarding checklist.

8. **Trust / proof**  
   Process transparency, training requirement, published fee list commitment — **not** fake network size or government logos.
