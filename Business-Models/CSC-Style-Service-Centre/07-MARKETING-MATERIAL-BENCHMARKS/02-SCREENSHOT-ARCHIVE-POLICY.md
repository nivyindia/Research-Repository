# Screenshot & Benchmark Archive Policy (Internal)

**Folder:** `07-MARKETING-MATERIAL-BENCHMARKS/`  
**Created:** 2026-10-04  
**Status:** Active policy  
**Audience:** Internal research team only

---

## 1. Purpose

Preserve provenance for marketing benchmark research without storing full third-party copyrighted brochures or redesigning them as Nivy assets.

---

## 2. What may be archived (internal only)

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---------|-------------|
| Public landing-page URL + access date | Full PDF brochure copy pasted into repo |
| Short factual notes (fee bands, CTA, claims) | Logos, photos, marketing copy verbatim |
| Screenshot of public page *for internal review only* (local/private storage) | Publishing competitor screenshots externally |
| Link + archive.org snapshot date if available | CSC / MeitY / bank logos without licence |

---

## 3. Naming convention (if local archive used)

```
MKT-{ID}_{Brand-slug}_{YYYY-MM-DD}.{png|pdf}
```

Example: `MKT-005_FIA-Global_2026-10-04.png`

Store **outside** public GitHub if screenshots contain dense copyrighted layout. Repo holds the **log row + URL + date** only.

---

## 4. Retention

- Log rows in `01-MARKETING-RESEARCH-RULES-AND-LOG.md` are permanent research evidence.  
- Screenshots: retain while useful for adaptation notes; delete when superseded.  
- Never treat a franchise ad income claim as a sourced economic figure.

---

## 5. Nivy originalisation gate

Original Nivy marketing assets may be drafted only after:
1. ≥5–10 diverse benchmark rows logged (satisfied for one-pager outline).  
2. Mandatory disclaimer block from sales kit included.  
3. No CSC/government emblem; no income guarantee.
