# CSC Current 2026 Evidence & Research Queue

**Research date:** 2026-10-04

## Verified current evidence

### 1. 80:20 VLE revenue-sharing baseline
MeitY CSC 2.0 recommends 80:20 revenue sharing between VLE and other stakeholders and asks State/UT administrations to devise stakeholder revenue-sharing policy.
Source: https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/CSC-2-0-Guidelines-Final-3rd-December-2015.pdf

### 2. CAG 2026 operating evidence
CAG Report No. 5 of 2026 says VLEs bear capital and recurring operating costs; in the audited CSC model 80% of commission on successful transactions flows to VLE and 20% is retained by CSC-SPV. It also documents prepaid wallets and periodic commission settlement for some services.
Source: https://cag.gov.in/uploads/media/Report-No-5-of-2026-Union-Government-FC-English-069ce328181a951-43720339.pdf

### 3. Current network and controls
As of 30 June 2026, PIB reported 5,16,787 functional CSCs, including 4,07,122 rural CSCs and 1,10,183 in Uttar Pradesh. Prescribed rate lists must be displayed; transactions are monitored; State/district teams inspect; grievance mechanisms exist; warnings, suspension and deactivation are available for violations.
Source: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2295103&lang=2&reg=48

### 4. Current transaction scale
FY2025-26 CSC portal + non-portal transactions were reported at 4,854.03 lakh nationally. This is network scale, not proof of average VLE income.
Source: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2290995&lang=2&reg=48

### 5. Banking commission timing
A government-hosted CSC banking FAQ states that CSC-SPV pays banking commission to VLE monthly based on the bank report and that CSC-SPV does not charge a fee for banking service registration.
Source: https://gramawardsachivalayam.ap.gov.in/GSWS/downloads/CSC/Services/FAQ%20on%20CSC%20Services.pdf

## Open research queue — service-by-service

Priority 1: Banking/DigiPay, insurance, BBPS, PAN, Aadhaar, IRCTC, PMFBY, e-Shram.

Priority 2: GeM facilitation, state G2C/e-District, agriculture, telemedicine, education/training.

For each service extract:
**customer fee → provider/backend fee → gross commission → VLE share → distributor/SCA/state share → CSC-SPV share → taxes → settlement timing → wallet/working capital → refund/chargeback → source/date/geography.**

## Important conclusion

No reliable current primary source found yet that gives one universal national percentage for a separate "distributor", "state owner" or "franchise seller". Do not invent one. The exact commercial waterfall must be tied to the relevant service agreement/state programme.

## Nivy reuse

The strongest reusable CSC controls are:
- unique operator ID;
- service-level rate card;
- transaction ledger;
- wallet/settlement;
- central MIS;
- training/certification;
- grievance system;
- inspection/audit;
- warning → suspension → deactivation;
- broad service catalogue to improve operator sustainability.
