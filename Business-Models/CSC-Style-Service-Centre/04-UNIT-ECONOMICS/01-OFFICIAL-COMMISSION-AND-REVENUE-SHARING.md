# CSC Official Commission & Revenue-Sharing Evidence

## National policy
MeitY CSC 2.0 guidelines state that commission/service charges are paid for successfully rendered services and recommend an 80:20 revenue-sharing ratio between the VLE and other stakeholders. State/UT administrations are to devise revenue-sharing policy among stakeholders.

This is a national policy-level recommendation, not a universal service-by-service payout table.

Source: MeitY, CSC 2.0 Implementation Guidelines, Section 11.2.
https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/CSC-2-0-Guidelines-Final-3rd-December-2015.pdf

## VLE economics
CSC 2.0 is designed to be self-sustaining from service delivery income. The VLE is therefore an entrepreneur/operator, not a centrally salaried employee in the general CSC model.

## What must be researched service-by-service
- Customer-facing fee
- Government/provider fee
- Service charge
- VLE commission
- Other stakeholder share
- State/district/SCA role where applicable
- Settlement timing
- Wallet mechanism
- Refund/reversal rules
- State-specific variation
- Source date and authority

## Current monitoring benchmark
A PIB release dated 5 August 2026 reported 5,16,787 total CSCs and 4,07,122 rural/Gram Panchayat-level CSCs as of 30 June 2026. It also states that prescribed service rate lists are mandatory for display, transactions are monitored regularly, State/District teams conduct inspections, and violations can lead to warning, suspension or deactivation.

Source: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2295103

## Modern monitoring benchmark
A 2026 PIB release reports an AI-based SPECTA module used to rank VLEs using service-delivery and transaction patterns and categorize services into business segments.

Source: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2290995
