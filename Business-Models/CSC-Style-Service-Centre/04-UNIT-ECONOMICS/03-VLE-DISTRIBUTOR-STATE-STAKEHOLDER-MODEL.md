# CSC VLE / Distributor / State Stakeholder Model

## VLE
The VLE is the local entrepreneur/operator running the CSC and delivering services to citizens.

## State / District
State/UT and district machinery participate in implementation, service integration, VLE selection/support and monitoring. CSC 2.0 says State/UT administrations should devise revenue-sharing policy among stakeholders.

## Distributor / SCA
Older CSC programme documentation references Service Centre Agencies (SCAs). Their role and commercial terms should be researched against the specific agreement/service period rather than treated as a current universal commission layer.

## Current monitoring benchmark
PIB 2026 states that CSC transactions are monitored regularly; State and district teams conduct inspections; prescribed service rate lists must be displayed; violations can result in warning, suspension or deactivation.

## Nivy adaptation
Separate: 1) Central platform/company, 2) Regional/state partner, 3) District/city partner, 4) Centre operator, 5) Specialist/service provider. Each layer needs written role, KPI, customer ownership, service economics, settlement and termination/transition rules.

Do not pay distributors merely for recruiting centres. Recurring commissions should primarily follow actual customer/service revenue and measurable support.
