# CSC Service-wise Findings — 2026

**Research date:** 2026-10-04  
**Status:** Primary-source extraction; exact commercial waterfall is still service-specific.

## Key audited service economics

CAG Report No. 5 of 2026 publishes CSC service categories with FY2024-25 transaction value/commission data. The table includes PMFBY, E-Recharge, State Government (G2C), BBPS, Tours & Travels, Insurance Renewal, Agriculture, Labour Services, Insurance Sales-GI, eStamp and PMSYM.

Primary source: https://saiindia.gov.in/webroot/uploads/download_audit_report/2026/5of2026UGEnglish-069ce38b4a151b0.15050638.pdf

**Important:** these figures are aggregate CSC ecosystem financial data, not a published VLE/distributor commission rate card. The CAG narrative separately states that 80% of commission on successful transactions flows to VLE and 20% is retained by CSC-SPV in the audited model.

## Service-specific evidence found

### Banking
A government-hosted CSC banking FAQ states:
- banking commission is paid by CSC-SPV to VLE monthly based on the bank's report;
- banking service registration does not require a CSC-SPV fee;
- VLE banking certification/training involves a ₹350 fee in the cited FAQ, with ₹175 re-exam fee after failure;
- TDS treatment is described in the FAQ: 10% where PAN is provided, 20% where PAN is not provided.

Source: https://gramawardsachivalayam.ap.gov.in/GSWS/downloads/CSC/Services/FAQ%20on%20CSC%20Services.pdf

These are not a universal current national banking commission percentage; they are evidence from the cited CSC banking FAQ.

### Insurance / DigiPay / e-Shram / Scholarship verification
CAG states that for these services commission is transferred to VLE accounts periodically rather than necessarily at transaction time. Exact percentage must be extracted from the applicable service agreement/rate card.

Source: https://saiindia.gov.in/webroot/uploads/download_audit_report/2026/5of2026UGEnglish-069ce38b4a151b0.15050638.pdf

### BBPS / E-Recharge / State G2C / PMFBY
CAG provides aggregate transaction/commission categories for these services, but the public report does not itself establish a universal distributor/state/VLE percentage for each category. Service-level primary schedules remain required.

### Current service ecosystem
PIB's 2025 CSC overview lists Aadhaar, PAN, passport, banking, insurance, pensions, loans, education, health, agriculture, utilities, legal services, travel and other services. A 2024 Ministry of Cooperation/CSC MoU says PACS can deliver more than 300 CSC e-services.

## Current conclusion

There is enough primary evidence to model:
**VLE → transaction commission → central settlement → periodic settlement for selected services → monitoring.**

There is NOT enough public primary evidence to publish one universal:
**VLE % + distributor % + State % + franchise fee**

for all CSC services.
