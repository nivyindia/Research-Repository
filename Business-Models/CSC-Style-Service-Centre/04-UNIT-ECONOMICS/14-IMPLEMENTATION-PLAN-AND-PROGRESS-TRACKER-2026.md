# CSC-Like Service Centre — Master Implementation Plan & Progress Tracker (2026)

## Objective
Build an evidence-first India-wide commercial intelligence database and Nivy business-model blueprint covering all 28 States + 8 UTs, major service categories, comparable networks, entry/setup/renewal fees, retailer/VLE/agent commissions, distributor/super-distributor/master/state shares, settlement rules, compliance, unit economics and Nivy's proposed commercial waterfall.

## Non-negotiable evidence rules
- Never invent a fee, percentage, commission or revenue share.
- Every numeric commercial claim must carry source, date/version where available, access date and confidence.
- Separate customer fee, statutory/provider fee, service charge and commission.
- Separate observed market data from derived benchmarks and Nivy PROPOSED economics.
- Never assume a distributor/super-distributor/state layer exists without evidence.
- Preserve GST-inclusive/exclusive status, refundable/non-refundable status and effective period.
- Aggregate transaction value is not retailer/VLE income.
- The CSC 80:20 audited framework is not a universal distributor waterfall.
- Unknown/unpublished values remain OPEN.

## Standard commercial waterfall
Customer payment → statutory/provider fee → service/processing fee → gross commission pool → retailer/VLE/agent → distributor → super/master distributor → state/regional partner → platform/provider → GST/TDS/other tax → net settlement.

Only populated layers supported by evidence.

## Per-jurisdiction extraction checklist
For every State/UT:
1. Official citizen-service/G2C/e-District platform.
2. State-authorized kiosk/service-centre programme.
3. CSC/VLE ecosystem and state-specific rules.
4. Banking/BC/AePS/DMT network.
5. Insurance/financial distribution network.
6. Major private comparable operators.
7. Full service catalogue.
8. Customer charges/rate cards.
9. Retailer/VLE/agent commission.
10. Distributor/super/master/state share where published.
11. Application/onboarding/franchise/distributor fees.
12. Security deposit/wallet/minimum float.
13. Hardware/software/setup/branding costs.
14. Recurring subscription/royalty/maintenance.
15. Renewal/re-certification fees.
16. Service activation fees.
17. Settlement cycle and payout mechanism.
18. GST/TDS/tax treatment.
19. Eligibility/KYC/training/certification.
20. Compliance, inspection, grievance, suspension/deactivation.
21. Territory/exclusivity and termination/refund terms.
22. Source URL + evidence excerpt + effective date + confidence.

## Nivy model design — only after evidence
Target architecture:
Nivy Central → State/Regional Partner → Super/Master Distributor → District/City Distributor → Certified Retailer/Service Centre → Customer.

Partner lifecycle:
Applicant → Training/Certification → Pilot → Active → Premium → Distributor → Super Distributor/State Partner.

Commercial principle:
Revenue should be tied primarily to genuine customer/service transactions and measurable value, not recruitment.

## Unit economics
For Lean/Standard/Premium models calculate:
- transactions/service/month
- average customer fee
- gross commission
- retailer income
- channel income
- operating expenses
- platform/software/royalty costs
- taxes/payment charges
- net operating income
- break-even transactions
- break-even revenue
- payback period
- ROI
- working-capital requirement.

All assumptions must be labelled ASSUMPTION; sourced figures must be labelled SOURCE; calculated figures must be labelled DERIVED; original Nivy commercial decisions must be labelled PROPOSED.
