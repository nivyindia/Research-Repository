## Verification rules
1. Prefer CSC SPV, MeitY, State Government, official service provider and government tender/agreement documents.
2. Record exact effective date.
3. Record state/UT because rates can vary.
4. Distinguish customer fee from commission.
5. Do not treat unofficial franchise websites as authoritative.
6. Do not infer a distributor/VLE split without evidence.
7. Mark old/expired schedules as historical.
