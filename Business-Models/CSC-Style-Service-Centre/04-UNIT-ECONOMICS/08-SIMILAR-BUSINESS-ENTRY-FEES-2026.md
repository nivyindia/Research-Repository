## 2. State-specific evidence

### Rajasthan — e-Mitra
- The Rajasthan e-Mitra RFP published in 2026 states a **₹1,500 + GST non-refundable application fee** for applicants seeking a new e-Mitra kiosk.
- Of that application amount, ₹1,000 + GST is transferred to the selected LSP for application processing.
- The RFP says the LSP shall not separately charge the applicant for applying for the kiosk.
- The same RFP revised the minimum/maximum bid-cap framework for service-provider revenue sharing to 12%–17% for all divisions; this is **not automatically the kiosk holder's commission** and should not be modelled as such without the final awarded contract.
- Rajasthan's official e-Mitra portal describes the model as self-sustaining, prepaid and based on transaction service charges/incentive-based revenue sharing.

### Uttar Pradesh / India-wide private benchmark
- GST Suvidha Kendra's published commercial plans provide one of the clearest current benchmark ladders:
  - Retailer: ₹5,000 + GST
  - Distributor: ₹25,000–₹50,000 + GST for 125–250 IDs
  - Exclusive Master Distributor: ₹3–5 lakh + GST
- Digital Suvidha Kendra publishes ₹35,999 franchise cost.
- These are **private-company commercial offers**, not government/CSC fees.

### Pan-India private benchmark
- Vakrangee states ₹4–6.5 lakh total capex depending on Kendra model/location, no security deposit, and 65%–80% revenue commission share to franchisees.
- PayNearby and Spice Money emphasize low/zero-entry distributor models but do not publish a universal current rupee joining fee on the pages reviewed.

## 3. Important distinction

Do **not** combine these numbers as if they are one common market tariff.

There are four different cost types:

1. **Application/registration fee** — e.g. Rajasthan e-Mitra ₹1,500 + GST.
2. **Franchise/license fee** — e.g. Digital Suvidha Kendra ₹35,999; GST Suvidha Kendra ₹5,000 retailer.
3. **Distributor/master-distributor acquisition fee** — e.g. GST Suvidha Kendra ₹25k–₹50k distributor and ₹3–5 lakh master distributor.
4. **Physical setup/capex** — e.g. Vakrangee ₹4–6.5 lakh; this is not necessarily a franchise fee.
