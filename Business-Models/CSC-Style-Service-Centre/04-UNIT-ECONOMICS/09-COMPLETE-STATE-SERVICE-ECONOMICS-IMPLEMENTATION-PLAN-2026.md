# Complete CSC-Like Service Centre Economics Research — Implementation Plan & Tracker (2026)

## Objective
Build an evidence-first, state-by-state commercial intelligence system for CSC-like multi-service centres and comparable private/state networks. Extract and normalize every relevant service/customer charge; retailer/VLE/agent commission; distributor, super-distributor/master-distributor and state/territory shares where documented; onboarding/franchise/distributor fees; security/wallet/setup costs; recurring fees; taxes; settlement; territory rights; unit economics and payback.

## Evidence rules
1. Never invent a percentage or rupee amount.
2. Every numeric commercial claim gets source URL, source type, date where available, access date and confidence.
3. Separate government rules, company pricing, contracts/RFPs, partner documents and third-party listings.
4. Customer fee is not commission; provider commission is not automatically retailer income.
5. Aggregate transaction value is not VLE income.
6. CSC audited 80:20 is not a universal waterfall.
7. Do not assume a distributor level exists without evidence.
8. Preserve GST-inclusive/exclusive status.
9. Mark refundable/non-refundable.
10. Unverified values are OPEN, never estimated.

## PHASE 0 — Research architecture
Canonical entities: Model/Brand, Government Programme, State/UT, Service, Provider, Channel Level, Fee Type, Commission Rule, Settlement Rule, Source/Evidence, Date/Version.

Channel labels, only where they exist: Central/Platform → State/Regional → Super/Master Distributor → District/City Distributor → Retailer/VLE/Agent → Customer.

Standard waterfall:
Customer pays → statutory/provider fee → service/processing fee → gross commission pool → retailer/VLE → distributor → super distributor → state/regional → platform/provider → GST/TDS/other tax → net settlement.

## PHASE 4 — Fee extraction taxonomy
A Entry: application, registration, onboarding, KYC, certification, franchise, licence.
B Network acquisition: retailer/VLE, district/city distributor, distributor, super distributor, master distributor, state/regional.
C Security/working capital: deposit, wallet, minimum float.
D Setup: interior, hardware, biometric, printer, scanner, POS, micro-ATM, signage, branding, software.
E Recurring: monthly platform, annual licence, royalty, subscription, maintenance.
F Renewal: annual/periodic renewal and re-certification.
G Service activation: PAN, insurance, travel, utility, micro-ATM, GST, accounting, loan, etc.
H Transaction economics: customer fee, provider fee, commission pool, channel shares, tax, net payout.

## PHASE 5 — Percentage/commission extraction
For each service capture: fixed ₹ or %, slab/bid/MDR/revenue-share basis; calculation base; retailer%; distributor%; super-distributor%; state/regional%; central/platform%; provider%; min/max; volume slab; geography; effective dates; exceptions.
Only calculate derived values from sourced inputs and label DERIVED.

## PHASE 6 — Source hierarchy
1 Government gazette/RFP/tender/circular
2 Official government portal/FAQ/rate card
3 Official company pricing/terms/brochure
4 Current partner agreement/channel document
5 Regulatory filing/annual report
6 CAG/PIB
7 Reputable industry source
8 Third-party franchise listing
9 Search snippet/blog

Third-party evidence must be labelled unverified until corroborated.

## PHASE 7 — Unit economics
For every major model build Lean, Standard and Premium outlet scenarios.
Monthly transactions by service × average commission = gross commission; add legitimate service revenue; subtract operating costs, platform/royalty, payment/bank charges and tax effects = net operating income.
Calculate break-even transactions, break-even revenue, payback, ROI and working capital using sourced transaction benchmarks wherever possible.

## PHASE 8 — Nivy proposed model
Only after evidence is collected, design:
Nivy Central → State/Regional Partner → Super/Master Distributor → District/City Distributor → Certified Retailer/Service Centre → Customer.
Acquisition paths: low-entry retailer/operator and higher-entry territory/network partner.
Graduation: Applicant → Certified → Pilot → Active → Premium → Distributor → Super Distributor/State Partner.
Revenue primarily tied to actual service/customer revenue, not recruitment.

## PHASE 9 — Fee distribution
Produce:
Layer A Observed Market: exact sourced fee/split.
Layer B Comparable Benchmark: range derived from multiple comparable models.
Layer C Nivy Proposed Economics: original commercial design, clearly marked PROPOSED.
Every proposed waterfall shows Customer price | provider/statutory cost | commission pool | retailer | distributor | super distributor | state partner | Nivy central | taxes | net.
