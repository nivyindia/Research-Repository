# Partner Health Scorecard

## Purpose
Standard monitoring framework for VLE/centre, distributor/district partner and future state/territory partner.

| Metric | Suggested weight |
|---|---:|
| Revenue / productive transactions | 20 |
| Customer retention | 15 |
| Service quality | 15 |
| Customer satisfaction | 10 |
| Compliance | 10 |
| Transaction activity | 10 |
| Training/certification | 5 |
| Reporting discipline | 5 |
| Complaint rate | 5 |
| Growth | 5 |

These weights are a proposed Nivy framework, not CSC official weights.

## Review cadence
- Daily: transactions, failures, cash/payment exceptions
- Weekly: sales, customers, unresolved complaints
- Monthly: economics, quality, partner score
- Quarterly: audit, capability re-certification, territory review

## Partner lifecycle
Applicant → KYC → Training → Certification → Pilot → Active Partner → Premium Partner → Territory/Franchise Partner.

## Failure controls
Low performance → coaching → written improvement plan → supervised period → suspension/termination only according to contract and applicable law.
