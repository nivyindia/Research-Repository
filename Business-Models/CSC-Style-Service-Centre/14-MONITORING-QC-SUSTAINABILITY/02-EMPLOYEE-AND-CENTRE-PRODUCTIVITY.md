# Employee & Centre Productivity

## Staffing principle
Start lean. Add headcount only when workload and unit economics justify it.

### Small centre
- Centre/Business Manager
- Client/Service Executive
- One operations specialist
- Shared/part-time specialist as required

### Medium centre
Add dedicated service specialists, sales/relationship and QC as transaction volume requires.

### Large hub
Separate operations, sales, training, production/service delivery and QC.

Exact staffing must be derived from measured workload, not a fixed headcount promise.

## Employee control loop
Role → KPI → Task → Output → QC → Customer result → Incentive → Training → Promotion/PIP.

## No-time-pass controls
- task queue
- SLA
- daily output log
- QC sampling
- exception report
- customer feedback
- weekly manager review

Attendance may be tracked, but output and quality should remain primary operational metrics, subject to employment law and contract terms.
