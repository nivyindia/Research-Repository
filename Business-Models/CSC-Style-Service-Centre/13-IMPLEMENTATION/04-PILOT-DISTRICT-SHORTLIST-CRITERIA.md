## 2. Selection criteria (score each district 1–5)

| # | Criterion | Why it matters | Evidence sources |
|---|-----------|----------------|------------------|
| 1 | **Support reachability** | L2/platform can visit or respond fast | Travel time, existing partner footprint |
| 2 | **Connectivity baseline** | Centres can run portals reliably | Local knowledge; avoid pure offline blocks for v1 |
| 3 | **Demand signal** | Citizens need assisted digital services | e-District volume proxies; field notes |
| 4 | **Competition honesty** | Dense CSC network is OK if Nivy differentiates on quality | PIB state density; local count estimates |
| 5 | **Recruitment feasibility** | Can find 5–15 serious L3 applicants | Lead gen test; camp response |
| 6 | **Compliance risk** | Lower history of fee abuse / brand confusion if known | Field intel; avoid if unmanageable |
| 7 | **DSP / gov channel clarity** | Understand local CSC 3.0 / DSP context without claiming it | UP CEG / tender public info |
| 8 | **Language / ops fit** | Training and fee list in working language | Hindi-first for most UP districts |

**Hard excludes:** No lawful service path for planned catalogue; no support owner; active legal conflict.

---

## 3. Scoring rule (draft)

- Shortlist districts with total score ≥ **28/40** and no hard exclude.  
- Prefer **one denser + one moderate** district only if support capacity allows.  
- Document scores in a simple sheet before recruitment launch.

---

## 4. Data to collect before locking names

- [ ] Current DSP names / tender references for candidate districts (public)  
- [ ] Rough existing CSC/VLE density (directional)  
- [ ] Travel/support plan from HQ or L2 base  
- [ ] 10+ warm L3 leads or camp plan  
- [ ] Authorised pilot service subset frozen

---

## 5. Non-goals

- Do not pick districts only for political visibility.  
- Do not claim partnership with DeGS/DSP.  
- Do not scale beyond support SLA capacity.
