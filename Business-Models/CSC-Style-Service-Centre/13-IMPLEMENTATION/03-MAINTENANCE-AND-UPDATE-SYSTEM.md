---

## 2. Source refresh rules

1. Prefer primary official URLs (gov, bank, CSC SPV, regulator).  
2. Third-party blogs = **attribution only**, never sole source for fees/authorisation.  
3. Date every figure; if undated, mark VERIFY.  
4. Historical vs current: separate sections; do not mix.  
5. Broken links: replace or mark dead with last-access date.
