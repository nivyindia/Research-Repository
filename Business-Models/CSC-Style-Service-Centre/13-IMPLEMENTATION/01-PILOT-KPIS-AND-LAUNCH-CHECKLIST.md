## 1. Pilot scope (draft)

| Dimension | Draft recommendation |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Geography | 1–2 districts in Uttar Pradesh (align with `11-STATE-AND-DISTRICT-MODELS/01-UTTAR-PRADESH-SNAPSHOT.md`) |
| Centres | 5–15 L3 Centre Partners |
| Duration | 90 days probation + 90 days steady observation |
| Services | Only Nivy-authorised catalogue subset with clear training |
| Tech | Partner portal + ticket + basic MIS (per tech blueprint) |

**Non-goals:** Claiming CSC status; statewide scale before pilot learnings; inventing commission tables.

---

## 2. Pilot KPIs (quality-first)

| KPI | Type | Draft target band | Notes |
|-----|------|-------------------|-------|
| Onboarding completion rate | Process | ≥80% of signed partners activated in 30 days | Training + hardware gate |
| Training certification rate | Process | 100% of live partners | Block activation without pass |
| Ticket first-response SLA | Ops | ≤1 business day | Support quality |
| Complaint rate per 100 txns | Quality | Track baseline; investigate spikes | Overcharge / process errors |
| Published fee list compliance | Compliance | 100% mystery checks pass | Brand + consumer protection |
| Brand guideline compliance | Brand | 100% (no CSC/gov marks) | Audit sample |
| Active days / week | Activity | ≥5 | Avoid ghost centres |
| MIS submission on time | Reporting | ≥90% | Monthly |
| Partner NPS / satisfaction | Experience | Baseline in pilot | Simple survey |
| Gross txn volume | Volume | Baseline only — no vanity targets that drive fraud | Quality over volume |

**Hard fail triggers:** fraud, data abuse, systematic overcharging, brand impersonation of CSC/government.

## 3. Launch checklist (pre-pilot)

### Product / ops
- [ ] Authorised service list freeze for pilot
- [ ] Fee list (citizen-facing) approved and template for display
- [ ] Ops daily checklist live (`06-OPERATIONS-AND-TRAINING`)
- [ ] Escalation matrix named owners
- [ ] Fraud / overcharge response playbook

### Commercial / legal
- [ ] Term sheet + agreement template legal-reviewed
- [ ] Disclaimer on all sales assets
- [ ] Territory rules written for pilot districts

### Tech
- [ ] Partner login provisioning process
- [ ] Ticket channel live
- [ ] Minimal MIS fields defined

### Sales / recruitment
- [ ] Funnel stages in CRM
- [ ] Application form fields = onboarding Stage A/B
- [ ] Sales team trained on non-claims language

### Partner readiness
- [ ] Onboarding checklist used for every pilot partner
- [ ] Hardware minimum list published
- [ ] Soft-launch dry run completed

## 4. During pilot — cadence

| Cadence | Activity |
|---------|----------|
| Daily | Ticket queue; critical incidents |
| Weekly | Partner check-in sample; KPI snapshot |
| Bi-weekly | Leadership pilot review |
| Day 30 / 60 / 90 | Formal probation gates |
| End of pilot | Retro + scale/no-scale decision memo |

---

## 5. Scale gates (exit criteria)

Proceed to broader recruitment only if:
1. No systemic compliance failures
2. Support SLAs met or plan to staff up
3. Unit economics assumptions updated with real pilot data (not invented)
4. Legal term sheet still valid
5. Tech stack stable for +N partners

---

## 6. Risk register (seed)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Partners market as CSC | Sales + brand audit + agreement termination clause |
| Overcharging citizens | Published list + mystery audit + penalty |
| Weak connectivity areas | Hardware/connectivity gate before activation |
| Support overload | Cap pilot headcount; tiered L2 support |
| Authorisation gap on a service | Remove from catalogue immediately |
