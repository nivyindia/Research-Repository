## 1. Funnel overview (three parallel tracks)

| Track | Primary audience | Typical entry | Primary CTA |
|-------|------------------|---------------|-------------|
| **A — Centre Partner (L3)** | Local entrepreneur / VLE-style operator | Web form, camp, referral | Apply for Centre Partner |
| **B — District / Distributor (L2)** | Existing multi-outlet operator or agency | Targeted outreach, webinar | Partner discovery call |
| **C — State / Master (L1)** | State-capable organisation | Warm intro, RFP-style, founder | Strategic partnership discussion |

All tracks share: lead qualification → due diligence → commercial term sheet → agreement → onboarding (see `05-FRANCHISE-PROGRAM-DESIGN/02-PARTNER-ONBOARDING-CHECKLIST.md`).

## 2. Lead stages (CRM-ready)

| Stage | Code | Definition | Exit criteria |
|-------|------|------------|---------------|
| 0 New | NEW | Lead captured, source tagged | Contact verified |
| 1 Contacted | CON | First human touch (call/WhatsApp/email) | Interest confirmed or closed-lost |
| 2 Qualified | QUAL | Meets basic eligibility + territory open | Application started |
| 3 Application | APP | Form + docs submitted | DD checklist complete |
| 4 Due diligence | DD | KYC, territory, conflict check | Clear or reject |
| 5 Proposal | PROP | Term sheet / proposal shared | Verbal/written accept |
| 6 Agreement | AGR | Legal agreement in process | Signed + fee/deposit if any |
| 7 Onboarding | ONB | Training + launch readiness | Activation |
| 8 Live | LIVE | Probation or full | KPI tracking |
| X Closed-lost | LOST | Reason coded | — |

**Required fields on every lead:** source, track (A/B/C), geography (state/district/pincode), channel owner, next action date.

## 3. Qualification checklist (lightweight)

### Centre Partner (L3)
- Adult resident; identity feasible
- Proposed public-facing location (photo or map pin)
- Digital literacy / willingness to train
- No hard exclusivity conflict disclosed
- Realistic expectation on investment (capex vs Nivy fees TBD)

### District / Distributor (L2)
- Existing footprint or recruitment capacity in target district(s)
- Ability to support multiple L3 partners
- Financial capacity for working capital / float (if model requires)
- Alignment with Nivy brand and compliance rules

### State / Master (L1)
- Organisational capability across multiple districts
- Prior channel or public-service delivery experience preferred
- Governance / compliance capacity
- Strategic fit (not claiming government DSP/SCA status)

## 6. Sales scripts — structure only

### Opening (call / WhatsApp)
- Confirm identity and interest track
- State clearly: independent Nivy programme, not CSC franchise
- Ask permission to explain model in 3 minutes

### Discovery questions
- Current activity / existing agencies?
- Location type (shop / home-front / dedicated)?
- Connectivity and device readiness?
- Territory preference?
- Investment comfort (ranges, not promises)?

### Objection handling themes (no scripts that invent fees)
- “Is this CSC?” → No; explain independent model and legal service access paths
- “What is the fee?” → Share only approved term sheet; never oral invent
- “Guaranteed income?” → No guarantees; quality + authorised services + local demand
- “Government scheme?” → Nivy private programme; any G2C only via lawful authorisation

### Close
- Share application link or document list
- Schedule DD call
- Log stage + next action in CRM

## 8. Mandatory disclaimer block (copy into every external sales asset)

> **Important:** Nivy Centre Partner / Distributor / Master programmes are independent commercial programmes of Nivy. They are **not** Common Service Centre (CSC) franchises, not MeitY/CSC SPV appointments, and not State Designated Agency or District Service Provider appointments unless a separate written government or CSC authorisation exists. Service delivery is limited to services for which Nivy or the partner holds lawful authorisation. Fees, commissions and territories are only those stated in the then-current written term sheet or agreement. No income is guaranteed. CSC name, logo and government emblems must not be used without licence.
