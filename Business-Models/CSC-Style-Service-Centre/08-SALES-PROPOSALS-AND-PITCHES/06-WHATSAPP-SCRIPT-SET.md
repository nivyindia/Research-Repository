# WhatsApp Script Set — Partner Recruitment (Structure)

**Folder:** `08-SALES-PROPOSALS-AND-PITCHES/`  
**Created:** 2026-10-04  
**Status:** Structure + safe language only  
**Blocked:** Specific fee/₹ figures until term-sheet freeze

---

## Rules

1. First message must not claim CSC franchise or government scheme.  
2. No income guarantee, no “earn ₹X”.  
3. Push to application form or discovery call.  
4. Log stage in CRM after each meaningful reply.

---

## A. Cold / lead first touch (Track A — Centre Partner)

```
Namaste {{Name}},

Nivy Centre Partner programme ke baare mein aapka interest note kiya.
Yeh independent commercial programme hai — CSC franchise ya government appointment nahi.

Short mein: local assisted digital services centre, training + support ke saath.
Income guarantee nahi hoti; details written term sheet mein hoti hain.

Kya 10-minute discovery call schedule karein, ya application link bhejun?

Dhanyavaad,
{{Sender}}
```

---

## B. After interest confirmed

```
Shukriya {{Name}}.

Aage ke steps:
1) Basic eligibility (location, digital readiness)
2) Application + documents
3) Due diligence
4) Written proposal / term sheet
5) Training + activation checklist

CSC logo ya government emblem use allowed nahi (bina alag licence ke).

Application: {{link}}
FAQ: {{link}}

Koi sawal ho to yahan likhein.
```

---

## C. Objection — “CSC franchise hai kya?”

```
Nahi — Nivy CSC SPV / MeitY appointment nahi deta.
Agar aapke paas pehle se legal CSC/BC authorisation hai, woh alag rehta hai.
Nivy ke under sirf authorised catalogue services, clear branding rules ke saath.

Detail FAQ: {{link}}
```

---

## D. Objection — “Kitna kamayenge?”

```
Hum guaranteed income claim nahi karte.
Kamai local demand, authorised services, aapki operations quality, aur costs par depend karti hai.
Koi bhi ₹/month figure oral promise nahi — sirf approved written term sheet.

Discovery call par categories of investment explain kar sakte hain (bina invented rates ke).
```

---

## E. Follow-up (Day 3 / Day 7)

```
{{Name}}, pehle message follow-up.
Agar abhi explore karna hai to application / call link yahan hai: {{link}}
Agar interest nahi, reply “STOP” — hum disturb nahi karenge.
```
