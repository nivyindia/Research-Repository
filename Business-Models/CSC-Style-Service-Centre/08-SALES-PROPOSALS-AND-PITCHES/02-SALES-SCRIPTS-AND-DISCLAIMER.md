## A. Universal disclaimer (every external touch)

Use the block from `01-SALES-FUNNEL-AND-PROPOSAL-SKELETON.md` section 8 on:
- Website / landing pages
- WhatsApp first messages (short form allowed — see B)
- Email signatures for sales
- Proposal cover / slide 2

> Nivy Centre Partner is an independent programme of Nivy — **not** a CSC franchise or government appointment. Services only where lawfully authorised. Fees only as per written term sheet. No income guarantee. Reply YES for details or STOP to opt out.

## B. Call flow — Centre Partner (structure)

1. **Permission** — “Is this a good time for 5 minutes on the Nivy centre partner idea?”
2. **Positioning** — Independent assisted digital centre model; explicitly not CSC franchise.
3. **Discovery** — location, existing work, devices, interest in training.
4. **Value** — authorised service access path + training + portal + brand rules + support (no invented commission %).
5. **Process** — application → KYC → agreement → training → launch.
6. **Objection** — map to approved FAQ; escalate fee questions to written term sheet only.
7. **Close** — send application link + document list; book follow-up.
8. **Log** — CRM stage, notes, next action.

**Do not say:** guaranteed earnings, “official CSC”, “government franchise”, specific unapproved rupee figures.

## C. Objection → response themes

| Objection | Theme |
|-----------|--------|
| Is this CSC? | Independent Nivy programme; CSC is separate ecosystem; we do not claim CSC appointment. |
| How much can I earn? | Depends on authorised services, local demand, quality; no guarantee; share only approved economics materials. |
| What is joining fee? | Only figures on current written term sheet; send PDF / link. |
| Do I get government work? | Only if a lawful authorisation path exists for that service; not automatic. |
| Can I use CSC board? | No — brand guidelines forbid CSC/gov marks without licence. |
| Territory exclusive? | Policy is density-friendly with performance; exclusivity only if written. |
