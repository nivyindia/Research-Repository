## Critical answers (must stay consistent everywhere)

### Is this a CSC franchise?
**No.** Nivy Centre Partner / Distributor / Master programmes are independent commercial programmes. They are **not** Common Service Centre (CSC) franchises, not MeitY/CSC SPV appointments, and not State Designated Agency or District Service Provider appointments unless a separate written government or CSC authorisation exists.

### Is income guaranteed?
**No.** No income, revenue, or profit is guaranteed. Earnings depend on lawful authorised services, local demand, operator skill, compliance, and costs. Any third-party “earn ₹X/month” claims about similar businesses are marketing — not Nivy promises.

### Can I use CSC or government logos?
**No**, unless you hold a separate written licence. Nivy branding rules forbid CSC, MeitY, Digital India, or government emblems without authorisation.

### What services can I offer?
Only services in the **current Nivy-authorised catalogue** for which a lawful access path exists (provider agreement, portal agent status, etc.). Catalogue can change; unauthorised services must stop immediately.

### Is there a registration fee?
Only fees stated in the **then-current written term sheet or agreement**. Do not rely on oral quotes. (CSC national VLE registration is separately documented as free on official FAQ — that is not a Nivy fee policy.)

### What investment do I need?
Typical categories: space, hardware, connectivity, working capital/float (if payment services), and any Nivy fees per term sheet. Exact amounts are partner-specific and must come from approved commercial documents.

### How does territory work?
Territory rules (density, soft exclusivity, or performance-based) are defined in the agreement. Dense markets (e.g. large CSC presence in some states) favour quality and support over hard exclusivity claims.

### What training is required?
Partners must complete required training and practical assessment before activation. Certification is a launch gate in the pilot model.

### How are citizen fees set?
Published centre fee list only. Overcharging is a compliance hard-fail. Official prescribed fees for government services must not be inflated with hidden charges.

### How do I apply?
Submit the application form / book a discovery call. Stages: contact → qualification → application → due diligence → proposal → agreement → onboarding.

### What if I already run a CSC or BC outlet?
You may still explore Nivy only for services you are lawfully allowed to offer under Nivy rules. Existing CSC/BC status does **not** transfer to Nivy and does not allow CSC branding under Nivy.

### Who do I contact for complaints (citizens)?
Nivy published grievance number (to be assigned for pilot). CSC network has its own channels (e.g. 14599) which are not Nivy support lines.
