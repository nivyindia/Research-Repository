## Implications for Nivy business-model research

1. Market is **large and dense** in major states (UP alone >1.1 lakh centres) — any parallel commercial network must differentiate on brand, service mix, support, and compliance, not on “being the only centre.”
2. Scale implies strong **platform + VLE** economics already exist; Nivy unit economics must be built from scratch with transparent assumptions, not by copying unpublished CSC internal shares.
3. Women VLE share (~13.5% of 5.16 lakh) is a public policy signal for inclusive partner recruitment design.
