# Creator Pipeline & Career Ladder

Discover → Assessment → Starter Training → Practical Pilot → Certified Creator → Growth Program → Monetization → Management → Brand Deals → Commerce/Own Business → Nivy Partner/Promoter → Centre/Franchise Candidate.

Track baseline and outcomes: content consistency, reach, retention, engagement, qualified leads, conversions, revenue and brand safety.

Creators can progress into editor, manager, trainer, scout or centre roles where capability supports it.
