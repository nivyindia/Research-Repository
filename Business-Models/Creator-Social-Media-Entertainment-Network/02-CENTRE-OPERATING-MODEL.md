# Creator Growth Centre Operating Model

## Small centre
Manager/relationship + creator/content operator + editor + shared digital marketing/production specialists.

## Services
Content planning, shooting, editing, thumbnails, channel/profile optimization, legitimate paid promotion, analytics, monetization strategy, brand outreach and campaign execution.

## Centre controls
Centre ID, creator ID, project ID, task/SLA, approval trail, payment ledger, campaign results, customer feedback and monthly health score.

## Scale rule
Pilot company-controlled centres first; partner centres after SOP, economics, training and QC are proven.
