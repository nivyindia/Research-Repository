# Partner Growth → Franchise Conversion Model

Reusable Nivy model: first supply clients/leads and enable a local operator to earn through commission or revenue share; later convert proven operators into upgraded partners or franchisees.

Core principle:
Value first → client supply → earning proof → trust → upgrade → franchise/territory expansion.

Lifecycle:
1. Select local operator.
2. Start as non-franchise partner.
3. Supply qualified leads, clients or projects.
4. Operator delivers under SOPs.
5. Nivy earns commission/revenue share.
6. Review performance over a proof period such as six months.
7. Offer upgraded partner/franchise package after value is demonstrated.
8. Upgrade can include branding, marketing material, signage, additional services, technology, training and customer-area standards.
9. High-potential operators can receive a premium service-centre format.
10. Expand local → district → state → country/international.

Applicable to GST/tax/accounting, CSC-style services, digital/IT, recruitment, education, construction, real estate, distributors and international partners.

Important: this is a research model. Fees, commissions, exclusivity, advertising claims and service authorization require separate validation for each vertical and jurisdiction.
