# Model Design

## Partner-first sales
Do not lead with “buy a franchise”. Lead with: “Work with us; we help bring clients/projects and you earn from delivery.”

## Partner phase
- Low/no initial franchise barrier
- Lead/project allocation
- Commission or revenue share
- Delivery SOPs
- Quality control
- CRM and performance tracking
- Trust building

## Conversion assessment
Review revenue, repeat customers, quality, turnaround time, customer satisfaction, compliance, reliability, capacity and local market potential.

## Upgrade package
Potential components:
- additional/high-margin services
- training
- branding kit
- banners, pamphlets and signage
- marketing support
- CRM/technology
- customer waiting/resting area
- premium centre design
- territory rights where appropriate

## Premium centre
For strong operators/locations: larger premises, professional reception, comfortable waiting area, AC/cooling where justified, standardized identity, service display and local marketing.

## Revenue architecture
Commission, revenue share, service margin, subscription, training, upgrade fee, franchise fee, technology/support fee and territory economics.

Every commercial number requires unit-economics validation.
