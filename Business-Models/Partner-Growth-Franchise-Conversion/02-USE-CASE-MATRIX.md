# Use Case Matrix

| Vertical | Initial offer | Partner earning | Upgrade |
|---|---|---|---|
| GST/Tax | Leads + workflow | Commission/service margin | Branded centre |
| CSC-style | Customer leads + enablement | Service margin/share | Upgraded centre |
| Accounting | SME clients | Recurring revenue share | Partner office |
| Digital/IT | Clients/projects | Project margin/share | Agency partner |
| Recruitment | Employer/candidate leads | Placement commission | Recruitment partner |
| Construction | Project leads | Project commission/margin | Distributor/franchise |
| Real estate | Buyer/seller leads | Transaction commission | Territory partner |
| International | Local clients | Revenue share | Country/territory partner |

Economics must be researched separately for each vertical.
