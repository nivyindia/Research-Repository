# Business Models — Master Index

## Purpose
Central map of reusable business-model research and implementation programmes.

## Models
1. CSC-Style-Service-Centre — citizen/business service centre, VLE, service portfolio, unit economics, partner/franchise, operations, state models, monitoring.
2. GST-Suvidha-Kendra — GST/tax-focused service-centre research; kept separate from Academy training.
3. Partner-Growth-Franchise-Conversion — prove value and partner earnings first, then upgrade to premium partner/franchise.
4. Tier-3-4-Talent-Academy-Jobs — talent discovery → training → practical work → jobs → career progression.
5. Creator-Social-Media-Entertainment-Network — YouTube, social, influencers, media/news creators, entertainment and other creator careers.
6. Marketing-Agency-Growth-Network — digital marketing, lead generation, SEO/PPC, social, content, CRM/automation and growth services.
7. Vyapari-Trade-Business-Solutions-Network — merchant/SME/entrepreneur growth and business-support centre model.
8. Network-Operating-System — shared lifecycle, entities, controls, finance, QC and monitoring across all models.

## Shared architecture
Lead → Diagnose → Demonstrate/Pilot → Contract → Deliver → QC → Settle → Measure Outcome → Retain → Upgrade → Partner/Franchise.

## Separation rule
Keep domain-specific evidence and economics in the relevant model. Shared operating principles can be reused through Network-Operating-System.

## Research discipline
REUSE → ADAPT → INTEGRATE → BUILD ONLY WHAT IS MISSING.

All factual claims used for implementation should carry provenance/evidence status. Do not mix proposed Nivy economics with verified third-party economics.
