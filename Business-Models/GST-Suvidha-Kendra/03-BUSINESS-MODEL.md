# Business Model Reverse Engineering

## Network layers
Research separately:
1. Brand/operator
2. GSP/ASP/technology provider
3. Master distributor
4. Distributor
5. Franchise/centre
6. Retailer/referral point
7. End customer

For each layer capture: entry requirements, investment, territory, services, technology access, responsibilities, revenue, commission, payout, support and risks.

## Revenue engines
- Direct service fees
- Backend/service commissions
- Recurring compliance services
- Referral commissions
- Transaction commissions
- Wallet/coupon economics
- Subscription/software fees
- Device/utility margins
- Partner network overrides

## Economics
Create a service-level unit economics table:
Customer price → backend cost → gross margin → partner share → retailer share → distributor share → operator share → taxes/fees → net contribution.

Advertised income is not to be treated as guaranteed.
