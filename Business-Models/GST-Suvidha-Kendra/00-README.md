# GST Suvidha Kendra / GST Seva Centre Business Research

## Objective
Research and reverse-engineer the publicly documented GST/tax/compliance/financial-service centre and franchise/distributor/retailer models, with GST Suvidha Kendra as the primary reference and comparable operators as secondary references.

## Scope exclusion
**Digital marketing is explicitly OUT OF SCOPE.** Do not import website design, SEO, Google/Facebook ads, social-media management, logo/creative design, or other digital-marketing services into the core GST-centre model. If a source bundles such services, record them separately as EXCLUDED.

## Reuse rule
REUSE → ADAPT → INTEGRATE → BUILD ONLY WHAT IS MISSING.
Reuse only material that is public and legally reusable (e.g. facts, service categories, publicly documented process concepts, links, open/licensed assets). Do not reproduce copyrighted text, proprietary training, private CRM screens, customer data, trademarks, logos, or confidential franchise material. Create Nivy-owned equivalents for anything protected.

## Target outcome
A complete, evidence-backed model covering:
1. Service catalogue
2. Customer segments and use cases
3. Centre/retailer/distributor/master-distributor hierarchy
4. Revenue and commission mechanics
5. Pricing/package structure
6. Partner/vendor/GSP/ASP dependencies
7. Licensing, eligibility and compliance requirements
8. Onboarding/KYC/document workflow
9. Service delivery SOPs
10. Training curriculum
11. CRM/app/portal requirements
12. Support/helpdesk/RM structure
13. Lead/referral/channel model — excluding digital marketing
14. Retailer/distributor recruitment
15. Territory/network model
16. Payout/reconciliation model
17. Forms, agreements and operational templates
18. Physical branding/material requirements
19. Risks, legal/IP boundaries and verification
20. Nivy-adapted implementation blueprint

## Evidence classes
- OFFICIAL: operator/Govt/regulator source
- PRIMARY-PUBLIC: published brochure/PDF/website from operator
- SECONDARY: credible third-party article
- INFERENCE: our derived business-model interpretation
- UNVERIFIED: claim requiring confirmation

Every important claim must carry source URL, date checked, evidence class and status.

## Completion rule
Do not mark a module COMPLETE until the primary source or at least two independent sources support it, where applicable.
