# Research Plan

## Phase 1 — Primary operator
Study GST Suvidha Kendra public pages, franchise pages, distributor/retailer pages, PDFs, service lists, pricing, commission references, training, onboarding, CRM/app, support, FAQs, terms and legal disclosures.

## Phase 2 — Comparable operators
Find comparable Indian models in:
- GST/tax filing facilitation
- accounting/compliance franchise
- business registration/document services
- PAN/DSC/FSSAI/MSME/IEC facilitation
- assisted digital financial services
- AEPS/BBPS/DMT/Micro-ATM (only where legally authorised)
- insurance/loan referral networks
- travel/utility add-on centres
- CSC-like assisted service networks

Use competitors to fill gaps and validate which components are industry-standard rather than unique to one brand.

## Phase 3 — Government/regulatory verification
Verify service authority and actual legal route using official sources: GSTN/GST portal, MCA, Income Tax, Udyam, FSSAI/FoSCoS, DGFT, Protean/NSDL or relevant PAN authority, UIDAI where applicable, RBI/payment-system rules, IRDAI, NPCI/BBPS and other relevant regulators/authorities.

## Phase 4 — Commercial reverse engineering
Capture:
- entry fee/license
- recurring fees
- wallet/security deposit/coupon mechanics
- retail/distributor/master-distributor economics
- commission per service
- payout frequency
- minimum volume
- territory rules
- onboarding/KYC
- support costs
- technology fees
- hardware/dongle/device dependencies

Do not assume advertised earnings are verified; label them as operator claims unless independently substantiated.

## Phase 5 — Operational reverse engineering
Map:
Lead → enquiry → KYC → document collection → order → verification → backend processing → approval/status → delivery → invoice → commission → reconciliation → support/complaint → renewal/recurring service.

## Phase 6 — Asset discovery
Find publicly downloadable:
- brochures
- service catalogues
- franchise decks
- commission tables
- training videos
- FAQs
- application forms
- checklists
- process diagrams
- sample agreements
- public terms/policies
- public marketing collateral

Store links and metadata first. Only copy/reuse files when licensing/permission permits.

## Phase 7 — Nivy adaptation
Build a clean-room Nivy model from verified facts and reusable patterns. Do not use GST Suvidha Kendra branding or imply affiliation.
