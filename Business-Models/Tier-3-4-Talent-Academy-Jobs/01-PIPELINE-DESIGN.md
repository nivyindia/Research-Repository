# Talent Pipeline Design

## Discover
Colleges, training institutes, hostels/community networks, social platforms, referrals, local events and Nivy Academy.

## Entry learning
Examples: free GST basics, introductory digital marketing, basic accounting and VA fundamentals.

## Assessment
Aptitude, accuracy, communication, learning speed, reliability, tools and role-specific competency.

## Training ladder
Free → Foundation → Professional → Advanced → Specialist → Lead/Trainer.

## Controlled real work
Give supervised, bounded assignments. Professional/compliance work must have appropriate review.

## Paid work
Successful candidates enter paid internship, project work or probation.

## Nivy Jobs
Place candidates into Nivy internal teams, partner companies or remote client delivery teams.

## Career progression
Specialist → Senior → QC → Team Lead → Trainer/Partner.

Quality principle: no free labour disguised as training. Real client work requires defined scope, supervision, payment rules and accountability.
