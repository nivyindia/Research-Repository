# Marketing Agency & Growth Network

## Scope
Digital marketing, SEO, PPC, social media management, content, lead generation, web presence, CRM/automation, reputation management and performance marketing for creators, merchants, SMEs and larger businesses.

## Model
Audit → small demonstration → paid pilot → recurring service → premium growth → performance/revenue-linked engagement where appropriate → partner/territory opportunity.

## Operating model
Central Nivy creates SOPs, technology, training, campaign frameworks and quality controls; local centres acquire/manage clients; specialists deliver.

## Revenue
Setup fees, retainers, campaign management fees, production packages, performance fees where measurable, referrals and premium services.

## Principle
Do not promise guaranteed rankings, leads, followers or revenue. Define baseline, target metrics, attribution and reporting.
