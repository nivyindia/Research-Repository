# Marketing Agency Service Matrix

Service families:
- Social media management
- YouTube/channel growth support
- Content strategy/production
- SEO
- PPC/paid media
- Lead generation
- Local marketing
- Website/landing pages
- CRM and automation
- Email marketing
- Reputation/review management
- Analytics/reporting
- Brand strategy

Each service must have: ICP, deliverables, SLA, price, direct cost, specialist cost, centre share, central share, partner share, KPI, risks, compliance and evidence source.
