## BDE formula

**Total = Performance (70%) + Work Ethics (30%)**  
Update weekly (momentum) + monthly (incentives).

### Performance (70%)

| Metric | Measure |
|--------|---------|
| Leads generated | Qualified leads in CRM |
| Conversion rate | Sales / leads × 100 |
| Revenue | Closed deal value |
| Social media reach | New prospects messaged (LI/IG etc.) |
| Target achievement | % of weekly/monthly target |

### Work ethics (30%)

| Metric | Measure |
|--------|---------|
| Punctuality | On-time reports, meetings, shift |
| Accountability | Own mistakes, early blockers, process follow |
| Initiative | Volunteer, ideas, help peers |
| Communication quality | Chats, emails, client calls, daily reports |
| Screen time efficiency | e.g. 7.5 hrs active FT focused |

### Tiers

| Tier | Name | Criteria | Rewards (as stated) |
|------|------|----------|---------------------|
| 1 | Elite Closer | Top 10% | ₹5,000 + BDE of Month + company page |
| 2 | Ace Performer | Next 20% | ₹2,500 + certificate |
| 3 | Core Achiever | Next 40% | ₹1,000 + manager message |
| 4 | Rising Star | Next 20% (new/trainee) | Recognition + mentorship priority |
| 5 | Improvement Zone | Bottom 10% | Coaching + PIP |

### Time-based incentives

- First conversion of day → ₹200 + shoutout  
- Social Media Star (week) → ₹500  
- Perfect Week (100% target + ethics) → ₹1,000  
- Team Player of Month (peer vote) → ₹1,500 + trophy  

Ops: daily Workfolio + CRM; weekly Friday scores; monthly lock + payouts first week next month.

## What companies look for (fresher framing)

**Performance:** goal achievement, quality, productivity, reliability.  
**Ethics:** ownership, proactiveness, teamwork, adaptability, integrity, positive attitude, communication, punctuality.  
Interview tip: STAR stories; on job: early, speak up, volunteer, help, honest with solutions.

## HR formula

**Total = Performance (60%) + Work Ethics (40%)**  
Monthly leaderboard; quarterly incentive lock.

### HR performance (60%)

Time-to-hire | Quality of hire (probation + 6-mo scores) | Retention (voluntary attrition) | Training effectiveness | Process adherence (payroll/compliance errors) | Social reach (employer branding posts)

### HR ethics (40%)

Integrity/confidentiality (zero breaches) | Empathy/support (360 feedback) | Proactiveness (initiatives) | Fairness/objectivity (360) | Discretion/tact (manager assessment)

### HR tiers

| Tier | Name | Rewards (as stated) |
|------|------|---------------------|
| 1 | Culture Champion | ₹7,000 + title/trophy + page feature |
| 2 | People's Leader | ₹4,000 + certificate |
| 3 | HR Star | ₹2,000 + meeting shoutout |
| 4 | Rising Guardian | Mentorship + L&D voucher |
| 5 | Focus Area | Training + PIP |

### HR spot incentives

Spot award critical incident ₹3,000; Social Media Star ₹2,000; Zero-Error Club (quarter) ₹5,000; Employee's Choice ₹3,000 + trophy.
