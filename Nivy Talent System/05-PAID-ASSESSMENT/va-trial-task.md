- Owner/Founder Name (if available)

- Google: “digital marketing agency USA”
- LinkedIn: Founder / CEO search

- Clutch.co / agency directories

### Output Format

| Founder Name | Agency | Contact | Website | Founder LinkedIn |
|--------------|--------|---------|---------|------------------|
| John Smith   | ABC Marketing | abc@gmail.com | www.abc.com | https://… |

### Proof Required
- Lead list (Google Sheet / Excel / Email body)
- Optional: screenshots of sources

## Task 2: Outreach Messaging (Exactly 50 messages)

- Only to USA-based agencies
- Use the official script below (do not change meaning)
- Personalize name where possible
- Professional, no spam blasting

### Official Message Script

> Hi [Name],  
> I came across your agency and really liked your work in the US market.  
> I’m reaching out because we help agencies improve client acquisition using structured lead generation systems that bring consistent inbound leads.  
> Would you be open to a quick conversation to explore if this could help your agency?  
> Regards,  
> [Your Name]

### Proof Required
- Exactly 50 screenshots of sent messages
- Each must clearly show: recipient name, message sent, chat visibility
