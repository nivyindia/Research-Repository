## Brief to candidate

Thank you for interest in the HR Executive role. This assignment gauges potential, foundational skills, and alignment with HR values. Attention to detail, professionalism, and timeliness are part of evaluation.

**Submission:** Single PDF named `YourName_HRExecutive_Assignment.pdf` emailed by stated deadline.

---

## Part 1: Communication & Professional Presence (15%)

Record a **1-minute video** (MP4/MOV):

- Who you are and academic background  
- Motivation for HR career  
- One value you would bring as an HR professional  

**Looking for:** Clarity, confidence, professional demeanor, conciseness.

---

## Part 2: HR Mindset & Employee Engagement (15%)

Scenario: HR rep for a team of 20 employees.

1. Propose **two initiatives** for a motivated, positive environment.  
2. Suggest **one essential policy** for a small company + one-sentence reason.  

**Looking for:** Empathy, creativity, core HR understanding, logic.

---

## Part 3: Written Communication & Etiquette (15%)

Draft a short email to **Ms. Ananya Sharma** inviting her to interview for **Marketing Associate**.

Include: clear subject, professional greeting, proposed date/time example, polite close with name + title.

**Looking for:** Format, clarity, grammar, punctuation.

---

## Part 4: Understanding of Professionalism (10%)

Answer in 3–4 sentences:  
“What does professionalism mean to you in the context of HR, and how do you intend to embody it?”

**Looking for:** Integrity, discretion, confidentiality, ethical commitment.

---

## Part 5: Problem-Solving & Stakeholder Management (10%)

Scenario: Candidate confirmed interview slot, no-showed with no prior communication.

Outline immediate action plan in 3–4 sentences (steps within first hour).

**Looking for:** Practical, patient, solution-oriented response.

---

## Part 6: Proficiency with HR Tools & Documentation (25%)

1. **Excel/Sheets:** Weekly attendance tracker for 5 employees (Mon–Fri); P/A codes; grey weekend columns; title, names, dates.  
2. **Docs:** One-page offer letter for a fresher role (e.g. Junior HR Executive) — title, compensation, start date, location, at-will if applicable, HR contact.  
3. **Slides:** 2-slide deck — (1) Strategic role of HR in a modern company; (2) Three key skills for successful HR pros.  
4. **Research:** LinkedIn profile of HR Head at TCS — paste URL; list 2 key skills from profile.  

**Looking for:** Accuracy, formatting, professional docs, basic research.

---

## Part 7: Commitment to Timeliness (10%)

Submit complete assignment by deadline.

**Looking for:** Reliability, discipline, respect for deadlines.
