## Purpose

Fresher-friendly filter for Business Development Executive / Sales. No prior sales experience required. Surfaces communication, curiosity, writing, persuasion, resilience, punctuality, and basic tools skill.

---

## Part 1: Communication & Confidence

Record a **1-minute video** introducing yourself:

- Who you are  
- Your strengths  
- Why you are excited to join as a BDE  

Variant: introduce yourself as if talking to a potential client (name, background, why they should listen). Mobile video is fine.

**Checks:** Confidence, fluency, energy, clarity.

---

## Part 2: Observation & Business Curiosity

Pick any brand/company (e.g. Swiggy, Amazon, Infosys, Zomato, Byju’s, Tata Motors).

- 2–4 sentences: why they are successful  
- 1–3 sentences: one way they could improve  

**Checks:** Curiosity, basic market understanding, critical thinking.

---

## Part 3: Writing & Professional Skills

Write a short **email** to placement officer / teacher requesting a meeting.

Must include: subject line, greeting, body, closing. Professional and polite.

**Checks:** Grammar, punctuation, politeness, formatting.

---

## Part 4: Persuasion (Sales Potential)

Convince a friend to try a new learning / food / app product in **5–6 sentences**.

**Checks:** Convincing power, creativity, tone.

---

## Part 5: Resilience & Work Ethics

Answer in 3–4 sentences one of:

- “If you get 20 rejections in a day, what will you do the next morning?”  
- “If 10 people said ‘No’ in a row, how will you motivate yourself to keep trying?”

**Checks:** Positive attitude, work ethics, seriousness.

---

## Part 6: Punctuality Test

Submit within the given deadline (e.g. 48 hours).

**Checks:** Reliability, punctuality, commitment.

---

## Part 7: Technical Skills (Basic Tools)

1. Create a simple Excel sheet with **5 fake** client names, phone numbers, emails.  
2. Save as `YourName_BDE_Assignment.xlsx` and submit with the assignment.

**Checks:** Excel basics, file handling, attention to detail.

---

## Optional: Training & learning speed

Share a short 5–10 min product video/PDF. Ask candidate to write **3 key features** in own words + **1-min sales pitch**.

**Checks:** Trainability.  
**Red flag:** “Didn’t understand,” “Too much work,” or avoidance.
