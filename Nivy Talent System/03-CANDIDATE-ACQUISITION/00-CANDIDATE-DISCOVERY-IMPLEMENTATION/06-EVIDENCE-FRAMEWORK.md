# Candidate Evidence Framework

## Evidence principle

Separate:

Claim → Evidence → Observation → Outcome

A CV or interview statement is a claim. A completed role-specific task is stronger evidence. Repeated work under normal conditions is stronger still.

## Evidence levels

| Level | Evidence | Use |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | no evidence | discovery only |
| E1 | CV/self-report | initial screening |
| E2 | structured screen | moderate signal |
| E3 | practical task | capability evidence |
| E4 | repeated paid tasks | reliability + capability |
| E5 | controlled real work | operational evidence |
| E6 | internship/probation outcome | downstream evidence |

## Reliability evidence

Track:
- deadline adherence
- instruction following
- blocker communication
- correction response
- documentation
- consistency
- supervision required

## Evidence record

For each competency:
- requirement
- candidate claim
- evidence source
- artifact/reference
- reviewer
- result
- confidence
- missing evidence
- next action

## Weak proxies to avoid

Do not substitute college prestige, CV polish, confidence, certificates alone, demographic attributes or city for job-relevant evidence.

## AI role

AI may extract claims, summarise work, identify missing evidence and suggest the next evidence step.

AI must not independently make irreversible hiring decisions.
