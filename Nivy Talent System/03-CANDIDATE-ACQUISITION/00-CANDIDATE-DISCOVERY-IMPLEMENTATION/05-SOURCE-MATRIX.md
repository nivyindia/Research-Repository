## Source-quality measurement

For each source/campaign track:

discovered → applied → eligible → screened → assessed → consistency completed → verified talent → hired/interned/projected → successful outcome → retained/re-engaged

Applicant volume is not source quality.

## Institution partner record

Organisation, type, city/region, website, public business contact, placement/recruitment contact where publicly listed, roles supplied, campaign history, response, candidate outcomes, active/inactive.

Do not collect private personal contact information without an appropriate basis.

## Community rules

Use official/approved channels and platform rules. Track join source, preferences, engagement, applications and unsubscribe/leave.

Do not build around unsolicited bulk messaging, fake accounts, credential sharing or prohibited scraping.
