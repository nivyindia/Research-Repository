# Nivy VA Hiring Funnel — Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

This SOP defines **exactly** what team members must do at each stage.  
Based on the final hiring + training integrated system.

---

## Core Operating Rules

- Deadlines are **STRICT** (no manual extension)
- No follow-ups in hiring stages
- Email = control system
- WhatsApp = notification only (except training funnel)
- Every action must be tracked in Google Sheet

---

## Team Roles

### 1. Funnel Manager
- Controls entire pipeline
- Decides batch movement
- Monitors performance metrics

### 2. Operations Executive
- Sends emails (manual/trigger)
- Updates Google Sheet
- Moves candidates between stages

### 3. Evaluator
- Checks test (if needed)
- Evaluates trial tasks
- Takes interviews

### 4. Training Manager
- Handles free training
- Tracks performance
- Decides retention/removal

---

## Stage 0: Job Awareness

### Tasks
- Prepare candidate list (50 per batch)
- Send Job Description Email (Email 1)
- Include: Job details, 48-hour deadline, Test link, WhatsApp links

### Track
- Email sent time
- Deadline expiry

---

## Stage 1: Application + Test

### Automation
- Google Form collects responses
- Auto scoring enabled

### Tasks (Ops)
- Monitor submissions
- Ensure data is recorded

### Email
- Auto Email: Submission Confirmation

### Decision
- PASS → Move to Stage 2
- FAIL / NO SUBMISSION → Move to PAID TRAINING PATH

---

## Stage 2: Trial Task

### Tasks
- Send Task Email (Email 4)
- Include: Task details, Submission format, Deadline (24 hrs)

### WhatsApp
- Send reminder (optional, minimal)

### Evaluation
- Evaluator checks: Accuracy, Execution, Reporting quality

### Decision
- PASS → Move to Stage 3
- FAIL / NO SUBMISSION → PAID TRAINING PATH

---

## Stage 3: Interview

### Tasks
- Send Interview Email (with Calendly)
- Track bookings

### Rules
- Book within 24 hrs
- Attend within 48 hrs

### Evaluation
- Communication, Thinking ability, Confidence

### Decision
- PASS → Stage 4
- FAIL → PAID TRAINING PATH

---

## Stage 4: Orientation

### Tasks
- Send orientation details
- Conduct session

### Check
- Attendance, Seriousness

### Decision
- ATTENDED → Internal Selection
- ABSENT → PAID TRAINING PATH (optional)

---

## Internal: Selection + Agreement

### Tasks
- Select top 10 candidates
- Send offer + agreement
- Collect signed agreement (24 hrs)

### Decision
- SIGNED → Free Training
- NOT SIGNED → Replace candidate

---

## Stage 5: Free Training (3 Months)

### Tasks
- Assign daily work
- Monitor KPIs
- Track attendance

### Metrics
- Performance, Discipline, Consistency

### Decision
- HIGH → Final Hiring
- LOW → PAID TRAINING PATH / EXIT

---

## Stage 6: Final Evaluation

### Tasks
- Final review
- Conduct final task/interview

### Decision
- PASS → Job Offer
- FAIL → PAID TRAINING PATH

---

## Paid Training SOP (Conversion System)

### Trigger Points
- Stage 1 Fail
- Stage 2 Fail
- Stage 3 Fail
- Stage 5 Low Performance

### Email Sequence (Auto)
Day 1 → Feedback  
Day 2 → Course Details  
Day 3 → Proof  
Day 4 → Urgency  
Day 5 → Final Call

### WhatsApp SOP
- Send testimonials
- Answer queries
- Push conversion

### Outcome
- PURCHASE → Paid Training Batch
- NO PURCHASE → Nurture Pool

---

## Tracking System (Mandatory)

Maintain Google Sheet with:
- Name
- Email
- Stage
- Status (Pass/Fail)
- Deadline
- Source
- Notes

---

## Daily Operations Checklist

- Check new applications
- Check deadlines expiring
- Send pending emails
- Update sheet
- Evaluate tasks/interviews
- Monitor training batch

---

## Final System Principle

Hiring Funnel = Filtering System  
Paid Training = Monetization + Talent Upgrade

No candidate wasted.  
Every candidate = Value.

This SOP ensures the system runs like a machine with minimal manual confusion.
