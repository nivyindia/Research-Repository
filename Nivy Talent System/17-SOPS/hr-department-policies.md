## Purpose & scope

Standards for managing workforce, labour-law compliance, well-being, productive workplace. Applies to HR team, hiring managers, and anyone in recruitment, training, payroll, performance, employee relations.

---

## 3.1 Recruitment & hiring

- Follow approved manpower plans and budgets  
- JDs up-to-date and approved before posting  
- Ads approved by HR before release  
- Fair, merit-based, non-discriminatory evaluation  
- Background checks before offer (history, references, criminal where legal)  

## 3.2 Onboarding & orientation

Formal orientation: history, mission, values; rules/policies; benefits/payroll; safety/compliance. Employment forms, contracts, IDs complete before day one.

## 3.3 Performance management

Annual (or bi-annual) reviews; clear KPIs at cycle start; underperformers on documented PIP.

## 3.4 Training & development

Quarterly needs assessment with dept heads; internal/external programs; participation records kept.

## 3.5 Leave & attendance

Official system/form for leave. Categories: annual, sick, maternity/paternity, emergency, unpaid. Monitor excessive absenteeism/tardiness.

## 3.6 Compensation & benefits

Monthly payroll; increments/bonuses from performance + budget; HR administers insurance, allowances, retirement plans.

## 3.7 Employee relations & grievances

Open-door; grievances in register; investigate within **5 working days**; confidentiality required.

## 3.8 Disciplinary procedures (progressive)

1. Verbal warning → 2. Written → 3. Final written → 4. Suspension/termination  
Severe cases (harassment, theft, violence) may go straight to termination.

## 3.9 Termination & resignation

Notice period (e.g. 30 days); exit interview; return company property; final pay incl. accrued leave within stated working days after last day.

## 3.10 HR records & confidentiality

Accurate secure personnel files; access restricted to authorized HR; data protection compliance.
