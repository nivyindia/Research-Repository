# VA Live Orientation Call — Script & Structure

## Objective

- Set clear expectations
- Filter out unserious candidates
- Align candidates with company work culture

---

## Step 1: Introduction (2–3 mins)

- Welcome everyone
- Brief about company and work model
- Clarify: "This is a performance-driven system, not a casual job"

---

## Step 2: Work Structure (5 mins)

Explain clearly:
- 8–9 hours daily work
- Fixed working hours (no flexibility)
- 6 days per week
- Remote work but strict tracking

Say clearly:  
"If you are looking for flexible or part-time work, this is NOT for you"

---

## Step 3: Daily Targets & Work (5 mins)

- Daily task assignment
- Daily reporting mandatory
- KPI-based performance tracking

"Your performance will be measured daily, weekly & monthly"

---

## Step 4: Strict Rules (5 mins)

- No work = No pay
- No report = No work counted
- Missing work = warning → removal
- 2 days no activity = immediate removal

Say:  
"We don’t chase people. Either you work or you are out"

---

## Step 5: No-Excuse Culture (5 mins)

No excuses like:
- "Network issue"
- "Busy today"
- "Will do later"

Say:  
"This system rewards execution, not excuses"

---

## Step 6: Commitment & Discipline (5 mins)

- 6-month minimum commitment
- $50 penalty if leaving early
- Attendance mandatory

Say:  
"If you are not sure, it’s better to leave now than waste time later"

---

## Step 7: Reality Check (Important)

Say clearly:
- "This is not easy"
- "You will be monitored"
- "Only consistent people survive here"

---

## Step 8: Drop-Off Filter (Most Important)

Ask directly:

"If anyone is not comfortable with:
- Fixed hours
- Daily work
- Strict discipline

You can leave now. No problem."

Pause for 30–60 seconds.  
This removes weak candidates.

---

## Step 9: Next Steps

- Inform about next stage / selection
- Explain selection will be based on actual work

Say:  
"Only those who perform will move forward"

---

## Final Line (Strong Close)

"We are building a serious team. If you are ready to work, you will grow fast. If not, this is not the right place for you."

---

## Note for Facilitator

- Be strict, not emotional
- Don’t convince candidates
- Let them disqualify themselves
- Goal = Filter, not impress
