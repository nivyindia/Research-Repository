## Section C — Retention & increment

### Performance-based increment bands

| Rating | Increment |
|--------|-----------|
| Outstanding | 12–20% |
| Exceeds expectations | 8–12% |
| Meets expectations | 5–8% |
| Needs improvement | 0–3% |
| Promotion / critical role | 10–25% |

- Eligibility: typically 12 months; PIP/probation excluded  
- Market/COLA: ~3–6%  
- Cap: generally ≤20% unless promotion  
- Cycle: annual (e.g. April/July); written increment letters  
- Special: retention bonuses, off-cycle market corrections  

### Retention levers

Compensation review, career paths, recognition, hybrid/flexibility, engagement events, leadership coaching, HiPo plans, DEI/safety, exit interviews + attrition reviews.

## Section D — Salary & promotion policy

- **Scope:** full-time employees  
- **Increment drivers:** performance, skills, market, company results, tenure, role expansion  
- **Promotion criteria:** consistent high ratings (e.g. 2 cycles), leadership, skills, contribution, readiness  
- **Process:** manager recommendation → HR/leadership review → decision  
- **Communication:** all outcomes in writing + feedback  
- **Review:** annual policy review by HR + management

## Section E — Awards & perks

**Awards:** EOM, sales/quality excellence, team player, innovation, integrity, long service, rising star, spot/mentor awards, fun cultural awards, monetary recognition.

**Perks:** bonuses/ESOPs/referrals/insurance; flexible hours/remote/PTO/wellness; L&D/mentorship/mobility; meals/gym/transport; outings/celebrations; modern (4-day week, subscriptions, CSR leave) as applicable.
