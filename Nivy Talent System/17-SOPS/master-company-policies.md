## 1. Introduction

Comprehensive guide for rules/procedures across departments. Applies to employees, contractors, management. Ownership: HR & Admin for updates/distribution. Key terms: SLA, KPI, procurement, compliance.

## 2. General company policies

- Code of conduct — professionalism, integrity, honesty  
- Confidentiality & data protection  
- Equal opportunity — no discrimination (gender, race, religion, disability)  
- Anti-harassment — zero tolerance  
- Workplace safety  
- IT & internet — work purpose primary; personal use minimal  

## 3. Departmental summaries

### Customer support
Service standards; SLAs (email 24h, calls 2h, complaints ack 12h); complaint log → investigate → resolve → follow-up; escalate if needed. Templates: call log, complaint resolution.

### Human resources
Merit hiring; attendance/leave; annual appraisal linked to promo/bonus; confidential grievances. Templates: leave request, performance review.

### Marketing
Brand guidelines; only authorized social posts; campaign approval before launch; research protocols. Templates: campaign brief, marketing report.

### Sales
Pipeline: prospect → qualify → proposal → negotiate → close. Leads in CRM within 24h. Discounts **>10%** need manager approval. Contracts legal-reviewed. Templates: visit report, quotation.

### Operations
SOPs, production targets, QC, inventory audits, quarterly preventive maintenance. Templates: production log, PM checklist.

### Procurement
Vendor selection (quality, cost, reliability, compliance); PR before buy; payment on verified invoices; annual supplier eval. Templates: PO, vendor evaluation.

### Admin & management
Facilities, asset register + annual audit, travel via approved vendors, meeting minutes. Templates: asset register, visitor log, minutes.
