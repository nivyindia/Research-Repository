# Stage 6 — Final Selection + Contract Signing

## Virtual Assistant Employment Agreement (Summary + Key Clauses)

**Parties:** Nivy (Company) and the Virtual Assistant (VA)

---

### 1. Role and Nature of Work
- Remote Virtual Assistant
- Duties: lead generation, outreach/messaging, data management, reporting, and other assigned tasks

### 2. Work Schedule and Availability
- 8–9 hours/day, 6 days/week
- Fixed working hours designated by Company
- Must be available and responsive during scheduled hours
- Unavailability without prior notice = absence

### 3. Training and Onboarding
- Structured training/onboarding may be provided
- Attendance mandatory
- Missing session without prior written approval may result in immediate termination

### 4. Probation Period
- Initial 3 months = probation
- Evaluation of performance, discipline, consistency
- Permanent continuation at Company’s sole discretion

### 5. Performance and KPIs
- Daily and weekly KPIs defined by Company
- Measured by task completion, output quality, timely reporting
- Minimum acceptable benchmark: **65%** (or higher as notified)

### 6. Reporting and Tracking
- Daily reporting mandatory (Company-approved tools)
- Monitoring via activity logs, screenshots, or output submissions
- No report = No work counted

### 7. Attendance and Leave
- Attendance mandatory during working hours
- Leaves pre-approved at least 24 hours in advance
- Emergency leave considered with valid reason
- Repeated absence/irregular attendance → termination

### 8. Compensation

**Training / Initial Phase (Performance-Based)**
| Month | Stipend |
|-------|---------|
| Month 1 | $140 |
| Month 2 | $200 |
| Month 3 | $240 |

If performance < 65%: sales-based payout $10–$35 per sale instead of fixed stipend.

**Post-Training / Job Phase**
- Base Salary: $280–$350 per month (performance/role dependent)
- Additional incentives possible

**Rules**
- Payment strictly contingent on task completion, quality, KPI achievement
- No work = No pay
- Payment cycle: monthly (within 5 days after cycle end)

### 9. Six-Month Commitment Clause
- Minimum 6-month commitment from joining date
- Early exit / breach: VA pays **$25 USD** as liquidated damages for training/onboarding costs

### 10. Quality Control
- Work must meet Company quality standards
- Low-quality or spam work = non-performance → withhold payment or terminate

### 11. Time Tracking
- Must remain active and responsive during working hours
- Idle/unexcused inactivity affects evaluation and compensation

### 12. Conflict of Interest
- No conflicting full-time work during assigned hours
- Violation → immediate termination

### 13. Discipline and Code of Conduct
Grounds for action:
- No work / incomplete tasks
- Failure to submit reports
- Irregular attendance
- Non-responsiveness during work hours

Three-step discipline:
1. First written warning
2. Final written warning
3. Termination

### 14. Payment Protection
Company may withhold/cancel payment for:
- Fake or incomplete work
- Misreporting / false data
- Spam or low-quality outreach
- Any Agreement/policy violation

### 15. Immediate Termination Conditions
- No work for 2 consecutive days
- Failure to respond during working hours
- Repeated failure to follow lawful instructions

### 16. Communication
- Active on official channels (WhatsApp, Slack, Teams) during working hours
- Delayed/no response impacts evaluation and may lead to termination

### 17. Confidentiality
- All Company data, client info, trade secrets, processes strictly confidential
- No disclosure during or after engagement
- Breach entitles Company to injunctive relief and other remedies

### 18. Equipment
VA responsible for:
- Laptop/desktop
- Stable internet
- Suitable remote work environment

### 19. Re-Application Policy
Removed candidates (poor performance/discipline) may not reapply for 3–6 months.

### 20. Growth and Promotion
High performers may receive increments, team leader roles, long-term opportunities at Company discretion.

### 21. General
- Governing law: local country laws
- Entire agreement clause
- Amendments only in writing signed by both parties
- Severability
