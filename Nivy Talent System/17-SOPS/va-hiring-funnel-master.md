# Nivy VA Hiring Funnel — Master (Candidate-First Model)

**Stages = Only candidate journey**  
**Everything else = Internal system embedded inside**

---

## Core Rules (System Level)

- Every stage has fixed deadline
- No late entries
- No reminders (hiring stages)
- Progression = invite-only (email controlled internally)

---

## Stage 0: Job Awareness

### Candidate Experience
- Sees job opportunity
- Understands: work hours, discipline, commitment
- Joins WhatsApp Group + Channel
- Decides to apply
- Must act within **48 hours**

### Internal (Hidden)
- Send JD email (batch of 50)
- Control entry via email
- Start deadline timer

---

## Stage 1: Application + Test

### Candidate Experience
- Fills application form
- Accepts terms
- Attempts test
- Deadline: **48 hours**

### Internal (Hidden)
- Auto evaluation (Google Form)
- Store data
- Send confirmation email
- Filter candidates

**Fail / No submission → PAID TRAINING PATH** (feedback + course offer + job path)

---

## Stage 2: Trial Task (Real Work)

### Candidate Experience
- Receives task
- Executes: Outreach, Lead generation, Reporting
- Submits work
- Deadline: **24 hours (strict)**

### Internal (Hidden)
- Send task email
- Evaluate submissions manually
- Decide progression

**Fail / No submission → PAID TRAINING PATH** (execution gap → upskill + re-entry)

---

## Stage 3: Interview

### Candidate Experience
- Receives interview opportunity
- Books slot
- Attends interview
- Rules: Book within 24 hrs | Attend within 48 hrs

### Internal (Hidden)
- Shortlist candidates
- Send interview links
- Evaluate performance

**Fail → PAID TRAINING PATH** (communication/thinking gap → upskill + re-entry)

---

## Stage 4: Orientation

### Candidate Experience
- Attends orientation session
- Understands: work structure, targets, rules

**Absent → removed → PAID TRAINING PATH (optional conversion)**

### Internal (Hidden)
- Send orientation details
- Final behavioral filtering

---

## Stage 5: Training & Probation (3 Months)

### Candidate Experience
- Works in real environment
- Completes tasks
- Follows SOPs
- Reports daily

### Internal (Hidden)
- Select limited candidates (top 10)
- Send offer + agreement
- Track: KPI, Discipline, Attendance

**Low performance → removed → PAID TRAINING PATH (upskill + re-entry option)**

---

## Stage 6: Final Evaluation → Job

### Candidate Experience
- Gets final outcome
  - High Performer → Full-time job
  - Low Performer → Exit OR PAID TRAINING PATH

### Internal (Hidden)
- Final evaluation
- Offer letter issuance

---

## Parallel Path: Paid Training Funnel (Hidden but Critical)

Candidate does not see this as a “stage”. It activates on failure at any point.

### Candidate Experience
- Receives feedback + improvement suggestion + training opportunity
- Can choose to join training or ignore

### Internal System
- Automated email sequence
- WhatsApp follow-ups
- Conversion system

---

## Clean Candidate View Flow

```
Job → Apply → Test → Task → Interview → Orientation → Training → Job
         ↓      ↓      ↓
       Fail → Training → Job Path
```

---

## Strategic Insight

- Candidate sees **journey**, not **system**
- Internal system controls everything silently
- Creates authority + seriousness
- Filters 80–90% low-quality candidates automatically

---

## Execution Note

- Hiring stages → No follow-ups
- Training funnel → Aggressive follow-ups

This balance ensures both **quality hiring + revenue generation**.

---

## Free Training Funnel (Internal Hiring Path)

Triggered ONLY after:
- Interview Passed
- Orientation Attended

Path: Stage 3 (Interview) → PASS → Stage 4 (Orientation) → ATTENDED → Internal Selection + Agreement → Enter Free Training (3 Months)

### Candidate Experience
- Joins company training system
- Works on real tasks (not theory)
- Daily task assignments, reporting, performance tracking, team environment, real client work

### Internal
- Select only Top 10
- Send offer + agreement
- Add to work groups
- Track daily: KPI, Discipline, Attendance, Task completion

### Outcome
- High performers → Permanent Job Offer (Salary + Incentives)
- Low performers → Removed / Redirect to Paid Training Path

---

## Paid Training Funnel (Revenue + Conversion)

Triggered at rejection points:
- Stage 1 (Form/Test) Fail or No Submission
- Stage 2 (Trial Task) Fail or No Submission
- Stage 3 (Interview) Fail
- Stage 5 (Free Training) Low Performance / Exit

### Email Flow (Auto)
Day 1 → Feedback  
Day 2 → Course details  
Day 3 → Proof / results  
Day 4 → Urgency  
Day 5 → Final call

### WhatsApp Flow
Follow-ups, Testimonials, Conversion chats

### Outcome
- Purchase → Joins Paid Training → Top performers can re-enter hiring / get job
- No Purchase → Nurture Pool (community + future job updates)

---

## Final Principle

**Main Hiring Flow** → Best candidates → Free Training → Job  
**Rejection at any point** → Paid Training → Improve → Re-enter / Job

Free Training = Company invests  
Paid Training = Candidate invests  
Both together = Hiring + Revenue + Talent Pipeline
