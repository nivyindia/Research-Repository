# Virtual Assistant Employment Agreement

**Parties:**  
**Company:** Nivy  
**Virtual Assistant (VA):** <<Full Name>>, R/o <<Address>>

This Agreement is made as of [Date].

---

## 1. Role and Nature of Work
1.1 VA works as Virtual Assistant in remote capacity.  
1.2 Duties include: lead generation, outreach and messaging, data management, reporting, and other tasks assigned by Company.

## 2. Work Schedule and Availability
2.1 Eight (8) to nine (9) hours per day, six (6) days per week during fixed working hours designated by Company.  
2.2 Must be available and responsive during scheduled hours. Unavailability without prior notice = absence.

## 3. Training and Onboarding
3.1 Company may provide structured training/onboarding.  
3.2 Attendance mandatory. Missing session without prior written approval may result in immediate termination.

## 4. Probation Period
4.1 Initial three (3) months = probation.  
4.2 Performance, discipline, and consistency evaluated.  
4.3 Permanent continuation at Company’s sole discretion.

## 5. Performance and KPIs
5.1 Meet daily and weekly KPIs defined by Company.  
5.2 Measured by task completion, output quality, timely reporting.  
5.3 Minimum acceptable benchmark: **65%** (or higher as notified in writing).

## 6. Reporting and Tracking
6.1 Daily reporting mandatory (Company-approved tools: Google Sheets, Forms, CRM).  
6.2 Monitoring via activity logs, screenshots, or output submissions.  
6.3 No report = No work counted for compensation or evaluation.

## 7. Attendance and Leave Policy
7.1 Attendance mandatory during working hours.  
7.2 Leaves pre-approved at least 24 hours in advance.  
7.3 Emergency leave may be considered with valid reason.  
7.4 Repeated absence or irregular attendance may lead to termination.

## 8. Compensation and Payment Terms

### 8.1 Training / Initial Phase (Performance-Based)
Stipend paid only if minimum 65% performance score achieved.

| Month | Stipend |
|-------|---------|
| Month 1 | $140 |
| Month 2 | $200 |
| Month 3 | $240 |

If performance < 65%: sales-based payout of $10 to $35 per sale instead of fixed stipend.

### 8.2 Post-Training / Job Phase
Upon successful probation completion:  
**Base Salary:** $280 to $350 per month (performance and role dependent).  
Additional incentives may apply based on output/results.

### 8.3 Performance Rules
Payment strictly contingent on task completion, output quality, and KPI achievement.  
No work = No pay. No report = No work counted.

### 8.4 Payment Cycle
Monthly. Processed within five (5) days after end of each cycle. Methods and exact dates communicated separately.

## 9. Six-Month Commitment Clause
9.1 Minimum six (6) month commitment from joining date.  
9.2 Early Exit / Breach: If VA resigns, stops working without notice, or fails to continue before completing six months, VA pays Company **$25 USD** as liquidated damages for training and onboarding costs (not as a penalty).

## 10. Quality Control
10.1 Work must meet Company quality standards.  
10.2 Low-quality or spam work may be treated as non-performance → withhold payment or terminate.

## 11. Time Tracking and Availability
11.1 Must remain active and responsive during all working hours.  
11.2 Idle time or unexcused inactivity may negatively affect evaluation and compensation.

## 12. Conflict of Interest
12.1 No conflicting full-time employment or other work during assigned working hours.  
12.2 Violation may result in immediate termination.

## 13. Discipline and Code of Conduct
13.1 Professional behaviour and communication at all times.  
13.2 Grounds for disciplinary action: (a) no work/incomplete tasks; (b) failure to submit reports; (c) irregular attendance; (d) non-responsiveness during work hours.  
13.3 Three-step structure: (1) First written warning; (2) Final written warning; (3) Termination.

## 14. Payment Protection Clause
Company may withhold or cancel payment for: fake/incomplete work; misreporting/false data; spam or low-quality outreach; any Agreement or policy violation.

## 15. Immediate Termination Conditions
Company may terminate immediately without prior notice if VA:  
- Performs no work for two (2) consecutive days;  
- Fails to respond to Company communications during working hours;  
- Repeatedly fails to follow lawful instructions.
