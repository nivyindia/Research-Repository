# Stage 1 — Job Details: Nivy Global Virtual Assistant (VA) Program

## Hiring Post (Part-Time Appointment Setter / SDR / BDM / Virtual Assistant)

**Location:** Remote | Philippines, India, South Africa & Mexico  
**Hours:** Flexible (2–3 Hours/Day)  
**Compensation:** 100% Commission-Based | Earn $70–$300 per closed deal

At Nivy we help businesses across the USA, UK, and Europe grow through Digital Marketing, Web Development, SaaS, AI, Lead Generation, and Business Growth solutions.

We are looking for highly motivated Appointment Setters / SDRs (1–2 years experience) who can generate conversations and book qualified meetings through LinkedIn and Email outreach.

### What You’ll Be Doing
- Prospect and identify decision-makers (CEOs, Founders, Directors)
- Conduct LinkedIn outreach and cold email campaigns
- Follow up with prospects using proven sequences
- Qualify leads and book discovery calls for the sales team
- Handle basic objections via text
- Maintain CRM and outreach tracking sheets

**No cold calling required.** Focus is LinkedIn messaging, email outreach, and appointment setting.

### What We’re Looking For
- 1–2+ years in B2B Sales, SDR, Appointment Setting, Lead Generation, Business Development, or International BPO
- Strong written English
- Experience with LinkedIn prospecting and outreach
- Understanding of LinkedIn account setup, warming, limits, and avoiding restrictions
- Experience with cold email and follow-up sequences
- Familiarity with Google Sheets, CRM tools, scheduling platforms
- Self-driven, resilient, results-oriented

### Bonus Experience
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- Apollo, Instantly, Smartlead, Lemlist or similar
- Lead scraping and list building
- Agency / SaaS / AI / Marketing service sales
- Multi-channel outreach campaigns

### Commission Structure

**Tier 1 (1–2 Sales):** 10%  
- $50 on $500 deals | $100 on $1,000 | $200 on $2,000

**Tier 2 (3–5 Sales):** 12%  
- $60 on $500 | $120 on $1,000 | $240 on $2,000

**Tier 3 (6+ Sales):** 15%  
- $75 on $500 | $150 on $1,000 | $300 on $2,000

Top performers can earn $1,800+ per month part-time.

### Attention to Detail Test
Include the keyword **"ROCKSTAR"** in your application message. Applications without it will not be considered.
