# City Sourcing System

**Created:** 2026-09-27  
**Context:** HQ Lucknow; remote-first roles; expansion narrative includes Dubai / Manila (company overview) — India city focus first.

---

## 1. City tiers

| Tier | Cities (example) | Tactic |
|------|------------------|--------|
| **T1 Primary** | Lucknow | Campus + local FB/WA groups + walk-in process if any |
| **T2 Near** | Kanpur, Prayagraj, Varanasi, Gorakhpur | Institute partners + targeted job portal boost |
| **T3 Remote talent** | Any India (metro + tier-2) | Pure remote ads; timezone IST meetings |
| **T4 Future** | International (policy later) | Legal/compliance gate before sourcing |

Adjust list in ops sheet; keep **T1+T2** capacity realistic for mentors.

---

## 2. Channel mix by city

| Channel | T1 | T2 | T3 |
|---------|----|----|-----|
| Institute MoU | High | High | Selective |
| LinkedIn / Naukri / Internshala | Med | High | High |
| Facebook / Instagram job creatives | High | Med | Med |
| Employee referral | High | Med | Med |
| WhatsApp community posts | High | Med | Low |

Source field on application must capture city + channel for analytics.

---

## 3. Localisation rules

- **Language:** JD in English; WA may use Hindi for T1/T2 where it improves completion  
- **Salary messaging:** always band + remote; no city-specific offline promise unless policy says so  
- **Meetings:** IST; camera-on policy unchanged  
- **Eligibility:** same national rules (marks, backlog, gap) — no city waiver without CHRO  

---

## 4. Weekly sourcing board (MVP Sheet)

Columns: `week | city_tier | city | channel | posts_count | applications | form_complete | screen_pass | owner`

Review every Monday with HR: kill low-ROI channel-city pairs.

---

## 5. Capacity guardrail

Do not open 10 cities if mentor bandwidth only supports 2 Academy batches.  
**Rule:** new city only if (a) one owner assigned and (b) batch seat plan exists.
