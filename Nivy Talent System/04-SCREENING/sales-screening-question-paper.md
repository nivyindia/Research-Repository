## Typical Selection Funnel for a BDE / Sales Profile

### Round 1: Application & Screening Filter

- **Purpose:** Filter non-negotiable criteria quickly.
- **Format:** Online application form.
- **Assessed:** Eligibility (degree/marks/backlogs/gaps), form completeness, laptop + stable internet for remote role.

### Round 2: Aptitude & Psychometric Assessment

- **Purpose:** Cognitive ability and sales-predictive traits.
- **Format:** Online timed test (60–90 minutes).
- **Assessed:** Numerical reasoning, verbal reasoning, logical reasoning, sales aptitude / SJTs, personality & drive (resilience, competitiveness, rejection tolerance).

### Round 3: Initial Screening Call (15–20 min)

- **Purpose:** Live communication, energy, motivation, culture fit.
- **Assessed:** Clarity/confidence of speech, energy, "Why sales / Why Nivy?", basic sell-me-this-pen or 60-second pitch.

### Round 4: Role-Play Assignment (key round)

- **Purpose:** Separate theorists from doers.
- **Format:** Video with Sales Manager; brief given beforehand (e.g. sell Nivy product to mid-size IT company).
- **Tasks:** Cold-call role-play (5–7 min) with objections; optional follow-up email (15 min).
- **Assessed:** Resilience, thinking on feet, process (rapport → qualify → value → close), email professionalism.

### Round 5: Final Interview with Sales Leadership

- **Purpose:** Long-term potential and ambition.
- **Assessed:** Career goals, mindset, realism about rejection/targets, quality of candidate questions.

### Filter for the 3 C's

1. **Competence** — brainpower and communication to do the job.  
2. **Coachability** — humility and trainability.  
3. **Character** — resilience, drive, integrity.

## Sample Question Themes (from source)

### Numerical (examples)

- Base + commission total income calculation
- Cost:Selling price ratio → profit %
- Successive % increase/decrease net change
- Team rate-to-target remaining daily units
- Pie-chart angle → regional sales value
- Weekly product sales totals and max difference week
- Discounted price; quarterly growth target units
- Lead conversion funnel: prospects → leads → sales %

### Verbal / logical (examples)

- Subject-verb agreement with collective nouns
- Error spotting; synonym of "persuasive" in sales = convincing
- Logical sales-call sequence: rapport → needs → solution → objections → close
- Opposite of "lucrative"; fill-in for "concise, _____, well-received"
- Next step after first meeting; "closing a sale" definition

### Sales scenarios (examples of preferred responses)

- "Send me information" → tailor what they need + schedule follow-up discussion
- Prospecting goal = pipeline of potential customers
- "I need to think it over" → uncover real concern with a clarifying question
- Prepare by researching company/industry/needs
- Shrinking client → adjust package supportively rather than abandon
- Conference follow-up within 24–48h, personalized
- Discuss price after value and needs are established
- Non-responsive lead → try another channel or new value, not spam/anger
- Late stakeholder → concise recap
- Consultative selling = understand problems and solve as advisor

### Objection handling (preferred patterns)

- "Price too high" → understand comparison + show long-term value
- "Happy with current supplier" → learn what they value; position as complement
- "Need to run by boss" → timeline + offer one-page summary
- "Not a good time" → ask when is better
- "Never heard of you" → brief benefit-focused intro
- Competitor cheaper → unique value/quality/support, not badmouthing
- Contract concerns → ask which clauses; show flexibility where possible
- Prior failed similar product → diagnose past challenges; differentiate approach
- Effective handling = listen, acknowledge, reasoned response
