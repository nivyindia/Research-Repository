# Candidate Communication Library (Email + WhatsApp)

**Created:** 2026-09-27
**Tone:** Clear, respectful, deadline-explicit. No fake guarantees.
**Also see:** `18-TEMPLATES/communication-screening-outcomes.md`

---

## A. Application received

**WA:** Hi {{name}}, we received your application for {{role}} at Nivy. Next step: complete this form by {{deadline}} -> {{link}}. Without it we cannot process your application.

**Email subject:** Application received — next step required

---

## B. Reminder (non-respondent)

**WA:** Reminder {{name}}: form/quiz for Nivy {{role}} closes **tonight**. Link: {{link}}

**Call script:** Hi {{name}}, this is {{hr}} from Nivy following up on your application. Please complete the mandatory form today to stay in process. Thank you.

---

## C. Role-fit pass — path choice

**Email subject:** Congratulations — choose your path at Nivy

Body: You scored {{score}}/20. Choose **one** by {{deadline}}:
**Path A — Direct job:** Quiz 2 -> {{link}} (FT band {{ft_band}})
**Path B — Academy:** Diagnostic -> {{link}} (stipend path, job on successful completion)

**WA:** Same two links, one-line each, deadline bold.

---

## D. Screen / practical pass

**WA:** You cleared the {{stage}} for {{role}}. Next: {{next_step}} by {{deadline}} -> {{link}}

---

## E. Interview invite

**Email subject:** Nivy {{role}} — interview {{date}}

Join {{meet_link}} at {{time}} IST. Camera on. Interviewer: {{interviewer}}. Join 5 min early.

**WA:** short version of same.

---

## F. Offer sent

**WA:** Offer letter sent to {{email}} for {{role}}. Please reply **ACCEPT** or sign by {{deadline}}. Questions? Message here.

---

## G. Internship / Academy welcome

**WA:** Welcome to Nivy {{track}} Academy. Offer is in your email. **Mandatory orientation:** {{date}} {{time}} {{meet}}. Attendance required for group access and batch start.

---

## H. Not selected / fail

**Email:** Thank you for your time. We will not move forward now. You may reapply after {{cooldown}}. We wish you success. — Nivy HR

**WA:** shorter; no score debate on chat.

---

## I. Documents request

**WA:** To proceed, upload within {{N}} days: ID, address, education marksheets, photo, bank proof -> {{link}}

---

## J. Pool nurture

**WA:** Hi {{name}}, we have openings for {{role}}. Still interested? Reply YES and your available start window.

**Email subject:** Nivy openings — {{role}}

---

## K. Probation review invite

**WA:** {{name}}, your Day-{{30|60|90}} review is {{date}} {{time}}. Please keep report folder ready.

---

## L. Exit / notice ack

**Email:** We acknowledge your resignation dated {{date}}. Last working day {{lwd}} per notice policy. Exit interview: {{slot}}. Return assets/access by {{lwd}}.

---

## Channel rules

| Channel | Use for |
|---------|---------|
| WhatsApp | Deadlines, reminders, short status |
| Email | Offers, formal fail, document lists, interviews |
| Call | Non-respondents, sensitive cases |
| Meet | Orientation, interviews, reviews |

**Never** paste full salary negotiation history in group chats.
**Never** share other candidates data.
