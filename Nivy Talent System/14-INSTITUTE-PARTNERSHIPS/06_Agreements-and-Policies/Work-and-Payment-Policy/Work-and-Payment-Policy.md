# Work and Payment Policy (Full Draft)

## Boundary
**Training / practice work** is for learning and assessment.  
**Client production work** is paid (or separately contracted) and is not unpaid “training.”

## Training work
- Assigned by trainers  
- May use simulated or anonymized data  
- Graded on rubrics  
- No client billing obligation on student  

## Paid work eligibility
- Competency threshold met  
- Written offer / terms  
- Confidentiality accepted  
- Tools and hours agreed  

## Payment
- Rates and schedule in the specific work order  
- Comply with applicable law (minimum wages, etc. where relevant)  
- Institute vs Nivy pay flow as per commercial schedule  

## Quality & conduct
Deadlines, rework rules, data security, professional communication.

## Stop work
Breach of confidentiality or quality may end assignment per order terms.
