# Services and Capabilities — Institute Partnership Context

**Document type:** Professional full draft  
**Status:** Updated with mutual growth options

---

## 1. Overview

Nivy provides a structured industry layer on top of an institute’s existing infrastructure. Core capabilities are activated first through a limited pilot. Additional growth services (co-branding, digital visibility, etc.) are offered only after results are visible.

---

## 2. Core Capability Areas (Pilot Stage)

### 2.1 Curriculum & Practical Design
- Industry-aligned learning outcomes
- Repeated practical workflows
- Portfolio-oriented task design

### 2.2 Delivery Support
- Flexible delivery models (Nivy-supported / hybrid / institute-led with quality checks)

### 2.3 Assessment Beyond Marks
- Process competence checks
- Portfolio review

### 2.4 Career Preparation
- Interview readiness
- Portfolio packaging
- Opportunity matching only where real demand exists

### 2.5 Tracking & Visibility
- Monthly progress view for the institute
- End-of-pilot outcomes summary

---

## 3. Priority Verticals

| Vertical | Focus |
|----------|--------|
| Accounting & Taxation Operations | Practical workflows, documentation, process discipline |
| Digital Marketing & Growth | Campaign logic, content systems, basic analytics |

---

## 4. Later-Stage Mutual Growth Options (After Pilot Results)

These are **optional** and discussed only after the institute has seen real pilot outcomes:

| Option | Benefit to Institute | Benefit to Nivy |
|--------|----------------------|-----------------|
| **Co-branding** | Shared credibility, stronger local positioning | Stronger ecosystem presence |
| **Digital Visibility Support** | More online reach, better student inquiries | New service revenue |
| **Association Effect** | Inbound students due to improved outcomes + association | Stronger partner network |
| **Paid Branding / Marketing Services** | Professional online presence for the institute | Direct revenue line |

**Principle:** Performance first. Commercial expansion later.

---

## 5. What Remains With the Institute

- Brand ownership
- Existing course fees
- Centre operations
- Final decision on pilot students and future options

---

## 6. What Nivy Does Not Do in Early Stage

- Force co-branding
- Force paid marketing services
- Guarantee student inflow numbers
- Take over institute brand or fees

---

## 7. Activation Sequence

1. Pilot discussion & written scope
2. Delivery + monthly visibility
3. Results review
4. Only then: optional co-branding / visibility / deeper association discussion
