# Services and Capabilities — Institute Partnership Context

**Document type:** Professional full draft  
**Purpose:** Clear statement of what Nivy brings to an institute partner  
**Status:** Full draft — ready for review

---

## 1. Overview

Within the Institute Growth & Industry Partnership Program, Nivy provides a structured set of capabilities that sit on top of an institute’s existing infrastructure. These capabilities are modular: a pilot may activate only a subset, with additional modules added later by mutual written agreement.

---

## 2. Core Capability Areas

### 2.1 Curriculum & Practical Design

- Industry-aligned learning outcomes for priority verticals (Accounting & Taxation operations, Digital Marketing)  
- Breakdown of theory into repeated, assessable practical workflows  
- Task design that produces portfolio evidence, not only exam scores  
- Version control of materials so quality does not drift across batches  

### 2.2 Delivery Support

- Delivery model options: Nivy-led, institute-faculty-led with Nivy materials and quality checks, or hybrid  
- Trainer briefing notes and session structures  
- Quality observation points during the pilot  

### 2.3 Assessment Beyond Marks

- Process competence checks  
- Portfolio review criteria  
- Mid-pilot and end-pilot assessment points  
- Clear distinction between “completed hours” and “demonstrated readiness”  

### 2.4 Career Preparation

- Interview preparation modules  
- Portfolio packaging guidance  
- Communication and workplace-behaviour basics  
- Matching support **only** where verified opportunities exist  

### 2.5 Tracking & Visibility

- Simple monthly progress view for the institute coordinator  
- Cohort-level metrics (attendance, assessment completion, portfolio status)  
- End-of-pilot outcomes summary  

---

## 3. Priority Verticals (Current)

| Vertical | Focus | Typical Practical Outputs |
|----------|--------|---------------------------|
| Accounting & Taxation Operations | Book-keeping workflows, GST basics in practice, reconciliation, documentation discipline | Working files, reconciliation statements, process checklists, portfolio samples |
| Digital Marketing & Growth | Campaign setup logic, content systems, basic analytics reading, tool workflows | Campaign plans, content calendars, basic performance read-outs, portfolio pieces |

Additional verticals (IT / Software / AI-assisted workflows, broader career-readiness) are available for later phases after the operating model is proven.

---

## 4. What Remains With the Institute

- Ownership of premises, brand and existing course portfolio  
- Fee collection for the institute’s own courses  
- Day-to-day centre administration  
- Final decision on which students enter a pilot cohort (within agreed criteria)  

---

## 5. What Nivy Does Not Provide Under This Program

- Franchise rights or exclusive territory claims at pilot stage  
- Guaranteed employment or placement numbers  
- Takeover of institute management or branding  
- Open-ended free training outside the written pilot annex  

---

## 6. How Capabilities Are Activated

1. Joint discussion of institute capacity and student profile  
2. Selection of one or two verticals for the pilot  
3. Written pilot annex (scope, numbers, included services, review points)  
4. Delivery and monthly visibility  
5. End-of-pilot review and decision on next phase  

No capability is assumed to be “included forever.” Everything is scoped.

---

## 7. Quality & Consistency Principle

The same practical standards and assessment logic should be recognisable whether the pilot runs in one city or several. Local adaptation of language and examples is encouraged; dilution of core practical requirements is not.
