## 1. Who We Are

Nivy Global works at the intersection of industry service delivery and structured talent development.

Our core domains include:

- Accounting, taxation and finance operations  
- Digital marketing and growth systems  
- Software, web and AI-assisted workflows  

We serve clients on the industry side and, in parallel, design training and readiness systems that help people move from classroom knowledge to practical competence.

---

## 2. Why We Work With Institutes

Thousands of computer institutes and coaching centres across India already possess three critical assets:

1. Physical classrooms and computer labs  
2. Local brand trust and student flow  
3. Administrative capacity to run batches  

What is often missing is a consistent industry-grade practical layer: repeated real workflows, rigorous assessment beyond marks, portfolio evidence, and a disciplined approach to career readiness.

Nivy does not ask institutes to abandon their identity or rebuild infrastructure. We add the missing operating layer so that the infrastructure they already own produces stronger student outcomes.

---

## 3. What We Are Not

To avoid confusion, we state clearly what this partnership is not:

- We are not a franchise seller  
- We are not a pure placement agency that appears only at the end of a course  
- We do not take over an institute’s brand or existing fee collection  
- We do not promise or market “100% placement”  

---

## 4. The Partnership Model in One Sentence

**Your infrastructure + our industry systems + a limited pilot that lets results speak before any long-term commitment.**

---

## 5. How We Engage

Engagement always begins with a written, time-bound pilot. Scope, student numbers, included elements and review points are frozen in advance. Only after both sides have seen actual delivery and outcomes do we discuss longer cooperation.

This approach protects the institute and keeps Nivy accountable to measurable reality rather than brochure claims.

---

## 6. Leadership & Delivery Capability

The founder brings approximately 15 years of industry experience across relevant domains. Delivery draws on experienced practitioners (including CA-qualified professionals for accounting tracks and domain specialists for marketing and technology tracks) according to the programs being run.

Exact trainer and delivery configuration for any given pilot is agreed in writing before start.
