# Elevator Pitch (Full Draft)

**Duration:** 30–60 seconds  
**Tone:** Respectful, non-salesy

---

Hello sir, I am [Your Name], speaking from Nivy Global.

We work in industry domains such as accounting, digital marketing, software and AI. We want to partner with computer institutes where students can get practical, industry-level training and, where possible, be prepared for real opportunities.

You already have classrooms, computers and students. We can help with curriculum, a practical training system, assessment and career preparation.

We can start with a six-month pilot in a limited written scope—no training fee on that scope; later you decide whether to continue based on results.

If you can spare 15–20 minutes, we can discuss a short plan suited to your institute.

---

## Do not say in elevator
- Job guarantee  
- Full fee-share percentages  
- Long course catalogue
