# Frequently Asked Questions (Full Draft)

### 1. What do you actually want to do?
**Short:** Add industry-level practical training and career prep on your existing institute—starting with a limited pilot.

### 2. What does your company gain?
**Short:** A pipeline of practically skilled candidates; better fit where there is need.

### 3. What does our institute gain?
**Short:** New practical programs, better student value, possible future revenue—without major new infrastructure investment.

### 4. Do we need to invest money / new capital?
**Short:** No major new infrastructure. Existing rooms/PCs. Some operating costs will be clarified upfront.

### 5. Why six months of free training?
**Short:** So both sides can see results without an immediate long-term lock-in. Free = only the **written scope**.

### 6. What happens after six months? Is paid hidden?
**Short:** Not hidden. After the pilot, paid option only by mutual agreement.

### 7. Is there a job guarantee for students?
**Short:** No. Skills + available verified opportunities. Guarantee only when a vacancy is confirmed.

### 8. Do you also hire into your company?
**Short:** When there is real need, eligible trained candidates may be considered.

### 9. How many real vacancies / projects now?
**Short:** We will share only the verified / tentative list—no overstatement.

### Policy anchors
- Free pilot = written scope only  
- Paid = new mutual agreement  
- No false job guarantee  
- Training ≠ unpaid client production
