# Objection Handling (Full Draft)

### 1. “Placement people already keep coming”
I understand, sir. The difference is we do not come only with drives or promises. We talk about a system with practical training, assessment, portfolio and career preparation.

### 2. “Why free? Will you trap us later?”
It is free so results can show in a limited scope first. The paid model is not hidden—we said from the start it will be decided by new agreement after the pilot.

### 3. “Give a job guarantee”
I cannot guarantee a job when no vacancy is confirmed. I can offer skills, assessment, interview prep, and fair consideration for available opportunities.

### 4. “Our courses are enough”
Absolutely—your base matters. We are not replacing it. We offer adding a practical layer and a career layer on top.

### Closing line
Your concern is valid. That is why we proceed in writing, from a limited scope, and by measuring—so trust remains.
