# Full Pitch (Full Draft)

**Duration:** 5–7 minutes

## 1. Opening
Hello sir, I am [Name] from Nivy Global. Thank you for your time.

We work in accounting, tax, digital marketing, website/software and AI tools at an industry level. We are speaking with institutes so their students can get not only course certificates, but also practical work understanding and career preparation.

With your permission, I will explain the model in 5–7 minutes; you may ask questions anytime.

## 2. Your strength
Your institute already has a foundation—classrooms, computers, local student reach, and teaching experience. This model does not require opening a new centre.

## 3. Gap (without attack)
Students often complete many courses, yet still lack real company workflows and interview readiness. Building a full senior team in every new domain inside the institute can be costly and hard.

## 4. What we add
- Industry-oriented curriculum and practical assignments  
- Approved trainers / experienced professionals  
- Skill assessment and portfolio  
- Resume, interview prep, career support  
- Where verified vacancies or projects exist, consideration for eligible students  

## 5. Programs
1. Accounting, GST, practical finance ops  
2. Digital marketing and growth  
3. Later: software / AI (when capacity allows)  
4. Career readiness with every program  

## 6. Split of work
| Institute | Nivy |
|-----------|------|
| Infra, local coordination | Curriculum, training system |
| Attendance support | Assessment, career prep |
| Coordinator | Monthly review, verified outreach |

## 7. Six-month pilot
Start with a limited, **written** scope. No training fee on that agreed scope. Who bears power, internet, local staff time will be written upfront. Monthly progress review; after six months paid partnership, change, or stop—only by **new agreement**.

## 8. Hiring — straight talk
We will help with skills, assessment, interview prep and access to **available** opportunities. We will **not guarantee a job to every student** when there is no confirmed vacancy.

## 9. Close
Next step: understand your courses and capacity → short written pilot proposal → limited pilot if both agree.
