# Cost and Revenue Models

Keep three lines **separate:** Pilot · Paid training · Outsourcing.

**Pilot:** Classroom/PC use typically institute · Power/internet institute · Core delivery Nivy (within free scope) · Travel/software TBD. Free ≠ every cost is zero.

**Paid:** Revenue share (after cost sheet) / Fixed delivery fee / Hybrid.

**Outsourcing:** Client billing · Worker pay · Institute supervision · Nivy margin — separate contract.
