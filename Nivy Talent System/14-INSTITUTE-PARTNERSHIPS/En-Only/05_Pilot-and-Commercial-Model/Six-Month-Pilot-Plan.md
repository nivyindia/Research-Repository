# Six-Month Pilot Plan

**Purpose:** Validate demand, quality, outcomes and economics before a long-term paid deal.

**Freeze in writing before start:** Programs, max students, hours, free scope, cost owners.

| Month | Focus |
|-------|--------|
| 1 | Readiness, batch select, baseline skills, kickoff |
| 2–3 | Core modules + weekly practice |
| 4–5 | Projects, competency tests, portfolio, career prep |
| 6 | Final assess, opportunity match (if real openings), joint review |

**After month 6:** Written decision within 15 days — Expand paid / Modify / Stop. Free terms do not auto-renew.
