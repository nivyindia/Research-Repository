# Master Plan — Institute Growth & Industry Partnership Program

**Status:** Control outline complete

## 1. Vision
Turn existing computer institutes / coaching centres into industry-connected training and career centres.
Use their existing resources instead of spending on major new infrastructure.

## 2. Positioning
**Primary name:** Institute Growth & Industry Partnership Program  
**Line:** Your Infrastructure. Our Industry Expertise. Shared Growth.  
**Risk-reduction offer:** Six-month limited pilot → measure → then paid decision.

## 3. Three parties
| Party | Brings |
|-------|--------|
| Institute | Rooms, PCs, local students, coordination |
| Nivy | Curriculum, trainers, practice, assessment, career support, verified opportunities |
| Student | Skills, portfolio, readiness, access to available opportunities |

## 4. Verticals (phased)
1. Accounting / tax / finance ops (priority)  
2. Digital marketing & growth (priority)  
3. IT / software / AI (later)  
4. Career readiness (with all)

## 5. Student path
Assess → Train → Practice → Evaluate → Portfolio → Interview prep → Opportunity match → Feedback → Follow-up

## 6. Commercial lines
- **Pilot:** free only within written scope  
- **Paid courses:** revenue share or delivery fee (after cost sheet)  
- **Outsourcing:** separate contract

## 7. Success
Repeatable, measurable, commercially sustainable system — local infrastructure + central quality and employer links.

## 8. Open decisions
- Brand: Nivy Global vs Nivy Academy  
- Pilot free-scope numbers  
- First 1–2 programs + real trainers  
- Real vacancy / project list
