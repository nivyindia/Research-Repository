# First Meeting Script (Full Draft)

Hello sir, how are you?

I am [Name], from Nivy Global. We work on the industry side in accounting, digital marketing, software and AI. The idea was to try a clear model of practical training and career preparation with institutes like yours that already have classrooms, computers and students.

If you give me two minutes, I will explain briefly.

Sir, students complete many courses, but often lack company-style practical work and interview preparation. You have the foundation. We can bring a system of curriculum, practical assignments, assessment and career support.

We are thinking of starting with a **limited six-month pilot**. Which programs and how many students—written and fixed first. No training fee on that agreed scope. We will review progress monthly. After six months you decide: paid partnership, change, or stop. No automatic lock-in.

Career support and available opportunities—**no guarantee for every student** without a vacancy.

Next step: understand your courses and capacity, then a one–two page pilot idea. When would 15–20 minutes be convenient this week?
