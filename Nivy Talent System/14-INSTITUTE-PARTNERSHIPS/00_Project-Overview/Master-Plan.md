# Master Plan — Institute Growth & Industry Partnership Program

**Program Name:** Institute Growth & Industry Partnership Program  
**Brand Positioning:** Your Infrastructure. Our Industry Expertise. Shared Growth.  
**Owner:** Nivy Global / Nivy Talent System  
**Status:** Planning → Documentation phase

---

## 1. Vision

Transform existing computer institutes and small/medium training centres into **Industry-Connected Training, Career & Outsourcing Centres** — without requiring them to build new infrastructure or hire a full senior industry team.

## 2. Core Model

| Party | Contribution |
|-------|-------------|
| **Partner Institute** | Classrooms, computers, local student acquisition, admissions, on-ground coordination |
| **Nivy Global** | Industry curriculum, experienced trainers, practical projects, assessments, career preparation, employer outreach, placement support, client work pipeline |
| **Students** | Industry-relevant skills, portfolio, interview readiness, access to paid opportunities where available |

## 3. Positioning (Primary)

**Institute Growth & Industry Partnership Program**

Supporting messages:
1. Expand Your Institute. Launch New Career Programs. Create New Revenue Opportunities.
2. Bring Real-World Industry Skills, Practical Training and Experienced Professionals to Your Students.
3. Strengthen Student Employability Through Practical Assessments, Interview Preparation and Placement Support.

Risk-reduction offer: **Start With a Six-Month Pilot. Evaluate the Results Before Committing Long-Term.**

## 4. Four Verticals (Phased Launch)

| Phase | Vertical | Priority |
|-------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Accounting, Taxation & Finance Operations | Highest (CA network ready) |
| 1 | Digital Marketing & Growth Operations | High |
| 2 | IT, Software Development & AI | Medium |
| All | Career Development & Job Readiness | Integrated with every vertical |

## 5. Student Journey (Standard System)

1. Assessment → Initial Skill Report  
2. Training → Training Progress Record  
3. Practical Work → Project Portfolio  
4. Evaluation → Competency Scorecard  
5. Career Preparation → Job-Ready Profile  
6. Opportunity Matching → Applications & Interview Pipeline  
7. Interview Feedback → Improvement Report  
8. Post-Placement Support → Follow-up Record  

## 6. Commercial Structure (Three Separate Lines)

| Line | Model |
|------|-------|
| Six-month pilot | Agreed free scope (documented limits) |
| Paid courses | Revenue share or fixed delivery fee (course-wise costing) |
| Outsourcing / workforce | Per-person, per-team or deliverable-based contract |

## 7. Pilot KPIs

- Enrolments & completion rate  
- Practical assessment pass rate  
- Portfolio completion  
- Interview-ready count  
- Interview opportunities given  
- Actual paid employment / projects  
- 30-day & 90-day retention  
- Revenue & cost per student  
- Institute & student satisfaction  

## 8. Critical Decisions Before Launch

1. Brand identity: Nivy Global vs Nivy Academy  
2. Pilot capacity: which 2–3 programs can start immediately  
3. Free pilot scope: institutes, students, hours, cost ownership  
4. Real hiring base: current vacancies vs future pipeline  
5. Future revenue rules: fee share, payment terms, client-project terms

A repeatable, measurable, commercially sustainable industry-training system that every partner institute can run — with local infrastructure and centralized curriculum, quality assurance and employer connections from Nivy.
