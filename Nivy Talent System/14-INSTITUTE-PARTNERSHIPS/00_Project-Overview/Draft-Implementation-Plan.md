## Quality rules for every draft

1. Lucknow-friendly Hindi where scripts; clear simple English OK in policies/tables.  
2. No job guarantee without verified vacancy.  
3. Free pilot always “written scope only”.  
4. Training ≠ unpaid client delivery.  
5. Mark `TBD` / `EXAMPLE` instead of fake numbers.  
6. One source of truth per topic — WA/email/PPT same claims.
