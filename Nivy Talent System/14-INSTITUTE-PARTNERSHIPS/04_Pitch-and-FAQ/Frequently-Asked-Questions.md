# Frequently Asked Questions — Institute Growth & Industry Partnership Program

**Purpose:** Ready answers for sales conversations, emails and meetings.  
**Rule:** Keep answers honest. Never invent commercial terms or placement guarantees.

Each question has:
- **Spoken short answer** (what you say in the room)
- **Detailed answer** (for email or deeper discussion)

---

### 1. What exactly do you want to do with our institute?

**Short:**  
We want to add structured industry practical training, assessment and career preparation on top of the infrastructure and students you already have — starting with a limited six-month pilot.

**Detailed:**  
We are not selling a franchise and we are not asking you to change your existing courses or brand. We bring curriculum design, practical task systems, assessment methods and career-preparation support, especially in Accounting & Taxation operations and Digital Marketing. Everything begins with a written pilot so both sides can measure results before any longer commitment.

---

### 2. Who is Nivy / Nivy Global?

**Short:**  
We work on the industry side — accounting, taxation, digital marketing, software and AI-assisted workflows — and we also build talent systems that connect training to real work readiness.

**Detailed:**  
Nivy Global operates at the intersection of industry service delivery and structured talent development. The Institute Partnership Program is one channel through which we help existing training centres raise the practical and career-readiness level of their students without forcing them to become something they are not.

---

### 3. Is the pilot free?

**Short:**  
Only the items written in the pilot annex are included at no charge. Everything outside that annex is either paid or out of scope.

**Detailed:**  
Before any batch starts we freeze a short written annex: which programs, maximum number of students, hours, mode of delivery, and exactly which elements are free or included. This protects both sides from later misunderstanding. We do not run open-ended “free forever” pilots.

---

### 4. Do you guarantee placements or jobs?

**Short:**  
No. We never guarantee jobs.

**Detailed:**  
Where we have verified demand from clients or internal projects, we introduce prepared candidates. Where we do not, we still equip students with portfolio, process competence and interview readiness. We will not allow “100% placement” language under this partnership. Metrics remain transparent and limited to what actually happens.

---

### 5. Will you take over our brand or our fee collection?

**Short:**  
No.

**Detailed:**  
Your institute name, existing course fees and day-to-day ownership stay with you. We operate as an added industry layer, not as a replacement management or franchise brand.

---

### 6. How many students do we need to start?

**Short:**  
We prefer a limited cohort. Exact number is frozen in the pilot annex.

**Detailed:**  
Starting small is intentional. It lets us protect quality, gather real feedback and avoid over-commitment on either side. Typical early cohorts are modest; the exact ceiling is written before the pilot begins.

---

### 7. Which courses / verticals do you cover first?

**Short:**  
Priority verticals are Accounting & Taxation operations and Digital Marketing. Others can come later.

**Detailed:**  
These two areas have clear practical workflows, measurable outputs and existing demand patterns. IT / Software / AI-assisted tracks and broader career-readiness modules can be added after the first pilot proves the operating model.

---

### 8. Who provides the trainers?

**Short:**  
Delivery model is agreed in the pilot annex — it can be Nivy-supported, institute-supported with our materials and quality checks, or a hybrid.

**Detailed:**  
We do not assume one single model for every partner. Some institutes have strong local faculty who only need better structure and practical task design. Others need more direct delivery support. This is decided case by case and written down before start.

---

### 9. What about student data and privacy?

**Short:**  
We use consent forms and a short privacy notice. We do not put personal student data into public repositories.

**Detailed:**  
Only data necessary for training, assessment and (where relevant) opportunity matching is collected. Students (or guardians where applicable) give written consent. Operational records stay in controlled systems, not in open GitHub or public folders.

---

### 10. What happens after six months?

**Short:**  
We review results together and decide: continue, expand, change terms, or close cleanly.

**Detailed:**  
The pilot has a defined end. There is no automatic renewal and no penalty for stopping. If both sides see value, we move to a new written agreement for the next phase. If not, we close with a short outcomes summary and part professionally.

---

### 11. How is money handled after the pilot?

**Short:**  
Three separate lines: pilot scope, paid training programs, and any outsourcing work. Numbers are frozen only after founder approval.

**Detailed:**  
We keep pilot economics, ongoing training revenue share (or delivery fees), and any project-outsourcing contracts completely separate. No commercial percentage is final until it is explicitly approved. We will not quote invented numbers in early conversations.

---

### 12. Can we run this in Hindi / local language?

**Short:**  
Yes. Materials and delivery can be Hindi, English or bilingual depending on the student batch and faculty comfort.

**Detailed:**  
We already maintain language packs (Hindi-only, English-only, and bilingual). Classroom delivery language is chosen for the specific cohort so students actually understand the practical work.

---

### 13. What do you need from us to prepare a pilot proposal?

**Short:**  
Basic capacity information, preferred vertical(s), and a decision-maker who can freeze the pilot annex.

**Detailed:**  
We need approximate classroom/PC availability, a sense of current student numbers in the relevant courses, one or two preferred verticals, and clarity on who will sign the pilot scope document. With that we can prepare a clear one-page pilot summary for your review.

---

**End of FAQ**
