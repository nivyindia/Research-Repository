# Frequently Asked Questions (Full Draft)

Har sawal: **Short (bolne ke liye)** · **Detailed**

---

### 1. Aap actually kya karna chahte ho?

**Short:** Aapke existing institute par industry-level practical training aur career preparation jodna—pehle limited pilot se.

**Detailed:** Hum franchise bechne nahi aaye. Aapke rooms/PCs/students ke saath hum curriculum, practical system, assessment aur career support laate hain. Job tab discuss hogi jab real opportunity ho.

---

### 2. Aapki company ko isse kya faida?

**Short:** Practical skills wale candidates ki pipeline; jahan need ho hiring/projects ke liye better fit.

**Detailed:** Nivy industry services chalati hai. Training quality se better talent milta hai. Institute ko replace nahi karte—partner banate hain.

---

### 3. Hamare institute ko kya faida?

**Short:** Naye practical programs, behtar student value, future mein extra revenue ki possibility—bina bade naye infra ke.

**Detailed:** Major naya centre nahi. Pilot se demand aur quality test. Successful hone par paid courses / baad mein capacity ke hisaab se aur models.

---

### 4. Kya humein koi paisa / naya investment lagana padega?

**Short:** Bada naya infrastructure nahi. Existing rooms/PCs use. Kuch operational costs (bijli, net, coordinator time) pehle se clear kiye jaayenge.

**Detailed:** Pilot ke agreed training scope par training fee nahi. Baaki costs likh kar decide. Koi chhupi capital demand nahi.

---

### 5. 6 mahine free training kyun?

**Short:** Dono sides results dekh sakein—demand, quality, operations—bina turant long-term lock ke.

**Detailed:** Free = sirf **likha hua scope**. Unlimited free service nahi. Baad mein paid alag agreement se.

---

### 6. 6 mahine baad kya hoga? Paid chhupa to nahi?

**Short:** Nahi chhupa. Shuru se kaha hai: pilot ke baad paid option mutual agreement se.

**Detailed:** Auto-convert free se paid mein nahi hoga. Nayi likhi sargeh. Agar nahi jodna, pilot ke baad exit possible.

---

### 7. Kya students ko naukri ki guarantee hai?

**Short:** Nahi. Skills + available verified opportunities. Guarantee tabhi jab confirmed vacancy ho.

**Detailed:** Hum taiyari aur matching support dete hain. Har enrollee ko job promise galat hoga.

---

### 8. Kya aap apni company mein bhi hire karte ho?

**Short:** Jab real need hoti hai, eligible trained candidates consider kar sakte hain.

**Detailed:** Pipeline banana alag; har student hire karna alag. Selection skills aur process par.

---

### 9. Abhi kitni real vacancies / projects hain?

**Short:** Jo verified / tentative list hai wahi share karenge—overstate nahi.

**Detailed:** List time ke saath badalti hai. Draft materials mein numbers `TBD` jab tak founder list freeze na kare.

---

### 10. Training kaun dega? Quality kaun check karega?

**Short:** Approved trainers / Nivy process; assessment rubrics; monthly review.

**Detailed:** Curriculum central; senior review jahan possible; institute attendance support.

---

### 11. Kya hamare purane courses band karne padenge?

**Short:** Nahi. Additional practical / career layer; replace mandatory nahi.

---

### 12. Kya hamare teachers ko bhi train kiya jayega?

**Short:** Jahan model allow kare, train-the-trainer baad mein; pehle delivery quality pe focus.

**Detailed:** Pilot mein primarily Nivy-approved delivery. Scale par local trainer enablement plan.

---

### 13. Student fee kaun collect karega?

**Short:** Pilot agreed scope: training fee nahi. Paid phase: agreement mein likha mode (institute collect + share, etc.).

---

### 14. Fee / revenue share kaise decide hoga?

**Short:** Cost sheet ke baad—share ya fixed delivery fee. Provisional ratios sirf example, final nahi.

---

### 15. Computer, internet, bijli, marketing kharch kaun?

**Short:** Pilot se pehle cost matrix mein likha jaayega. Assume mat karo sab free hai.

---

### 16. Kya student real client ka kaam karenge?

**Short:** Training assignments alag. Real client work sirf eligibility + written terms + payment policy ke saath.

---

### 17. Real kaam par payment kaise?

**Short:** Alag work-and-payment policy; legal compliance; rates pehle clear.

---

### 18. Student beech mein chhod de to?

**Short:** Attendance / completion rules pilot agreement mein; reporting honest.

---

### 19. Pilot successful na ho to?

**Short:** Joint review; modify ya stop. Forced long-term nahi.

---

### 20. Kya long-term agreement mein bandhna padega?

**Short:** Pilot limited term. Long-term sirf dono agree karein to.

---

### 21. Data safe kaise?

**Short:** Consent + privacy rules; employer share sirf relevant + consent-based.

---

### 22. Baad mein outsourcing saath mein?

**Short:** Possible agar capacity aur quality fit ho—**alag commercial contract**.

---

## Policy anchors (always)

- Free pilot = written scope only  
- Paid = new mutual agreement  
- No false job guarantee  
- Training ≠ unpaid client production
