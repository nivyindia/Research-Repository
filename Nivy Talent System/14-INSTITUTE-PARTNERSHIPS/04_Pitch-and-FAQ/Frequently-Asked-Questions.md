# Frequently Asked Questions — Institute Partnerships

**Q: What is the pilot duration?**
A: Standard pilot is 6 months.

**Q: Who pays for training?**
A: Model depends on commercial agreement (institute-sponsored / shared / student contribution).

**Q: Are opportunities guaranteed?**
A: We provide verified vacancies and structured matching; placement is outcome-dependent on student performance and market.

**Q: What data do you collect on students?**
A: Only necessary assessment and career data under consent and privacy policy.

**Q: Can we start with one department?**
A: Yes, preferred for pilot.
