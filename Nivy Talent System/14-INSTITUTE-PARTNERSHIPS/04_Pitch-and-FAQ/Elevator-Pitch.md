# Elevator Pitch — Outline (30–60 seconds)

## Structure

1. **Who** — Nivy Global; industry work in accounting, digital marketing, software/AI.  
2. **Why we are here** — Looking for institutes whose students can get practical industry training and, where suitable, real work opportunities.  
3. **What we propose** — Your classrooms + computers; our curriculum, trainers, assessments, career support.  
4. **How we start** — Six-month limited free pilot, measure results, then decide paid partnership.  
5. **Ask** — 15–20 minute meeting to understand your institute and share a short plan.  

## Hindi (Lucknow tone) draft direction

Namaste sir, main Nivy Global se…  
Hum accounting, digital marketing, software/AI mein industry kaam karte hain.  
Aapke paas classrooms, computers aur students hain — hum industry-level practical training aur career preparation laana chahte hain.  
Shuruaat mein limited scope ka 6-month pilot (agreed training free), baad mein results dekh kar aage.  
Kya 15 minute baith kar aapke institute ke hisaab se plan dekh sakte hain?

**Avoid:** Job guarantee, heavy English, pressure close.
