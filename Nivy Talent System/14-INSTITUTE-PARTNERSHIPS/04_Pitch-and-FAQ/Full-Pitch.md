# Full Pitch (Full Draft)

**Duration:** 5–7 minutes  
**Use:** In-person or detailed call after permission

---

## 1. Opening

Namaste sir, main [Naam], Nivy Global se. Dhanyavad time dene ke liye.

Hum accounting, taxation, digital marketing, website/software aur AI tools ke area mein industry ka kaam karte hain. Hum kuch institutes ke saath is liye baat kar rahe hain ki unke students ko sirf course certificate nahi, balki practical kaam ki samajh aur career preparation bhi mil sake.

Agar aap allow karein, main 5–7 minute mein model samjha deta hoon; beech mein aap sawal kar sakte hain.

---

## 2. Aapki taakat

Aapke institute ke paas pehle se foundation hai—classrooms, computers, local students tak pahunch, aur sikhane ka experience. Naya centre kholne ki zaroorat is model mein nahi hai. Hum usi foundation par industry-oriented layer jodne ki baat karte hain.

---

## 3. Soft gap (bina attack kiye)

Aaj kal students kai courses kar lete hain. Phir bhi kai baar unhe ye samajhne mein dikkat hoti hai ki company mein actual workflow kaise chalta hai, documentation kaise hoti hai, aur interview / job ke liye kya taiyari chahiye.

Har naye domain ke liye alag senior team aur updated practical system khada karna institute ke liye mehnga aur mushkil ho sakta hai. Yahi jagah hum milkar bhar sakte hain.

---

## 4. Hum kya jodte hain

- Industry-oriented curriculum aur practical assignments  
- Approved trainers / experienced professionals (program ke hisaab se)  
- Skill assessment aur portfolio  
- Resume, interview preparation, career support  
- Jahan **verified** vacancy ya project ho, wahan eligible students ko consider karna  

---

## 5. Programs (phased)

Pehle un areas se shuru jahan hum deliver kar sakte hain:

1. **Accounting, GST, practical finance operations**  
2. **Digital marketing aur growth operations**  
3. Baad mein: software / AI (jab trainer capacity clear ho)  
4. **Career readiness** har program ke saath  

---

## 6. Kaam ka batwara

| Aap (Institute) | Hum (Nivy) |
|-----------------|------------|
| Classrooms, computers | Curriculum, training system |
| Local coordination, attendance | Practicals, assessment |
| Student outreach support | Career prep, monthly review |
| Designated coordinator | Verified opportunity outreach |

---

## 7. Chheh mahine ka pilot

Shuruaat **limited, likhe hue scope** se:

- Kaunse programs, kitne students, kitne hours—pehle clear  
- Us agreed scope par training fee nahi (pilot)  
- Bijli, internet, local staff time jaise costs pehle se kaun uthayega—likha hoga  
- Har mahine progress review  
- Chheh mahine baad: aage paid partnership, change, ya band—**aapki aur humari dono ki nai agreement**  

Free pilot ko chhupa hua paid model nahi banaya gaya. Baad ka paid option shuru se transparent hai.

---

## 8. Aage commercial (high level)

Agar pilot theek chale to:

- Paid practical courses (revenue share **ya** fixed delivery fee—cost sheet ke baad)  
- Advanced modules  
- Capacity ho to outsourcing / workforce delivery **alag contract** par  

Har line alag terms; ek formula mein sab mix nahi.

---

## 9. Naukri / hiring — seedhi baat

- Hum skills, assessment, interview prep aur **available** opportunities tak pahunch mein madad karenge  
- Nivy mein jab real need / vacancy ho, eligible students consider ho sakte hain  
- Doosri companies / projects jahan verified opportunity ho, wahan bhi  
- **Har student ko job guarantee nahi** denge jab confirmed vacancy na ho  

---

## 10. Close

Agla practical step:

1. Aapke courses, computers, student volume thoda samajhna  
2. Aapke hisaab se short written pilot proposal  
3. Dono OK hon to limited pilot start  

Aap bataiye—is hafte kab 20 minute assessment / detailed baat convenient rahegi?
