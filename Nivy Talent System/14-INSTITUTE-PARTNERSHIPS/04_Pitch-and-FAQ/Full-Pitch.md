# Full Pitch — Outline (5–7 minutes)

## Sequence (same as presentation flow)

1. **Opening** — Interest in students + company intro (not sales dump).  
2. **Their strength** — You already have infrastructure and local reach.  
3. **The gap** — Theory courses vs industry workflows; weak practical + career path.  
4. **Our addition** — Curriculum, practical work, assessment, career prep, verified opportunities.  
5. **Verticals** — Accounting, Digital Marketing, (later) Software/AI + Career layer.  
6. **How work is split** — Institute: infra + coordination; Nivy: training system + quality + career.  
7. **Six-month pilot** — Free only for written scope; measure KPIs; then mutual decision.  
8. **Future revenue** — Paid courses, share/fee models, possible outsourcing if capacity fits.  
9. **Hiring honesty** — Our company and other employers may consider eligible students; no blanket guarantee.  
10. **Close** — Assess institute → written short proposal → pilot if both agree.  

## Key phrases (Hindi)

- “Aapke existing institute ko aage badhane ka ek practical tareeka”  
- “Pehle limited pilot, phir results, phir aage”  
- “Naukri ki guarantee nahi — asli skills aur available opportunities”
