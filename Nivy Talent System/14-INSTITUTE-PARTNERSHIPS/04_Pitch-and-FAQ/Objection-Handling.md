# Objection Handling — Outline (Phase B1)

## Objection → Response direction (outline)

| # | Objection | Response direction |
|---|-----------|--------------------|
| 1 | Placement wale pehle se aate hain | Hum sirf placement nahi — training + practical + assessment + career system |
| 2 | Free kyun? Baad mein pakadoge? | Scope likha hua; paid option day-1 clear; pilot ke baad aap decide |
| 3 | Naukri guarantee do | Guarantee nahi; skills + confirmed vacancy par selection |
| 4 | Hamare courses kaafi hain | Replace nahi; practical + career layer add |
| 5 | Trainer quality? | Curriculum, senior review, assessments, monthly report process |
| 6 | Investment nahi karna | Major new infra nahi; existing rooms/PCs; ops costs pehle list |
| 7 | Students fee nahi denge | Pilot se demand test; paid tab jab value dikhe |
| 8 | Students / data le jaoge | Consent + privacy; institute relationship respect |
| 9 | Outsourcing mein loss? | Alag commercial schedule; salary/supervision/margin written |
| 10 | Time nahi / baad mein | Short one-pager + later 15 min; no pressure |
| 11 | Pehle results dikhao | Pilot is the results mechanism; limited start |
| 12 | Exclusive lock-in? | Pilot ke baad mutual; no forced long term |

## Tone rules
- Unke current training ko attack mat karo
- Commercial future chhupao mat
- Hamesha joint review after pilot invite karo
