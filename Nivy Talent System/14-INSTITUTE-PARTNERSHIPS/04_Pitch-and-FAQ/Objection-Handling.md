# Objection Handling (Full Draft)

**Tone:** Calm, respectful, factual Hindi

---

### 1. “Placement wale pehle se aate rehte hain”

Samajh sakta hoon sir. Farq ye hai ki hum sirf drive / promise par nahi aate. Practical training, assessment, portfolio aur career prep ke saath system ki baat hai. Placement sirf jahan opportunity verified ho.

---

### 2. “Free kyun? Baad mein pakadoge?”

Is liye free rakha ki pehle limited scope mein results dikhein. Paid model chhupa nahi—shuru se kaha hai pilot ke baad nayi agreement se decide hoga. Scope likha hoga; unlimited free nahi.

---

### 3. “Naukri guarantee do”

Main guarantee nahi de sakta jab vacancy confirm na ho. De sakta hoon: skills, assessment, interview prep, aur available opportunities par fair consideration. Brochure wali false guarantee nahi denge.

---

### 4. “Hamare courses kaafi hain”

Bilkul—aapka base important hai. Hum replace nahi kar rahe. Uspar practical workflow aur career layer jodne ka option de rahe hain. Jo useful lage wahi pilot mein lein.

---

### 5. “Trainer quality?”

Delivery approved process se hogi: curriculum, practicals, rubrics, monthly review. Naam aur schedule pilot se pehle clear.

---

### 6. “Investment nahi karna”

Bada naya infra nahi maangte. Existing rooms/PCs. Bijli/net/coordinator time pehle matrix mein. Chhupi capital call nahi.

---

### 7. “Students fee nahi denge”

Is liye pehle limited pilot—demand aur value test. Paid tab jab dono ko lage students value de rahe hain.

---

### 8. “Students / data le jaoge”

Consent aur privacy rules. Institute relationship respect. Employer ko relevant info consent ke saath.

---

### 9. “Outsourcing mein loss ho jaayega”

Outsourcing alag contract hoga: billing, worker pay, supervision, margin, rework—pehle likha. Training pilot se auto-merge nahi.

---

### 10. “Time nahi / baad mein”

Theek hai. Ek page summary chhod / bhej deta hoon. Jab suvidha ho 15 minute fix kar lena. Pressure nahi.

---

### 11. “Pehle results dikhao”

Pilot hi results ka mechanism hai—limited students, monthly measure, phir decide. Bina pilot ke bade promise nahi.

---

### 12. “Exclusive lock-in?”

Pilot limited. Long exclusive tabhi jab dono explicitly agree karein. Forced lock-in nahi.

---

## Closing line after any hard objection

Aapki concern valid hai. Is liye hum likh kar, limited scope se, measure karke aage badhte hain—taaki trust rahe.
