# Six-Month Pilot Plan

## Overview
Time-bound, measurable pilot between Nivy and the partner institute.

| Month | Focus | Key activities |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | Kickoff & baseline | Cohort lock, orientation, baseline assessment, RACI confirm |
| 2–3 | Training core | Module delivery, mid-point review, early feedback |
| 4–5 | Matching & prep | Opportunity pipeline, interview prep, applications |
| 6 | Outcomes & decision | Placement support, final report, go/no-go on scale |

## Cadence
- Weekly internal ops sync (Nivy)  
- Bi-weekly or monthly institute update  
- End-of-pilot formal review meeting

## Success metrics
See Success-Metrics.md (completion, conversion, feedback, NPS).

## Exit / scale
Either continue / expand under new agreement, or close cleanly per Exit-and-Dispute process.
