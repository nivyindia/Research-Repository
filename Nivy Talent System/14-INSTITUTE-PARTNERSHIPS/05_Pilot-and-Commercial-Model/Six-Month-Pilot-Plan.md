# Six-Month Pilot Plan — Outline (Phase C1)

## 1. Purpose
- Validate demand, quality, outcomes, economics before long-term paid deal

## 2. Must define in writing before start
- Programs (max 1–2 verticals)
- Max students / batches
- Hours & delivery mode
- What is free vs still paid by institute (power, net, local staff time)
- Trainer travel / software cost owner
- KPI list & review dates
- Exit: no auto-renewal of free terms

## 3. Month plan (template)
| Month | Focus |
|-------|--------|
| 1 | Assessment, batch select, baseline skills, kickoff |
| 2–3 | Core training + practicals |
| 4–5 | Projects, competency tests, portfolio, career prep |
| 6 | Final assess, opportunity match (if real), joint review |

## 4. KPIs
- Enrolment, completion, practical pass, portfolio, interview-ready, interviews, paid work, retention, cost, satisfaction

## 5. After month 6
- Written decision: expand paid / modify / stop
