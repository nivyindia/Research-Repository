# Six-Month Pilot Plan — Outline

## Purpose

Test demand, training quality, student outcomes and commercial viability **before** long-term paid partnership.

## Must define in writing before start

1. Which programs (max 1–2 verticals)  
2. Max students / batches  
3. Training hours & delivery mode  
4. What is free vs what costs institute still pays (power, net, local staff time)  
5. Trainer travel / software cost owner  
6. KPI list & monthly review date  
7. Exit / no auto-renewal of free terms  

## Month-by-month (template)

| Month | Focus |
|-------|--------|
| 1 | Institute assessment, batch selection, baseline skill test, kickoff |
| 2–3 | Core training + practical assignments |
| 4–5 | Projects, competency tests, portfolio, career prep |
| 6 | Final assessments, opportunity matching (where real), joint review |

## KPIs to measure

Enrolment, completion, practical pass, portfolio, interview-ready, interviews given, paid work/employment, 30/90-day retention, cost & revenue (if any), satisfaction.

## After month 6

Written decision: expand paid / modify / stop. No silent continuation of free delivery.
