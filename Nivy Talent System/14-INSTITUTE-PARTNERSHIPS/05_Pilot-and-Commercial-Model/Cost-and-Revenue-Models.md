# Cost and Revenue Models — Outline

## Three separate lines (do not mix)

### 1. Pilot
- Free only for pre-agreed scope  
- Cost matrix: who pays electricity, internet, trainer travel, tools, marketing  

### 2. Paid training
- Model A: Revenue share on course fee (after cost sheet)  
- Model B: Fixed delivery fee per student/batch  
- Model C: Hybrid (base fee + share on advanced modules)  
- Provisional 50:50 only as modelling start — not final without costs  

### 3. Outsourcing / workforce
- Client billing rate  
- Worker compensation (legal compliance)  
- Institute supervision fee  
- Nivy client acquisition & management margin  
- Rework, absence, replacement rules  

## Pricing philosophy

Price by value package (hours, projects, assessment, career support), not by copying CCC/ADCA market rates alone.
