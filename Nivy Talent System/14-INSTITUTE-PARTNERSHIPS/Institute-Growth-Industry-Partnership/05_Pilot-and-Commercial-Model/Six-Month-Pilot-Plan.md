# Six-Month Pilot
**Pre-launch:** institute/cohort, trainer, module, timetable, lab/software, outcomes, rubric, cost, student notice/consent, privacy and work/payment rules approved.
| Period | Activity | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-start | Needs, baseline, agreement | Signed scope and baseline |
| Month 1 | Foundation and diagnostic | Attendance, diagnostic rubric |
| Month 2 | Guided practice | Assignments and feedback |
| Month 3 | Midpoint review | Minutes and corrective actions |
| Month 4 | Applied demo project | Project rubric; synthetic data by default |
| Month 5 | Portfolio/interview preparation | Portfolio checklist |
| Month 6 | Final assessment and decision | Final report; scale/revise/stop |
Monthly reviews cover attendance, completion, competency, feedback, cost variance, risks and incidents. Positive training results do not prove job placement or income outcomes.
