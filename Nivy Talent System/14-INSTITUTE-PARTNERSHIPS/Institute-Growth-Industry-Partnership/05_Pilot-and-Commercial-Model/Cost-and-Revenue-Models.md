# Cost and Revenue Models
## Cost lines
Trainer prep/delivery, travel, software/licences, materials, lab, admin/support, assessment, certification if any, marketing/printing, tax and contingency.
## Options to compare
1. Fixed-fee pilot with deliverables/milestones.
2. Per-cohort/per-student fee with minimum cohort and refund rules.
3. Revenue share with exact revenue base, deductions, taxes, refunds, reporting, payment dates and audit rights.
4. Verified sponsored pilot.
5. In-kind contributions itemised by party.
