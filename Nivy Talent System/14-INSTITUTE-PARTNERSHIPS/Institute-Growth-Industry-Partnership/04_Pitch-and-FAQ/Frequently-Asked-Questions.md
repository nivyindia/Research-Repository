# FAQs
**Job guarantee?** नहीं; selection employer/client पर निर्भर है।
**Upfront cost?** Scope, trainer, software, travel and other costs assess करने के बाद लिखित estimate।
**Student fee?** केवल पहले से disclosed, agreed and written terms पर।
**Certificate/recognition?** Issuer and recognition status verify होगा; unsupported accreditation claim नहीं।
**Client projects?** केवल real authorised project, supervision, scope, consent, IP and payment terms के साथ।
**Student data?** Minimum necessary, restricted access, agreed retention; public GitHub में नहीं।
**Pilot के बाद?** Review के बाद continue/revise/pause/close.
**International opportunities?** केवल verified and currently valid opportunities; कोई broad guarantee नहीं।
