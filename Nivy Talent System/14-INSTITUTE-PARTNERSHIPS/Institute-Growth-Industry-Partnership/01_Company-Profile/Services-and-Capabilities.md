## Capability gate
हर module शुरू करने से पहले दर्ज करें:
- Named trainer and backup
- Delivery hours and cohort limit
- Lesson plan and learning outcomes
- Licensed/legal software and lab requirements
- Assignments and answer keys/rubrics
- Student support and escalation
- Cost and any external dependency
- Assessment and quality review
- Data/security risk controls

Gate pass न हो तो module को proposal में confirmed deliverable की तरह न दिखाएं।
