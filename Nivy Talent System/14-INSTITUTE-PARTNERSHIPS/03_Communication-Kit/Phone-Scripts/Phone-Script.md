# Phone Script (Full Draft)

---

## A. Cold opening (20–30 sec)

Namaste sir, main [Naam], Nivy Global se bol raha hoon.  
Hum accounting, digital marketing aur related industry kaam karte hain. Kuch computer institutes se is liye baat kar rahe hain ki students ko practical industry training aur career preparation mil sake—aapke existing classrooms aur computers ke saath.  
Kya 2 minute sun sakte hain?

---

## B. Agar haan

Short model: infra aapka, training system humara. Shuruaat limited 6-month pilot se—likhe scope par training fee nahi; baad mein results dekh kar decide.  
Agar interest ho to 15–20 minute visit ya detailed call fix kar lein. Kab theek rahega?

---

## C. Agar busy

Koi baat nahi sir. Kab 10 minute call ya short visit better rahega? Main WhatsApp par ek page summary bhej sakta hoon—jab time mile dekh lijiyega.

---

## D. Agar “job guarantee?”

Guarantee tabhi jab vacancy confirm ho. Hum skills, assessment aur available opportunities par focus karte hain—false guarantee nahi.

---

## E. Agar not interested

Theek hai sir, aapki raay ka samman. Agar baad mein practical training side explore karein to contact open hai. Namaste.

---

## F. After call (mandatory)

Log: date · interest · objection · next action · next date → Contact tracker.
