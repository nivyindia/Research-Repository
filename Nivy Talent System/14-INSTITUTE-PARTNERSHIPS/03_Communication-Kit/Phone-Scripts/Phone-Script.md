1. **Opening 20–30 sec**
   - Name, company, domains

2. **If yes — mini pitch**
   - Infra aapka / training system humara

5. **If not interested**
   - Polite close; door open for future

6. **Always after call**
   - Log in Contact tracker: date, interest, next step
