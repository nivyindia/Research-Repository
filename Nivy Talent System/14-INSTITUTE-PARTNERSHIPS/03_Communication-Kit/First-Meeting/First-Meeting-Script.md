# First Meeting Script — Outline (Lucknow Hindi)

**Tone:** Tahzeebdar, apna-pan, non-salesy. No heavy English, no Urdu flourish, no pressure.

## Opening

- Namaste / Kaise hain aap?
- Nivy Global se — industry kaam (accounting, digital marketing, software/AI)
- Aap pehle se students sikhate hain; hum soch rahe the milkar practical training + career preparation kaise jod sakte hain
- 2 minute baat karne ki ijazat?

## If they say “bataiye”

- Students courses kar lete hain lekin industry ka real kaam aur job-ready skills ka gap
- Aapke paas classrooms, computers, students
- Hum curriculum, practical training system, assessment, career prep laa sakte hain
- Existing institute mein additional industry-training program

## Free pilot (clear, limited)

- Selected institutes ke saath 6-month pilot
- Pehle se likhe scope ke hisaab se training support; us scope par training fee nahi
- Results track: skills, completion, interview readiness, available opportunities
- Baad mein aap decide — paid partnership ya nahi

## Benefit to institute

- Naya major infra nahi
- Naye practical programs test karne ka mauka
- Reputation + future admissions possibility
- Baad mein revenue share / outsourcing only if mutual agree

## Job honesty

- Training + career support + available opportunities
- Guarantee nahi jab confirmed vacancy na ho

## Close

- Institute thoda samajh lein (courses, computers, students)
- Short written plan
- Is hafte 15–20 min meeting?

**Do not:** Dump full commercial model, attack their training, promise jobs.
