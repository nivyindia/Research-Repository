# Email Templates — Outline (Phase B2)

## Templates to draft later

### 1. Introduction email
- Subject line options
- Who we are
- Why writing
- Pilot concept (one paragraph)
- Ask for call/meeting
- Optional one-page attach

### 2. Proposal send email
- Thanks for meeting
- What is attached
- 3 key points summary
- Open questions invite
- Suggested next step

### 3. Meeting confirmation email
- Time/place
- Agenda
- What to prepare (courses list, approx student count, PC count)

### 4. Follow-up email (no response)
- Soft reminder
- Re-attach one-pager
- Easy opt-out line

## Style
- Formal-warm Hindi or simple English
- No aggressive CTA language
