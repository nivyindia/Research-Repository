# WhatsApp Templates (Full Draft)

**Style:** Short, respectful Hindi

---

### 1. First message

Namaste sir,  
Main [Naam], Nivy Global.  
[Call/visit] ke baad short yaad dilane ke liye: hum institutes ke saath practical industry training + career preparation ka limited pilot model discuss kar rahe hain—aapke existing classrooms/computers ke saath.  
Ek page summary bhej raha hoon. Jab time mile padh lijiye; sawal ho to batayiye.

---

### 2. Send PDF / one-pager

Sir, summary / presentation PDF attach hai.  
Usme roles, 6-month pilot idea aur next step hai.  
15 minute call/meeting ke liye koi din bata dein?

---

### 3. Follow-up (no reply)

Sir, pehle wala message dekh paaye?  
Agar abhi busy hain to bata dein—pressure nahi, sirf clarity ke liye.

---

### 4. Meeting propose

Sir, meeting ke liye 2 options:  
1) [Din, time]  
2) [Din, time]  
Institute par / online—jo aapke liye aasan ho.

---

### 5. Meeting confirm

Confirm: [Din, time, jagah].  
Agenda: aapke courses/capacity samajhna + pilot idea short mein.  
Milte hain.

---

### 6. Reminder (day before)

Sir, kal [time] meeting ki yaad.  
Koi change ho to bata dein.

---

### 7. Post-meeting thanks

Sir, aaj baat ke liye dhanyavad.  
Note kiya: [1–2 points].  
2–3 din mein short written proposal bhejunga.

---

### 8. Proposal sent

Sir, pilot proposal attach/link hai.  
Padh kar sawal bhej dein; uske baad next step fix kar lenge.

---

### 9. Soft decline reply

Theek hai sir, aapke faisle ka samman.  
Agar baad mein practical training side sochen to message kar sakte hain.

---

### 10. Pilot welcome

Sir, pilot start ke liye next steps:  
1) Coordinator naam  
2) Batch size confirm  
3) Start date  
4) Baseline assessment slot  
Detail kal call par finalize kar lein.
