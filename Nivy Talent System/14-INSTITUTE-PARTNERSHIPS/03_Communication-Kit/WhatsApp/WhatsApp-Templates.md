# WhatsApp Templates — Outline

## 1. First message (after call / visit)

Namaste sir,  
Main [Name], Nivy Global.  
Aaj / kal baat hui thi — institute ke students ke liye industry-level practical training aur career preparation ka short model share karna tha.  
Ek page ka summary bhej raha hoon. Jab time mile padh lijiye; sawal ho to batayiye.

## 2. Sending presentation PDF

Sir, baithak ke hisaab se short presentation / summary PDF attach hai.  
Isme infrastructure aapka, training system humara, aur 6-month pilot ka idea hai.  
Padhne ke baad 15 minute call / meeting fix kar lein?

## 3. Follow-up (no reply)

Sir, pehle wala message dekh paaye?  
Agar abhi busy hain to bata dein kab suvidha ho — pressure nahi, sirf clarity ke liye.

## 4. Meeting confirm

Sir, [din/time] meeting confirm.  
Location / online?  
Main institute ke current courses aur facilities samajh kar aapke hisaab se pilot idea discuss karunga.

## 5. After meeting thank-you

Sir, aaj baat ke liye dhanyavad.  
Jo points note kiye: [1–2 lines].  
Agle 2–3 din mein short written proposal bhejunga.

**Style:** Short, respectful, no spam, no ALL CAPS sales.
