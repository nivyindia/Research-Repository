Hours and exact week counts: **TBD** after pilot annex freeze.

## A. Practical Accounting & Tax Operations

**Objective:** Students perform basic office-style accounting workflows with documentation discipline—not only software menu tours.

### Modules
1. Business documents & books overview  
2. Recording routines (sales/purchase/cash/bank concepts)  
3. Bank reconciliation practice  
4. GST-oriented data prep & exception spotting (supervised)  
5. AP/AR hygiene  
6. Month-end checklist & simple MIS  
7. Review comments & error logs  
8. Career prep for accounts roles  

### Tools (as available)
Tally / spreadsheets / others per lab — list in batch plan.

### Outputs
Recon exercise · sample filing prep pack (non-live) · error log · portfolio folder

**Compliance note:** Live statutory filing only by authorized persons under law; training uses supervised/simulated data.

---

## B. Digital Marketing & Growth Operations

**Objective:** Plan and report work against business goals; not only “post creatives.”

### Modules
1. Funnel & goal setting  
2. SEO / content basics  
3. Social presence ops  
4. Ads concepts (platform-agnostic discipline)  
5. Landing / tracking hygiene  
6. CRM / lead list hygiene  
7. Weekly reporting pack  
8. AI-assisted drafting with human QA  
9. Career prep for marketing ops roles  

### Outputs
Mini audit · content calendar sample · report dashboard sample · portfolio
