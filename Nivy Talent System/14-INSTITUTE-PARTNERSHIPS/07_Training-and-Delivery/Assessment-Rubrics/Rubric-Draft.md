# Assessment Rubric (Draft)

Scale: **1** Beginning · **2** Developing · **3** Proficient · **4** Strong

| Dimension | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|-----------|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Many material errors | Frequent fixes needed | Minor errors | Reliable |
| Process completeness | Steps skipped | Partial | Most steps | Full + checks |
| Documentation | Unclear | Basic | Clear | Review-ready |
| Tool use | Stuck often | Needs guidance | Independent | Efficient |
| Timeliness | Late / incomplete | Often late | On time | Early/buffer |
| Communication | Unclear | Basic | Clear updates | Professional |

**Interview-ready flag (suggested):** Average ≥ 3 on core dimensions + portfolio complete + mock interview done.  
Exact threshold: **TBD** per pilot.
