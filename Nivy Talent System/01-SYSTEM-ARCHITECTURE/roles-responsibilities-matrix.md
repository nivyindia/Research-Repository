## Sheet extract: Sales ladder

CSO/VP → Sales Director → Regional SM → Area SM → Key Account Manager → Sales Exec → Inside Sales → Sales Coordinator → **Sales Trainee**

Trainee KPIs: training completion, knowledge score, leads generated, process participation, field reports.

**Mentor / Supervisor / Team Lead:** first-line support, task assign, quality, attendance, escalate; KPIs on team productivity, on-time completion, quality, retention.
