## Recruitment & Training SOP (day-by-day)

**Objective:** Filter → guide → onboard to **direct job** or **3-month academy**.

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 0 | Job live; apps into database |
| 1 | Bulk WhatsApp + optional email → Quiz 1 (role fit) deadline Day 2 |
| 2 | Call non-respondents; form closes EOD; auto-reply pass/fail |
| 3 | Path choice email: **A Direct Job** (Quiz 2) vs **B Academy** (Quiz 3) |
| 4 | Quiz 2/3 close; grade (Job ~≥16/20; Academy diagnostic lower bar) |
| 5 | Path A → HR interview invites; Path B → Academy offer + orientation meet |
| 6–7 | HR interviews / mandatory orientation; WA group only after attendance |
| 8+ | Batch start |

**Path A:** FT BDE ₹18–30k + incentives; multi-round interviews.  
**Path B:** Stipend path (up to ~₹14k stated); job on successful completion.

## HR track day themes (from Sales Training Schedule sheet)

Day 1: HR intro, tools, about us, recruitment process with deadlines  
Day 2: Social profiles, job posting (LinkedIn etc.), Bitrix, ChatGPT emails  
Day 3: Checking reports, interviews/MCQ pass-fail, offer letters, document verification  
Day 4: HR policies + employee feedback loops  
Day 5: Master MCQ → Day 7 interview → Day 90 performance test  

**KPI examples:** Applications 500/mo target context; Hired/Trained/Passed/Joined tracking via forms+Excel.
