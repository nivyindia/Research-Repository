## Marketing funnel stages

| Stage | Objective | KPI examples | Activities / content |
|-------|-----------|--------------|----------------------|
| Awareness | Brand visibility | Impressions, reach | Paid ads, SM campaigns; blogs, infographics |
| Consideration | Trial & comparison | CTR | Email nurture, demos; case studies, comparison guides |
| Conversion | First purchase/sign-up | Lead→customer rate | Discounts, trials, offers; demo videos |
| Retention | Keep & engage | Repeat purchase / renewal | Loyalty, newsletters; support guides, how-tos |
| Advocacy | Brand promoters | NPS | Referrals, UGC, testimonials; success stories |

## Services knowledge (sample answers)

- SEO types: on-page, off-page, technical — **not** "Paid SEO"  
- SEO goal: organic rankings + qualified traffic  
- PPC platform for instant visibility: Google Ads; USP: budget control + measurable ROI  
- SMM: engagement + brand awareness  
- Content: thought leadership  
- Email: high ROI + relationships  
- CRO method: heatmap analysis  
- Influencer: niche audiences  
- Accounting services include bookkeeping/recon/payroll — **not** SMM  

## Market & competitor

SMB pain: limited marketing budget | E-com: high ads + abandoned carts | Trend: voice search | GDPR/CCPA: limit tracking | Large agency weakness: rigid + high cost  

## CRM / prospecting / BANT

CRM: manage leads & customer data; status = sales cycle position.  
Lead sources: LinkedIn, freelancing, Google Ads — not payroll.  
Cold open: rapport + relevance.  
**BANT A = Authority.** Need Q: current lead-gen problem.
