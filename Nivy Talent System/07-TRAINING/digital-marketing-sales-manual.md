## Chapters

1. **Understand prospect’s business** — industry research; decision-makers; discovery Qs (biggest challenge, lead gen, budget)  
2. **Build credibility** — agency intro, clients, certifications, success metrics (brochure)  
3. **Present solutions** — map SEO/PPC/SMM/content to client problems  
4. **Case studies & social proof** — ≥3 before/after with ROI, traffic, leads  
5. **Price & ROI** — value over cost; projections; transparent pricing models  
6. **Objection handling** — “tried before,” “too expensive,” “in-house” responses  
7. **Closing** — trial, assumptive, urgency  
8. **Follow-up** — summary email, resources, regular non-pushy check-ins  

Appendix: cold email SEO, LinkedIn scripts, follow-up sequence, onboarding checklist.

## Client onboarding checklist

**Pre-kickoff:** welcome, questionnaire, brand assets, signed contract, invoice setup  

**Kickoff:** team intro, business model/USP/market, objectives, competitors, past campaigns, KPIs  

**Access:** CMS, GA4, GSC, GTM, socials, ad accounts, ESP, CRM, brand guide  

**Strategy:** audit, competitor analysis, personas, funnel, content/SEO/ads plans, timeline  

**Tools:** PM tool, dashboards, reporting cadence, comms channel  

**Execution:** launch + QA tracking/landing pages; early optimize  

**Post:** recap email, onboarding doc, 30-day report meeting, feedback, upsell notes
