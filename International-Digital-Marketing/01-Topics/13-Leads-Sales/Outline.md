# Leads-Sales — Full Outline (International)

## 1. Funnel & definitions
- Full path: awareness → customer → advocate
- MQL/SQL/SAL definitions with sales agreement
- Entry/exit evidence rules (not activity theater)
- Speed-to-lead SLAs by market/timezone
- Recycling and nurture re-entry rules

## 2. Demand generation
- Category education programs
- Events (virtual/physical) by region
- Webinars language strategy
- Community and creator-led demand
- Partnership and co-marketing

## 3. Demand capture
- High-intent SEO/SEM alignment
- LPs and form strategy by country
- Demo, trial, quote, contact paths
- Chat/WhatsApp capture where preferred
- Phone and local sales office routing

## 4. Lead modes
- Inbound, outbound, ABM tiers
- Marketplace leads and channel partners
- Data quality and enrichment standards
- Consent-compliant prospecting rules by country

## 5. ABM (if B2B)
- Tier 1/2/3 account lists by market
- Plays: 1:1, 1:few, 1:many
- Sales + marketing joint cadences

## 6. Sales enablement
- Global deck + local case inserts
- ROI tools, security one-pagers, procurement packs
- Battlecards, objection maps
- Demo environments and sandbox data rules
- Competitive displacement plays

## 7. Process steps
1. Define stages + SLA
2. Map offers to stages per market
3. Build capture points + routing
4. Enablement kit v1
5. Funnel review rituals
6. ABM pilot if justified

## 8. Outputs
- Stage dictionary
- Lead scoring model
- Routing matrix (country/language)
- Enablement library index
