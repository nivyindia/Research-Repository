# Search-SEO — Full Outline (International)

## 1. Goals & KPIs by market
- Organic revenue/leads by country
- Non-brand growth, brand SERP ownership
- Local pack / Maps where relevant
- Shopping/organic product visibility
- AI answer/citation presence (secondary metric)

## 2. Technical SEO
- Crawl budget, indexation, log insights (enterprise)
- CWV, mobile, JS rendering issues
- Canonical, pagination, faceted nav rules
- International technical (with Global-Local)
- App indexing / news / video SEO if applicable

## 3. On-page & content SEO
- Intent-balanced cluster model
- Title/meta/H1 standards per language
- E-E-A-T signals: authors, sources, experience
- Multilingual keyword research (not translated English lists only)
- Internal linking at scale

## 4. Off-page & digital PR
- Link earning themes by market
- Local publications and HARO-style sources
- Damaging link monitoring

## 5. Local SEO
- Google Business Profile (or local equivalents)
- NAP consistency, categories, services
- Reviews response playbook
- Multi-location architecture

## 6. Ecommerce / marketplace search
- Product data quality, unique descriptions
- Category architecture
- Feed SEO for Shopping surfaces

## 7. AI search / AEO / GEO
- Entity clarity (Organization, Product, Person)
- Answer-ready structure (clear questions, facts)
- Original data and citable assets
- Tracking approach for AI surfaces
- Principle: strong classic SEO + helpful content first

## 8. Other discovery
- YouTube search optimization
- Social search keywords (TikTok, etc.)
- Voice and visual search notes where material

## 9. Process steps
1. Market-level goals
2. Technical + content audit
3. Cluster maps per language
4. Roadmap 90 days
5. Measurement (GSC per property, analytics, conversions)
6. International QA

## 10. Outputs
- Audit summaries per P1 market
- Cluster maps
- Technical backlog
- Local SEO checklist
- AI visibility tracking notes
