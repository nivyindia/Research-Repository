# Market Research — Full Outline (International)

## 1. Market sizing & dynamics
- TAM / SAM / SOM method and assumptions
- Category growth, substitutes, adjacent categories
- Digital ad/media spend context by market (where data exists)
- Search demand and category query trends
- Ecommerce vs offline mix by country
- B2B vs B2C purchasing norms by market

## 2. Customer & demand insights
- Jobs-to-be-done and trigger events
- Barriers: price, trust, regulation, switching cost
- Journey length and research channels by country
- Seasonality, fiscal calendars, cultural peaks (Ramadan, Diwali, CNY, BFCM, etc.)
- Payment preferences and credit norms
- Language preference (official vs business English)

## 3. Country / city intelligence
- Macro: GDP/capita, digital penetration, smartphone, logistics
- Language(s), scripts, RTL needs
- Currency, tax (VAT/GST), invoicing norms
- Dominant search engines (not only Google)
- Dominant social / messaging / video apps
- Marketplaces and retail media importance
- Review platforms and trust signals
- Local media and influencer landscape
- Data residency and privacy regime summary

## 4. Market selection & prioritization
- Scorecard: size, growth, competition, fit, difficulty, strategic value
- P1/P2/P3 tiers and entry sequence
- Beachhead city/region inside a country
- Partnership vs direct entry signals

## 5. Expansion path design
- Country → region → city → audience → offer → channel
- Standard global playbook vs local rewrite triggers
- Resource implications (content, support, ads, legal)

## 6. Research process steps
1. Desk research (official stats, platform insights, industry reports)
2. Demand validation (search, social, marketplace signals)
3. Competitor footprint by market
4. Primary research sample plan (interviews/surveys)
5. Scorecard workshop
6. Document sources, date, confidence level

## 7. Outputs
- Global market map
- Scorecard workbook
- P1 market one-pagers
- Assumptions & risk log
- Local platform matrix by country

## 8. Ongoing
- Quarterly signal review (demand, regulation, platform shifts)
- War-room inputs for new country launches
