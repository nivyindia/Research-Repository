# Strategy — Outline (kya explain karna hai)

> Full essay nahi — ye checklist hai. Phase 2 mein har point expand hoga.

## 1. Business objectives
- Revenue / profit targets
- Market share / new markets
- Customer acquisition vs retention balance
- Brand awareness goals
- Product / geography expansion goals
- Time horizon (90 days / 6 months / 12 months)

## 2. Marketing objectives (SMART)
- Awareness metrics (reach, share of voice)
- Demand creation vs demand capture
- Traffic, leads, MQL, SQL targets
- Sales / revenue influenced by marketing
- Retention / advocacy targets

## 3. Strategy building blocks
- Segmentation approach
- Targeting criteria (who we will / will not serve)
- Positioning statement
- Differentiation / USP / UVP
- Category strategy (create / compete / niche)
- Competitive advantage summary

## 4. GTM & growth model
- Go-to-market motion (product-led / sales-led / hybrid)
- Market-entry approach (per country if international)
- Funnel vs flywheel vs growth loops (which we use)
- Primary growth channels (3 max at start)

## 5. Planning frameworks to apply
- RACE: Plan → Reach → Act → Convert → Engage (map our work)
- SOSTAC / Opportunity–Strategy–Action (planning cycle)
- OKRs linked to marketing KPIs
- 90-day action plan structure

## 6. Steps (process)
1. Lock business goal + north-star metric
2. Translate to marketing objectives
3. Define audience + positioning
4. Choose channel priorities
5. Set budget bands + KPI tree
6. Write 90-day plan + owners

## 7. Outputs we will produce later
- One-page strategy summary
- KPI tree
- Channel priority matrix
- 90-day roadmap

## 8. Measure & improve
- Leading vs lagging indicators
- Review cadence (weekly / monthly / quarterly)
- When to change strategy vs tactics
