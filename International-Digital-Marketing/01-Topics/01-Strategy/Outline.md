# Strategy — Full Outline (International)

> Expand-ready points. Sources: RACE, OSA, plan templates, multi-market GTM.

## 1. Business objectives
- Revenue, profit, contribution margin by market
- Market share / category rank targets by country
- New logo vs expansion vs churn-offset goals
- Brand awareness / consideration goals (where measured)
- Product-line and geography expansion goals
- Time horizons: 90 days / 6 months / 12 months / multi-year
- Board/investor metrics vs operating metrics

## 2. Marketing objectives (SMART + math)
- Single north-star metric per planning period
- Pipeline math: revenue → deals → SQLs → MQLs → leads → traffic
- Demand creation vs demand capture split by market
- CAC, LTV, payback targets by segment and country
- Share of voice / branded search goals where relevant
- Retention/NRR influence goals (link CRM-Retention)

## 3. STP & positioning (global + local)
- Global ICP vs local segment variants
- Targeting: who we serve / explicit who we skip
- Positioning statement (global) + local proof adaptations
- USP/UVP vs competitors per priority market
- Category strategy: create / disrupt / niche / premium
- Message hierarchy: global core → regional variants

## 4. GTM & growth model
- Motion: PLG / sales-led / partner-led / hybrid by market
- Direct vs marketplace vs reseller motions
- Funnel vs flywheel vs growth loops (document choice)
- Primary 3 channels per market phase (avoid scatter)
- Owned / paid / earned mix intent by maturity of market

## 5. International strategy layer
- Market prioritization tiers (P1/P2/P3)
- Globalise → standardise → localise decisions
- Central vs local marketing ownership (hub-spoke)
- Brand consistency rules vs local flexibility rules
- Currency reporting and FX assumptions in plans
- Regulatory constraints that change offer/claims by country

## 6. Frameworks to apply (reuse)
- RACE: Plan → Reach → Act → Convert → Engage
- OSA: Opportunity → Strategy → Action (90-day)
- OKR cascade: company → marketing → channel
- Competitive advantage one-pager

## 7. Process steps
1. Lock business goal + north-star
2. Pipeline math by market tier
3. Audience + positioning (global + local notes)
4. Channel priorities + budget bands by country
5. 90-day plan + RACI
6. Review cadence (weekly tactics / monthly / quarterly strategy)

## 8. Outputs
- One-page global strategy
- Per-market strategy appendix (P1 markets)
- KPI tree
- Channel priority matrix
- 90-day roadmap

## 9. Measure & improve
- Leading vs lagging indicators
- Kill/scale rules for tactics
- Quarterly strategy refresh
- Cross-market learnings transfer process
