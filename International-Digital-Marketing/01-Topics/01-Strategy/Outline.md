# Strategy — Outline (stronger points)

> Essay nahi — expand-ready checklist. Sources: RACE, plan templates, north-star KPI practice.

## 1. Business objectives (lock first)
- Revenue / profit / contribution margin targets
- New customers vs expansion revenue split
- Market share or category position goal
- Geographic expansion targets (link Market-Research + Global-Local)
- Timeboxes: 90 days / 6 months / 12 months

## 2. Marketing objectives (SMART + pipeline math)
- North-star metric (1 only): e.g. qualified leads, paying customers, revenue influenced
- Supporting metrics: traffic, MQL, SQL, win rate, CAC, LTV
- Demand **creation** vs demand **capture** targets (separate)
- Awareness: reach / SOV only if tied to pipeline later

## 3. Audience & position
- Who we serve / who we skip (explicit)
- STP: segments → target → position
- Positioning one-liner + proof points
- USP / UVP vs top 3 competitors (link Competitors)

## 4. GTM & growth system
- Motion: product-led / sales-led / hybrid
- Primary growth loops or funnel model
- Max 3 focus channels at start (avoid scatter)
- Owned vs paid vs earned mix intent

## 5. Frameworks to map (reuse, don’t reinvent)
- **RACE:** Plan → Reach → Act → Convert → Engage
- **OSA:** Opportunity (audit + targets) → Strategy → Action (90-day)
- OKR tree → marketing KPI tree
- Phase calendar: fix foundations → scale winners

## 6. Process steps
1. Business goal + north-star
2. Pipeline / conversion math (back-calculate volume needed)
3. Audience + positioning
4. Channel priorities + budget bands
5. 90-day plan with owners
6. Review cadence (weekly tactics / monthly strategy)

## 7. Outputs
- One-page strategy
- KPI tree
- Channel priority matrix (why each channel)
- 90-day roadmap

## 8. Improve
- Leading vs lagging indicators
- Kill criteria for tactics
- Quarterly strategy refresh rules
