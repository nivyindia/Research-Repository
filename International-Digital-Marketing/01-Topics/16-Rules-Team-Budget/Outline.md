# Rules-Team-Budget — Full Outline (International)

## 1. Privacy & data protection
- GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PDPA, etc. applicability matrix
- Lawful basis, consent, legitimate interest assessments (high level)
- Cookie/CMP requirements by market
- Cross-border transfer mechanisms (high level)
- Data subject request process ownership
- Children's data / sensitive categories restrictions

## 2. Marketing & advertising compliance
- Platform policies (Google, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, etc.)
- Industry rules: health, finance, alcohol, gambling, politics by country
- Substantiation of claims; comparative advertising rules
- Influencer/affiliate disclosure (local law + platform)
- Copyright, trademark, music licensing in creatives
- Testimonials and endorsements rules
- Accessibility obligations for digital properties

## 3. Brand safety, fraud, security
- Brand safety tiers and blocklists
- Invalid traffic monitoring
- Ads.txt / sellers.json awareness for programmatic
- Account security (2FA, access reviews)
- Incident response contact path

## 4. Team & operating model
- In-house vs agency vs hybrid by function and market
- RACI for strategy, spend, creative, legal, data
- Local market managers vs global centers of excellence
- Training curriculum and certification (platform badges)
- Knowledge base and SOP ownership
- Vendor procurement and MSAs

## 5. Budget & commercial
- Global budget pool vs market P&Ls
- Allocation methods (objective-based, stage-based)
- Test vs scale % rules
- Media vs production vs tools vs people split
- Currency, invoicing entities, tax
- ROI/ROAS targets by channel maturity
- Reallocation cadence

## 6. Process steps
1. Build compliance matrix by P1 market
2. Policy one-pagers for claims/disclosure
3. RACI and approval workflows
4. Budget templates multi-currency
5. Access and vendor reviews
6. Quarterly governance board

## 7. Outputs
- Compliance matrix
- Policy pack
- RACI
- Budget model
- Vendor scorecard
- Incident/crisis runbook outline
