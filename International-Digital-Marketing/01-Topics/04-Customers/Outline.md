# Customers — Outline

## 1. ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
- Industry / firmographics (B2B) or demographics (B2C)
- Company size, revenue, geography
- Decision maker roles and buying committee
- Budget range and tech environment
- Pain, trigger events, intent signals
- Buying cycle length

## 2. Personas
- Buyer / user / influencer / champion / blocker
- Goals, fears, objections
- Content and channel preferences
- Jobs-to-be-done

## 3. Segmentation
- Demographic / geographic / firmographic
- Psychographic / behavioral / intent
- Lifecycle stage and value tiers
- Engagement levels

## 4. Data types we will use
- Anonymous, first-party, zero-party
- What we will not rely on (privacy constraints)

## 5. Steps
1. Draft ICP hypotheses
2. Validate with sales/support data or interviews
3. Write 2–4 personas
4. Map segments to offers and channels
5. Refresh when product or market changes

## 6. Outputs
- ICP one-pager
- Persona sheets
- Segment → channel map
