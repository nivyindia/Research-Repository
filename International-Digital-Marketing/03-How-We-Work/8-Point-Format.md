# Har Topic Mein Kya Likhna Hai (8 Points)

Har topic / sub-topic ke **Details** mein ye 8 blocks:

| # | Point | Matlab |
|---|--------|--------|
| 1 | **Why** | Kyun zaroori, objective, outcome |
| 2 | **What** | Scope, inputs, outputs |
| 3 | **How** | Process, steps, SOP link |
| 4 | **Who** | Owner, human, AI agent |
| 5 | **Tools** | Software, resources, APIs |
| 6 | **Automate** | Automation, AI, agents, integrations |
| 7 | **Measure** | KPI, quality, cost, ROI |
| 8 | **Improve** | Feedback, optimize, reuse, scale |

Isse zyada fields mat badhao. Extra tags (country, business type) alag short note mein.
