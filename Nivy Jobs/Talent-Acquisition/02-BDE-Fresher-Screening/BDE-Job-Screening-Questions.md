## Typical Selection Funnel for a BDE / Sales Profile

### Round 1: Application & Screening Filter
- Online form
- Eligibility (degree, marks, backlogs, gap years)
- Clarity & detail of application
- Laptop + stable internet confirmation

### Round 2: Aptitude & Psychometric Assessment
- Timed online test (60–90 min)
- Numerical reasoning, Verbal reasoning, Logical reasoning
- Sales aptitude & situational judgment
- Personality & drive (resilience, competitiveness, comfort with rejection)

### Round 3: Initial Screening Call (15–20 min)
- Communication clarity, energy, motivation, cultural fit
- Basic selling (e.g. sell-this-pen / 60-second pitch)

### Round 4: Role-Play Assignment (Key Round)
- Video call with Sales Manager
- Cold call role-play + optional follow-up email
- Assess resilience, thinking on feet, process, professionalism

### Round 5: Final Interview with Sales Leadership (30–45 min)
- Career goals, mindset, realism about challenges
- Quality of candidate’s questions

**Filter for 3 C’s:** Competence · Coachability · Character

## Sample Questions (from source)

### Numerical Reasoning (examples)
1. Base ₹40,000 + 10% commission on ₹3,00,000 sales → total income?
2. Cost:Selling = 5:6 → profit %?
3. Price +20% then −20% → net change?
4. Team of 8, target 1000 units; after 15 days 400 sold → units/day/person remaining?
5–10. Pie chart, table interpretation, discounts, growth targets, conversion funnel math

### Verbal & Logical (examples)
- Grammar / subject-verb agreement
- Error spotting
- Synonym of "Persuasive" in sales
- Logical sequence of a sales call
- Opposite of "Lucrative"
- Next step after first meeting; meaning of "Closing a sale"

### Sales Scenarios & Objection Handling (examples)
- "Send me information" → best response
- Goal of prospecting
- "I need to think it over"
- Preparing for a sales meeting
- Shrinking long-term client
- Follow-up after conference
- When to discuss price
- Non-responsive qualified lead
- Stakeholder joins late
- Consultative selling definition
- "Price too high" / "Happy with current supplier" / "Need to run by boss" / "Not a good time" / "Never heard of you" / Competitor cheaper / Contract too long / Tried similar before

**Effective objection handling:** Listen, acknowledge, reasoned response; then advance the conversation.
