**VIRTUAL ASSISTANT EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is made and entered into as of [Date] by and between:

**The Company:** Nivy (hereinafter referred to as the "Company")  
**The Virtual Assistant:** <<Full Name>>, R/o <<Address>>  
(hereinafter referred to as the "VA")

**WHEREAS**, the Company desires to engage the VA to perform virtual assistant services on a remote basis, and the VA desires to provide such services under the terms and conditions set forth below;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

**1. Role and Nature of Work**  
1.1 The VA shall work as a Virtual Assistant in a remote capacity.  
1.2 Duties shall include, but are not limited to: lead generation, outreach and messaging, data management, reporting, and other tasks as assigned by the Company from time to time.

**2. Work Schedule and Availability**  
2.1 The VA shall work eight (8) to nine (9) hours per day, six (6) days per week during fixed working hours designated by the Company.  
2.2 The VA must be available and responsive during all scheduled working hours. Any unavailability without prior notice shall be treated as an absence.

**3. Training and Onboarding**  
3.1 The Company may provide structured training or onboarding.  
3.2 Attendance during training is mandatory. Missing any training session without prior written approval may result in immediate termination of this Agreement.

**4. Probation Period**  
4.1 The initial three (3) months of engagement shall constitute a probation period.  
4.2 During probation, the VA’s performance, discipline, and consistency shall be evaluated.  
4.3 Continuation of the engagement on a permanent basis is contingent upon successful completion of the probation period, as determined by the Company in its sole discretion.

**5. Performance and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**  
5.1 The VA shall meet daily and weekly KPIs as defined by the Company.  
5.2 Performance shall be measured based on task completion, output quality, and timely reporting.  
5.3 The minimum acceptable performance benchmark is 65% (or such higher percentage as the Company may establish in writing).

**6. Reporting and Tracking**  
6.1 Daily reporting is mandatory and shall be completed using Company-approved tools (e.g., Google Sheets, Forms, CRM).  
6.2 The Company may monitor work through activity logs, screenshots, or output submissions.  
6.3 No report = No work counted for purposes of compensation or performance evaluation.

**7. Attendance and Leave Policy**  
7.1 Attendance during working hours is mandatory.  
7.2 Leaves must be pre-approved at least twenty-four (24) hours in advance.  
7.3 Emergency leave may be considered upon submission of a valid reason.  
7.4 Repeated absence or irregular attendance may lead to termination of this Agreement.

**8. Compensation and Payment Terms**  
8.1 Training / Initial Phase (Performance-Based)  
A stipend shall be paid only if the VA achieves a minimum 65% performance score.

| Month | Stipend Amount |
|-------|----------------|
| Month 1 | $140 |
| Month 2 | $200 |
| Month 3 | $240 |

If performance falls below 65%, the VA shall receive a sales-based payout of $10 to $35 per sale, instead of a fixed stipend.

8.2 Post-Training / Job Phase  
Upon successful completion of the probation period:  
**Base Salary**: $280 to $350 per month (depending on performance and role).  
Additional incentives may be paid based on output or results.

8.3 Performance Rules  
Payment is strictly contingent upon task completion, output quality, and KPI achievement.  
No work = No pay. No report = No work counted.

8.4 Payment Cycle  
The payment cycle is monthly. Payments shall be processed within five (5) days after the end of each payment cycle.

**9. Six-Month Commitment Clause**  
9.1 The VA agrees to a minimum six (6) month commitment from the date of joining.  
9.2 Early Exit / Breach: If the VA voluntarily resigns, stops working without notice, or otherwise fails to continue the engagement before completing six (6) months, the VA agrees to pay the Company **$25 USD** as liquidated damages for training and onboarding costs (and not as a penalty).

**10. Quality Control**  
10.1 All work shall meet the Company’s defined quality standards.  
10.2 Low-quality or spam work may be treated as non-performance and may result in withholding of payment or termination.

**11. Time Tracking and Availability**  
11.1 The VA must remain active and responsive during all working hours.  
11.2 Idle time or unexcused inactivity may negatively affect performance evaluation and compensation.

**12. Conflict of Interest**  
12.1 The VA shall not engage in any conflicting full-time employment or other work during assigned working hours.  
12.2 Any violation of this Section 12 may result in immediate termination of this Agreement.

**13. Discipline and Code of Conduct**  
13.1 The VA shall maintain professional behaviour and communication at all times.  
13.2 Grounds for disciplinary action: (a) No work or incomplete tasks; (b) Failure to submit reports; (c) Irregular attendance; (d) Non-responsiveness during work hours.  
13.3 Discipline: (1) First written warning; (2) Final written warning; (3) Termination.

**14. Payment Protection Clause**  
The Company reserves the right to withhold or cancel any payment in the event of: Fake or incomplete work; Misreporting or false data; Spam or low-quality outreach; Any violation of this Agreement or Company policies.

**15. Immediate Termination Conditions**  
The Company may terminate this Agreement immediately, without prior notice, if the VA: Performs no work for two (2) consecutive days; Fails to respond to Company communications during working hours; Repeatedly fails to follow lawful instructions.

**16. Communication Requirements**  
The VA must remain active on official communication channels (e.g., WhatsApp, Slack, Teams) during all working hours.

**17. Confidentiality**  
17.1 All Company data, client information, trade secrets, and internal processes are strictly confidential.  
17.2 The VA agrees not to disclose, share, or misuse any confidential information during or after the term of this Agreement.  
17.3 Breach of confidentiality shall entitle the Company to seek injunctive relief and any other remedies available at law or in equity.

**18. Equipment and Requirements**  
The VA shall be solely responsible for providing and maintaining: A laptop or desktop computer; A stable internet connection; A suitable remote work environment free from distractions.

**19. Re-Application Policy**  
Candidates removed due to poor performance or disciplinary violations may not reapply for a period of three (3) to six (6) months, as determined by the Company.

**20. Growth and Promotion**  
High-performing VAs may receive salary increments, team leader roles, and long-term growth opportunities at the Company’s sole discretion.

**21. General Provisions**  
21.1 Governing Law — laws of the local country.  
21.2 Entire Agreement.  
21.3 Amendments in writing signed by both parties.  
21.4 Severability.  
21.5 Waiver.
