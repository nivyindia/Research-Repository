# Stage 1: Job Details — Nivy Global Virtual Assistant (VA) Program

## Hiring: Part-Time Appointment Setter / SDR / BDM / Virtual Assistant (Remote)

- **Location:** Remote | Philippines, India, South Africa & Mexico
- **Hours:** Flexible 2–3 Hours/Day (OR Full-Time 8–9 hours variant exists)
- **Pay:** 100% Commission-Based | Earn $70–$300 per closed deal

At Nivy, we're helping businesses across USA, UK, and Europe grow through Digital Marketing, Web Development, SaaS, AI, Lead Generation, and Business Growth solutions.

We're looking for 1–2 years experienced Appointment Setters / SDRs who generate conversations and book qualified meetings via LinkedIn and Email outreach.

### What You'll Be Doing

- Prospect and identify decision-makers (CEOs, Founders, Directors)
- LinkedIn outreach and cold email campaigns
- Follow up using proven sequences
- Qualify leads and book discovery calls
- Handle basic objections via text
- Maintain CRM and tracking sheets

**No cold calling required.**

### What We're Looking For

- 1–2+ years B2B Sales / SDR / Appointment Setting / Lead Gen / BPO experience
- Strong written English
- LinkedIn prospecting experience + account safety knowledge
- Cold email + follow-up sequences
- Google Sheets, CRM, scheduling tools
- Self-driven, resilient, results-oriented

**Bonus:** LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Apollo, Instantly, Smartlead, Lemlist, agency/SaaS/AI sales experience
