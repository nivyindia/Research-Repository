## Stage 7: Training & Probation Period (3 Months)

> **Note:** The source Google Doc contained only the heading. Content below is reconstructed from S0 system design and S6 agreement for completeness. Expand when fuller source material is available.

### Candidate Experience
- Works in real environment
- Completes tasks per SOPs
- Reports daily
- Performance tracked (KPI, discipline, attendance)

### Internal
- Select limited candidates (typically top 10)
- Send offer + agreement
- Track daily KPIs
- High performers → Permanent offer
- Low performers → Exit or redirect to Paid Training Path

### Duration
3 months probation from joining date.

### Outcomes
- ≥65% (or higher threshold) → Continue / confirm
- <65% → Performance-based payout only or exit
- <50% → Exit + certificate only (per JD rules)
