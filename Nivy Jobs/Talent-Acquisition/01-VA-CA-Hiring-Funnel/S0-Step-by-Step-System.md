# NIVY VA HIRING FUNNEL (FINAL – CLEAN CANDIDATE-FIRST MODEL)

- **Stages = Only candidate journey**
- **Everything else = Internal system embedded inside**

## CORE RULE (SYSTEM LEVEL)

- Every stage has fixed deadline
- No late entries
- No reminders (hiring stages)
- Progression = invite-only (email controlled internally)

---

## STAGE 0: JOB AWARENESS

**Candidate Experience**
- Sees job opportunity
- Understands: Work hours, Discipline, Commitment
- Joins: WhatsApp Group + WhatsApp Channel
- Decides to apply
- Must act within **48 hours**

**Internal (Hidden)**
- Send JD email (batch of 50)
- Control entry via email
- Start deadline timer

---

## STAGE 1: APPLICATION + TEST

**Candidate Experience**
- Fills application form
- Accepts terms
- Attempts test
- Deadline: **48 hours**

**Internal**
- Auto evaluation (Google Form)
- Store data, send confirmation, filter candidates
- Fail / No submission → **PAID TRAINING PATH**

---

## STAGE 2: TRIAL TASK (REAL WORK)

**Candidate Experience**
- Receives task (Outreach, Lead generation, Reporting)
- Submits work
- Deadline: **24 hours (strict)**

**Internal**
- Send task email, evaluate submissions
- Fail / No submission → **PAID TRAINING PATH**

---

## STAGE 3: INTERVIEW

**Candidate Experience**
- Books slot, attends interview
- Book within 24 hours, Attend within 48 hours

**Internal**
- Shortlist, send links, evaluate
- Fail → **PAID TRAINING PATH**

---

## STAGE 4: ORIENTATION

**Candidate Experience**
- Attends orientation (work structure, targets, rules)
- Absent → removed → **PAID TRAINING PATH (optional)**

**Internal**
- Send details, final behavioral filtering

---

## STAGE 5: TRAINING & PROBATION (3 MONTHS)

**Candidate Experience**
- Works in real environment, follows SOPs, reports daily

**Internal**
- Select limited candidates (top 10)
- Send offer + agreement
- Track KPI, Discipline, Attendance
- Low performance → **PAID TRAINING PATH**

---

## STAGE 6: FINAL EVALUATION → JOB

- High Performer → Full-time job
- Low Performer → Exit OR **PAID TRAINING PATH**

---

## PARALLEL PATH: PAID TRAINING FUNNEL (REVENUE + CONVERSION)

Triggered at rejection points (Stage 1/2/3/5 fail).

**Candidate receives:** Feedback + Improvement suggestion + Training opportunity

**Internal:** Automated email sequence (Day 1–5) + WhatsApp follow-ups

**Outcome:** Purchase → Paid Training → Top performers can re-enter hiring / get job

---

## FINAL CLEAN FLOW (CANDIDATE VIEW)

```
Job → Apply → Test → Task → Interview → Orientation → Training → Job
        ↓      ↓      ↓
      Fail → Training → Job Path
```

**Strategic insight:** Candidate sees journey, not system. Filters 80–90% low-quality candidates automatically.

**Execution note:** Hiring stages → No follow-ups | Training funnel → Aggressive follow-ups
