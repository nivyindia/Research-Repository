## Objective

- Attract high-volume candidates
- Filter unserious people BEFORE they enter system
- Control flow in batches (max 50 candidates)

## Responsible

- Operations Executive (executes)
- Funnel Manager (approves batch & monitors)

## Input

- Candidate database / leads (ads, referrals, portals)
- Email list (minimum 50 per batch)

## Step-by-Step

1. **Prepare Batch** — Exactly 50 candidates; valid email; role relevance. Do NOT send >50.
2. **Send JD Email (Email 1)** — Role clarity, 8–9 hrs, fixed timing, daily reporting, 6-month commitment, **48-hour deadline**, application/test link, WhatsApp Group + Channel. Batch send with same timestamp.
3. **Start Deadline Timer** — Note send time; Deadline = +48 hours. Track in sheet.
4. **WhatsApp Entry** — Candidates join group/channel. DO NOT engage actively.
5. **No Follow-up Rule** — No reminders, no WhatsApp pings, no resend. This is the first discipline filter.

## Success Metrics

- 50 emails sent
- Expected apply rate: 20–30% (good); <10% = weak JD/audience

## Failure Conditions

- Sending >50 → chaos
- Weak JD → low-quality applicants
- Follow-ups → breaks discipline filter

## Output

- Applied within 48h → Stage 1
- Not applied → Ignore for current batch; keep for future

**Strategic note:** This stage tests seriousness before effort is invested. "If they don’t act in 48 hours, they won’t perform in job."
