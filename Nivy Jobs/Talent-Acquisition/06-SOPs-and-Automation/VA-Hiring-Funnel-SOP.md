# NIVY VA HIRING FUNNEL – SOP

Defines EXACTLY what team members must do at each stage.

## CORE OPERATING RULES

- Deadlines STRICT (no manual extension)
- No follow-ups in hiring stages
- Email = control system
- WhatsApp = notification only (except training funnel)
- Every action tracked in Google Sheet

## TEAM ROLES

1. **Funnel Manager** — Controls pipeline, batch movement, metrics
2. **Operations Executive** — Sends emails, updates sheet, moves stages
3. **Evaluator** — Test/task evaluation, interviews
4. **Training Manager** — Free training, performance, retention/removal

---

## STAGE 0: JOB AWARENESS

- Prepare list (50 per batch)
- Send JD Email (Email 1) with details, 48h deadline, test link, WhatsApp links
- Track send time + deadline

## STAGE 1: APPLICATION + TEST

- Google Form + auto scoring
- Monitor submissions
- Auto confirmation email
- PASS → Stage 2 | FAIL/NO SUBMISSION → Paid Training Path

## STAGE 2: TRIAL TASK

- Send Task Email (24h deadline)
- Minimal WhatsApp reminder optional
- Evaluator checks accuracy, execution, reporting
- PASS → Stage 3 | FAIL → Paid Training Path

## STAGE 3: INTERVIEW

- Send Interview Email + Calendly
- Book within 24h, attend within 48h
- Evaluate communication, thinking, confidence
- PASS → Stage 4 | FAIL → Paid Training Path

## STAGE 4: ORIENTATION

- Send details, conduct session
- Check attendance + seriousness
- ATTENDED → Internal Selection | ABSENT → Paid Training (optional)

## INTERNAL: SELECTION + AGREEMENT

- Select top 10
- Send offer + agreement (collect signed within 24h)
- SIGNED → Free Training | NOT SIGNED → Replace

## STAGE 5: FREE TRAINING (3 MONTHS)

- Assign daily work, monitor KPIs, attendance
- HIGH → Final Hiring | LOW → Paid Training / Exit

## STAGE 6: FINAL EVALUATION

- Final review / task / interview
- PASS → Job Offer | FAIL → Paid Training Path

---

## PAID TRAINING SOP (CONVERSION)

**Triggers:** Stage 1/2/3 fail, Stage 5 low performance

**Email sequence (auto):** Day 1 Feedback → Day 2 Course → Day 3 Proof → Day 4 Urgency → Day 5 Final Call

**WhatsApp:** Testimonials, queries, conversion push

**Outcome:** PURCHASE → Paid Training Batch | NO → Nurture Pool

---

## TRACKING (MANDATORY)

Sheet columns: Name | Email | Stage | Status (Pass/Fail) | Deadline | Source | Notes

## DAILY OPS CHECKLIST

- New applications
- Deadlines expiring
- Pending emails
- Update sheet
- Evaluate tasks/interviews
- Monitor training batch

**Principle:** Hiring Funnel = Filtering | Paid Training = Monetization + Talent Upgrade | No candidate wasted.
