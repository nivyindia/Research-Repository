# Nivy Master Roadmap — Owner View

## North Star

**International project revenue with a repeatable acquisition and delivery system.**

### Stage 1 — Acquire capacity
- Recruit BDE / sales-acquisition capacity
- Finalize screening → interview → paid test → selection → onboarding
- Give the selected resource defined channels, ICPs, scripts and tracking

### Stage 2 — Activate acquisition
- International CPA/tax/bookkeeping
- Digital marketing
- Software/web/app development
- AI automation / agentic AI
- Virtual assistant services
- Upwork/Fiverr
- Direct email/outreach
- Partnerships/referrals

### Stage 3 — Convert
Lead → qualification → discovery → proposal → follow-up → close → onboarding.

### Stage 4 — Deliver and retain
Client → project → delivery → QC → reporting → repeat work / referral.

### Stage 5 — Systemize
CRM + email + messaging + calendar + project management + accounting + HR + automation.

### Stage 6 — Scale
Talent pipeline → team leads → partner network → additional markets/channels → AIOS-assisted operations.

## Parallel enabling tracks

- Nivy Advisory service packaging
- Nivy Next service packaging
- Minimum viable digital presence
- Talent pipeline
- AIOS / automation research and implementation
- Academy / Jobs / partner ecosystem

## Sequencing rule

Revenue-generating execution takes precedence over building infrastructure that does not yet support a concrete revenue or delivery need.
