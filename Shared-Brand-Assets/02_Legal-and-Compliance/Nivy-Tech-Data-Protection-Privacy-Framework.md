# Nivy Tech Data Protection & Privacy Framework

**Policy Name:** Nivy Tech Data Protection & Privacy Framework  
**Effective Date:** Aug 26, 2025  
**Version:** 3.4  
**Canonical location:** Shared-Brand-Assets/02_Legal-and-Compliance/ (Batch 1)

**1.1 Purpose**  
Nivy Tech is committed to protecting the privacy and security of all personal data we process. This framework formalizes procedures for complying with global data protection laws including GDPR and CCPA. It applies to all personal data processed by Nivy Tech.

**1.2 Scope**  
Applies to all employees, contractors, and sub-processors acting on behalf of Nivy Tech.

### Policy 1: Data Classification & Inventory  
Maintain a Record of Processing Activities (ROPA). DPO responsible; reviewed quarterly.

### Policy 2: Lawful Basis for Processing  
Contract, Legitimate Interests, Consent, Legal Obligation — documented in ROPA. LIA where Legitimate Interests; explicit consent where required.

### Policy 3: Data Subject Rights Procedure  
Requests via privacy@nivytech.com; verify identity; fulfill within one month; document in tracker.

### Policy 4: Data Protection by Design and by Default  
DPIA mandatory for high-risk processing; DPO approval before start.

### Policy 5: Data Breach Response Protocol  
Notify DPO immediately → contain → assess → notify authority within 72h if required → document & review.

### Policy 6: Third-Party Vendor & Sub-Processor Management  
Due diligence + written DPA (GDPR Art. 28) before data sharing.

### Policy 7: Data Retention and Erasure  
Retention periods in ROPA; secure deletion after expiry.

### Policy 8: Employee Training and Confidentiality  
Mandatory training on hire and annually; need-to-know access.
