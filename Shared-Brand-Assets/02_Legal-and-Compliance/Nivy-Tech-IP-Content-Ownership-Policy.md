# Nivy Tech Intellectual Property & Content Ownership Policy

**Effective Date:** 26 August 2025  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Canonical location:** Shared-Brand-Assets/02_Legal-and-Compliance/ (Batch 1)

### 1. Purpose  
Defines ownership, usage, and protection of IP created or used by Nivy Tech.

### 2–4. Ownership  
- **Pre-Existing IP:** Nivy Tech retains (strategies, tools, templates). Client retains their pre-existing IP.  
- **Project IP (Full Delivery):** Upon full payment, Foreground IP assigned to client.  
- **Limited License Model:** Nivy may retain ownership and grant non-exclusive license.

### 5. Background IP  
Reusable frameworks/templates licensed for project use; not for resale outside scope.

### 6. Third-Party IP  
Stock/fonts/software governed by their licenses; client must comply.

### 7–9. Moral rights, infringement warranty, employee/contractor IP  
Project IP does not infringe third-party rights; employee/contractor IP belongs to Nivy Tech within engagement scope.
