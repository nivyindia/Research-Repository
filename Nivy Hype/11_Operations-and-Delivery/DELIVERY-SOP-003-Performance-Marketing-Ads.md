# 📋 DELIVERY SOP-003 — Performance Marketing (Ads Management)

> **SOP ID:** DELIVERY-SOP-003  
> **Package:** Performance Marketing Starter + Performance Marketing Growth  
> **Category:** Paid Ads  
> **Delivery Team:** Junior VA (Starter) / Junior–Senior VA (Growth)  
> **Cycle:** One-time setup (Starter) + Monthly recurring (Growth)  
> **Owner:** Supervisor  
> **Status:** ✅ Active — 2026-04-30

---

## 🎯 PURPOSE

This SOP governs the **setup and ongoing management** of paid advertising campaigns. Ads are the highest-ROI service — but also highest-risk if done wrong. Every VA managing ads must follow this SOP exactly. No campaigns go live without Supervisor sign-off.

> ⚠️ **Hard rule:** No ad account gets touched without written client authorisation. No budget gets spent without Supervisor approval. No campaign goes live without QC sign-off.

| Component | Starter L1 (One-Time) | Growth L2 (Monthly) |

---

## 🗓️ STARTER L1 — ADS SETUP (7–10 Days)

### Day 1–2 — Client Onboarding & Access
- [ ] Send Ads Onboarding Form
- [ ] Request Admin access to Facebook Business Manager
- [ ] Verify pixel is installed
- [ ] Confirm campaign objective

### Day 3–4 — Audience Research & Campaign Structure
- [ ] Build 2–3 audience sets
- [ ] Define campaign structure: 1 Campaign → 2 Ad Sets → 2 Ads per Ad Set
- [ ] Submit to Supervisor for approval before creating in Ads Manager

### Day 5–6 — Ad Creative Production
- [ ] Write 2 ad copy variations (Problem → Solution → Proof → CTA)
- [ ] Design 2 ad visuals in Canva (1080×1080 + 1080×1920)
- [ ] Submit creatives to Supervisor for QC

### Day 7–8 — Campaign Build & Pre-Launch QC
- [ ] Build campaign in Facebook Ads Manager
- [ ] Pre-launch checklist (pixel, URL, budget approval, Supervisor sign-off)
- [ ] Launch campaign
- [ ] Monitor first 6 hours

### Day 9–10 — Handover
- [ ] Send client Campaign Launch Summary

---

## 🔄 GROWTH L2 — MONTHLY ADS MANAGEMENT

### WEEK 1 — REVIEW & PLANNING
- [ ] Pull last month’s performance data
- [ ] Identify winners and losers
- [ ] Pause underperforming ads
- [ ] Submit strategy to Supervisor

### WEEK 2 — NEW CREATIVE PRODUCTION
- [ ] Produce 4 new ad variations
- [ ] A/B test rule: change ONE variable at a time
- [ ] Submit to Supervisor for QC

### WEEK 3–4 — OPTIMISATION & REPORTING
- [ ] Daily monitoring (spend, disapprovals, frequency)
- [ ] Weekly report every Friday
- [ ] Monthly report by Day 28

---

## ✅ QUALITY CONTROL CHECKLIST (Supervisor)

**Before any campaign goes live:**
- [ ] Client written budget approval
- [ ] Pixel confirmed firing
- [ ] Ad copy: no prohibited words
- [ ] Ad visual: text under 20%
- [ ] Destination URL working
- [ ] Supervisor sign-off received

---

## ⚠️ ESCALATION RULES

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Ad disapproved | Fix creative/copy, resubmit. Flag if rejected twice |
| CPA exceeds target by 50%+ for 7 days | Pause campaign. Flag to Supervisor |
| Accidental overspend | Pause all ads. Notify Supervisor immediately |
