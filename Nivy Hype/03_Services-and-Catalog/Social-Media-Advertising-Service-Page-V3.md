# 4 · Is This Right For You?

**Strong fit if:**

- You want to scale lead generation or sales through paid social channels
- You have a proven offer and a landing page or funnel that converts
- You’re ready to invest $2,000+ per month in ad spend
- You want full-funnel strategy: cold audience, warm retargeting, and retention
- You’ve run paid social before but haven’t seen consistent, scalable returns

**Not the right fit if:**

- Your offer or funnel isn’t converting yet — ads will amplify the problem, not fix it
- You have less than $1,000/month in ad budget
- You want guaranteed CPL numbers before campaign launch — real performance requires testing
