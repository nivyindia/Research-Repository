> **Package:** SMM Starter + SMM Growth

> **Delivery Team:** Fresher VA (Starter) / Junior VA (Growth)

## 🎯 PURPOSE

This SOP governs the **end-to-end delivery** of the Social Media Management package for every active client. Every VA delivering SMM must follow this SOP without deviation. Supervisors use this to QC output.

## 🗓️ MONTHLY DELIVERY CYCLE

### WEEK 1 — ONBOARDING & PLANNING (Day 1–5)

**Day 1 — Client Onboarding (New Clients Only)**
- [ ] Send Client Onboarding Form (collect: brand tone, target audience, content pillars, colours, do-not-post list, login credentials)
- [ ] Confirm platform access: Instagram, Facebook Business Manager, Meta Business Suite
- [ ] Add client to internal tracking sheet
- [ ] Confirm content approval process with client

**Day 2–3 — Content Planning**
- [ ] Create monthly content calendar (4 content pillars)
- [ ] Content pillars: Educational, Promotional, Behind-the-Scenes, Engagement/CTA
- [ ] Submit calendar to Supervisor for review before sending to client

**Day 4–5 — Client Calendar Approval**
- [ ] Send content calendar to client for approval
- [ ] Chase once at 24 hrs if no reply
- [ ] If no reply at 48 hrs → escalate to Supervisor

### WEEK 2–3 — CONTENT PRODUCTION (Day 6–21)

**Graphic Post Creation**
- [ ] Use approved brand kit
- [ ] Tool: Canva
- [ ] Each post: graphic + caption + 3–5 hashtags
- [ ] Caption structure: Hook → Value/Story → CTA

**Reel Creation**
- [ ] Script reel (15–30 sec) using Hook → Value → CTA format
- [ ] Edit reel: captions ON, trending audio, transitions
- [ ] Export at 1080×1920 (9:16)

**Content Approval**
- [ ] Bundle all posts for the week into one approval folder
- [ ] Max 1 round of revisions per post

### WEEK 2–4 — SCHEDULING & PUBLISHING

- [ ] Use Meta Business Suite to schedule approved posts
- [ ] Never publish without client approval
- [ ] Daily engagement: reply comments within 4 hrs, DMs within 2 hrs

### WEEK 4 — REPORTING & RENEWAL (Day 25–30)

- [ ] Pull monthly data from Meta Business Suite
- [ ] Fill Monthly Report Template
- [ ] Submit to Supervisor for QC
- [ ] Prepare next month's content calendar draft

---

## ✅ QUALITY CONTROL CHECKLIST (Supervisor)

- [ ] Brand colours and fonts match client brand kit
- [ ] No spelling or grammar errors
- [ ] CTA present in every post
- [ ] Reels: captions visible, audio trending, ratio correct
- [ ] Calendar submitted to client on Day 4
- [ ] Approval confirmed before any post is scheduled

---

## ⚠️ ESCALATION RULES

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Client not approving calendar after 48 hrs | Escalate to Supervisor |
| Client requesting work outside package | Do NOT deliver. Flag to Supervisor |
| Negative comment / PR issue | Do NOT respond. Screenshot + escalate within 1 hr |
| Platform access lost | Stop all work. Notify Supervisor within 30 min |
