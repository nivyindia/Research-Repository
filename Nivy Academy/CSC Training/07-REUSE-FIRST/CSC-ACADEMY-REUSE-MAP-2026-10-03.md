# CSC Academy Reuse Map — 2026-10-03

## Purpose

Identify existing CSC Academy training/service material that can be reused before Nivy creates new CSC training content.

## Verified reusable material

| Existing resource | What Nivy can reuse | Nivy action |
|---|---|---|
| CSC Academy Services List | Existing service names, department/owner mapping, education/skill/academy catalogue | Link/reference; verify current availability before operator training |
| VLE Training | VLE orientation, training ecosystem and delivery approach | Use as primary orientation reference |
| Education Services | Existing education-service structure and programme categories | Reuse structure; do not rebuild equivalent catalogue |
| Skill Course catalogue | Existing course descriptions and learning topics | Link/adapt where permitted |
| Telecentre Entrepreneur Course (TEC) material | Fundamentals of Telecentre, Entrepreneur/Entrepreneurship, G2C/B2C, CSC Scheme, Digital Seva Portal, Digital Securities/Wellness, Finance & Accounting, GST, Soft Skills, Business Communication | Use as the base for VLE foundation training |
| IIBF BC/BF exam training material | Student registration, exam-centre workflow, service marketing, pricing/commission and FAQ structure | Reuse as reference for BC/BF training; verify current process |
| CSC Academy skill FAQ | Self-paced learning, exam/assessment concepts and course delivery model | Reuse as orientation; verify current fee/commission before operational use |
| CSC Academy PwD training guidance | Centre requirements, attendance, document collection and record-keeping examples | Reuse only where the current scheme remains applicable |

## High-priority finding

The TEC material already covers several subjects we were preparing to build ourselves: telecentre fundamentals, entrepreneurship, G2C/B2C, CSC scheme, Digital Seva Portal, digital security/wellness, finance/accounting, GST and business communication.

Therefore these should **not be independently rebuilt as first-party Nivy lessons**. Instead:

**Existing official material → link/reference → add Nivy practical checklist only where needed → assess candidate → record provenance.**

## Important verification rules

- Existing material is not automatically current merely because it is on an official domain.
- Current portal/UI, fee, commission, eligibility and authorization must be rechecked before operational training.
- Do not expose or reproduce real Aadhaar/OTP/password/biometric credentials in training material.
- Public availability does not grant permission to republish copyrighted course content. Prefer links, summaries, references and permitted adaptation.
