## Important boundary

This audit does **not** establish that every CSC service has an existing official training course. Absence from this audit means only that a reusable training source was not verified in this pass.

For service-level operational training, verify:
1. exact current service name
2. current Digital Seva/department portal
3. CSC/VLE authorization
4. eligibility
5. required documents
6. authentication method
7. current fee/commission where applicable
8. workflow and payment
9. receipt/status/correction
10. escalation path
11. current training reference
12. last verification date

## Build policy

Do not create duplicate theory, FAQ, course catalogues or partner curricula where an official reusable source exists.

Create Nivy-owned material only for:
- state-specific practical SOPs
- current operator checklists
- QC/error handling
- synthetic practice cases
- assessment/practical tests
- escalation matrices
- integration instructions
- gaps not covered by official material
