# CSC Research — Official Sources

## Government baseline

### PIB — MoU with CSC e-Governance Service
https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2039070&lang=2&reg=48

Published: 30 July 2024.

Key finding: Government states that CSCs provide more than 300 e-services and lists:
- PM welfare schemes
- Central government services
- State government services
- Financial inclusion
- Aadhaar services
- Agricultural services
- E-mobility/smart products
- B2C services
- Other services

### PIB — Common Services Centres: 16 years of digital empowerment
https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2144094&lang=2&reg=48

Published: 16 July 2025.

Key finding: Provides a broader public list covering Aadhaar, PAN, passport, certificates, DigiLocker, utilities, banking, DigiPay, insurance, pensions, loans, education, health, agriculture, legal, e-commerce, travel and other services.

### PIB — CSC network/current status
https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2290995&lang=2&reg=48

Published: 29 July 2026.

Use for current CSC network context.

### PIB — CSC network in Bihar/current national counts
https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2295103&lang=2&reg=48

Published: 5 August 2026.

Key finding: As of 30 June 2026, 4,07,122 rural CSCs were reported as functional at Gram Panchayat level nationally. The release also states that prescribed service rate lists are mandatory for CSCs to display.

## Research rule

Government sources establish the service families and official context. They should be combined with current Digital Seva Portal/service-specific official documentation when creating a service SOP.

### Reuse rule
Official material is the preferred source of truth. Existing practical videos are supplementary. Do not copy or republish copyrighted material merely because it is publicly accessible; link to it, summarize it, or adapt only where permitted. Preserve provenance and licensing information.
