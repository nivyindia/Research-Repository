## Core operator skills that apply across almost every CSC service

1. Customer identification and eligibility check.
2. Document scanning, PDF creation, compression, naming and upload.
3. Accurate form filling and cross-checking against source documents.
4. OTP/consent handling without recording or exposing credentials.
5. Payment, receipt and transaction-reference verification.
6. Status tracking and customer communication.
7. Failed transaction, refund and escalation handling.
8. Data privacy and safe deletion/retention practices.
9. Basic printer/scanner/biometric/device troubleshooting where authorized.
10. Daily transaction register and reconciliation.
11. Service-rate display and customer grievance process.
12. Knowing when a service requires separate authorization, certification, agent ID or state-specific access.
