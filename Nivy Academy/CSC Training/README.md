# CSC Training & Services Research

Purpose: Build a reusable, service-wise training library for a CSC/VLE operator.

## Repository position

`Nivy Academy/CSC Training/` is the training/learning layer. Hiring, screening, paid tests and candidate progression remain under `Nivy Talent System/`.

## Scope

Government sources describe CSCs as delivering **300+ e-services**. The exact service menu available to an individual VLE can vary by activation, authorization, state/UT, partner, and current Digital Seva Portal configuration.

This research therefore uses three layers:

1. **Core National Catalogue** — major CSC service families and commonly exposed services.
2. **Service-Level Training Matrix** — what the operator must learn, documents/process, portal, and training video.
3. **Expansion Queue** — state/partner/portal-specific services to be added only after verified discovery.

## Folder structure

- `01-MASTER-CATALOGUE/` — service inventory and classification
- `02-TRAINING-MATRIX/` — service-wise SOP/training requirements
- `03-YOUTUBE-TRAINING/` — verified videos and YouTube search fallbacks
- `04-SOURCES/` — official/reference research
- `05-IMPLEMENTATION/` — curriculum, practical tests and progress tracker

## Source rule

Prefer:
1. Official CSC/SPV/government documentation
2. Official/partner training material
3. Recent, practical YouTube tutorials
4. YouTube search only when a suitable video could not be reliably verified

Do not treat a third-party YouTube video as an official instruction. Portal screens and procedures can change.

## Current research baseline

PIB's 2024 CSC/PACS release explicitly describes 300+ CSC e-services and lists welfare schemes, central government services, state government services, financial inclusion, Aadhaar, agriculture, e-mobility, B2C and other services.

PIB's July 2025 CSC overview provides a broad service list including Aadhaar, PAN, passport, certificates, DigiLocker, utilities, banking, DigiPay/AEPS, insurance, pensions, loans, education, health, agriculture, e-commerce, legal services, travel and other services.

## Next expansion

The catalogue should be expanded from the national service families into individual services only when a current official/credible source identifies the service. This avoids inventing a fake "300-service list" from unofficial lists.
