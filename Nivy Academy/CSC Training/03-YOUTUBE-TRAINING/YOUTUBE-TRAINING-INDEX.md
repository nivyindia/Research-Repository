## Service-specific YouTube search queue

When no individual video was reliably verified, use the service-specific search URL in the master catalogue and select a recent, practical tutorial.

Search rules:
1. Prefer recent videos.
2. Prefer videos showing the actual portal screen and full workflow.
3. Check the video date and comments for portal changes.
4. Do not assume a third-party tutorial is an official CSC instruction.
5. For Aadhaar, banking, insurance, legal, tax and other regulated workflows, cross-check against the relevant official portal/rules.
6. Replace a search URL with a specific video URL after verification.
