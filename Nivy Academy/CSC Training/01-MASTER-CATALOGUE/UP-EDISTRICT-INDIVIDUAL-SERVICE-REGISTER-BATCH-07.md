# UP Individual Service Register — Batch 07

Batch 07 consolidates additional UP service evidence and establishes canonical mapping rules.

## Newly captured service groups

- Rojgaar Sangam: job-seeker registration, employer registration, government-job search.
- Agriculture: complaint registration, complaint status, millet processing and packing establishment registration, farmer information services.
- Higher education: result hold/cancellation and marksheet/certificate correction.
- Electricity/agriculture: private tube-well connection.
- Municipal/local: business licences and public-address permission.

## Mapping rules

1. One canonical Service ID per actual service.
2. An indexed portal title is not automatically an e-District row.
3. Municipal and local-body services remain separate until official mapping is verified.
4. Information-only services are not treated as operator-deliverable services.
5. Training Ready requires authorization, current workflow, documents, fee/SLA where applicable, practical task, QC and verification date.

## Current catalogue distinction

The current NGSP UP index and the UP Jan Seva/e-District service inventory are maintained as separate datasets. The exact 353-row operator catalogue is not frozen until the current official operator menu is extracted.
