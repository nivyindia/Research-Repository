## Research rule
A row is **Training Ready** only after the exact current official service identity, department/provider, availability, authorization, eligibility, documents, authentication, fee where applicable, workflow, payment/receipt, correction/status process, escalation path and current training reference have been verified.

No real Aadhaar/PAN/bank credentials, OTPs, passwords, biometrics or private keys are to be stored in training material.
