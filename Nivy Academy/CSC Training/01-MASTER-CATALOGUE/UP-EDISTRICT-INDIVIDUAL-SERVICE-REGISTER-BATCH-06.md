## Catalogue mapping
Required mapping values: Confirmed e-District/Jan Seva; Confirmed UP department portal; Municipal/local body; University/education portal; Partner/authorized service; Information-only; Unmapped.

## Training priority
After authorization is confirmed, prioritize scholarship/fee reimbursement, Kanya Sumangla, Shadi Anudan, land records, certificates, PDS, pension, employment and local-body services. Specialized university, ex-servicemen and regulated licensing services remain separate modules until operator authorization is verified.

## Data-quality rule
An NGSP listing alone does not prove CSC/Jan Seva submission authorization. Verify authorization, current workflow, fee, SLA and documents before Training Ready.
