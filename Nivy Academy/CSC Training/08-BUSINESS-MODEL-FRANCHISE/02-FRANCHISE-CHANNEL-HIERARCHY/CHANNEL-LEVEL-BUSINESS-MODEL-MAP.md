# CSC-Style Channel-Level Business Model Map

## Historical/reference architecture

### Central / SPV
- Platform/ecosystem governance
- Service aggregation
- Partner/provider relationships
- Standards, technology and monitoring

### State level
Historical CSC materials describe a State Designated Agency role and state-level implementation/policy support. Current legal/operating arrangements must be verified separately for each programme/state.

### District / network level
Historical CSC materials describe Service Centre Agencies as organisations that build, operate and manage VLE networks. Modern programmes may use different entities and contracts.

### VLE / franchisee level
- Physical/local service centre
- Citizen acquisition and service delivery
- Document/application assistance
- Payments and receipts
- Local marketing
- Customer support
- Compliance with programme/provider rules

## Benchmark channel variants to research

1. Franchisee/VLE model
2. District distributor/channel partner
3. State channel partner/master franchise
4. Service-specific authorised agent
5. Education/academy centre
6. Retail/e-commerce distributor
7. Banking BC/CSP
8. Government-service facilitation centre
9. Hybrid digital + physical service centre

## Critical distinction

A CSC-style commercial model should NOT be presented as an official CSC franchise unless the relevant authority has actually authorised that relationship. Nivy's eventual programme must have its own brand, agreement, pricing, territory rules and compliance framework unless it is operating under an actual CSC/partner contract.
