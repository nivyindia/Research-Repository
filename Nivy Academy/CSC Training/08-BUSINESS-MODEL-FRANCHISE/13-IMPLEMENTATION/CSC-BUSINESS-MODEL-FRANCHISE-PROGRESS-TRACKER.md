## Definition of done

The business-model library is complete only when:
- channel levels are documented;
- each service has authorization requirements;
- economics are source/date tagged;
- public benchmarks are linked with provenance;
- protected content is not republished without permission;
- original Nivy materials are clearly separated from benchmark material;
- franchise/distributor contracts receive legal review before use.
