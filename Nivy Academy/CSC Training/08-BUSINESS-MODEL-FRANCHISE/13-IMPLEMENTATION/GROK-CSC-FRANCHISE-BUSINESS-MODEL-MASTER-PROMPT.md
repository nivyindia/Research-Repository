## Copyright and legal rule

Do NOT make a "same-to-same" copy of copyrighted brochures, proposals, websites, manuals, graphics, logos, trademarks, proprietary wording or designs merely because they are publicly accessible.

Instead:
- preserve the source URL;
- extract factual business structure;
- summarise the content;
- record the material's architecture;
- record what is reusable under its licence;
- identify what must be recreated originally;
- create original Nivy versions.

If an explicit licence permits reuse, record the licence and comply with it.

Never imply that Nivy is CSC or an authorised CSC franchise unless actual authorisation exists.

## What to research

### 1. Business model
Find how the CSC ecosystem creates value:
- who owns/controls the platform;
- who appoints whom;
- who pays whom;
- who owns the customer relationship;
- who provides services;
- who bears compliance risk;
- who provides technology;
- who provides training;
- how support works;
- how revenue is shared;
- how disputes are handled.

Separate historical CSC architecture from current operating arrangements.

### 2. Channel hierarchy
Build a comparison of:
- central
- state
- district
- block/cluster
- VLE/franchisee
- service-specific agent
- distributor/retailer

For each, capture:
- appointment mechanism
- territory
- exclusivity
- investment
- infrastructure
- deposit/security if any
- targets
- recurring fees
- revenue share
- support
- training
- audit
- termination
- replacement
- reporting

Do not invent numbers.

### 3. Service portfolio
Build a canonical service matrix:
Service | Provider | Category | Authorization | Channel level | Revenue type | Customer | Workflow source | Training source | Current verification date.

Reuse the existing CSC service research in:
`Nivy Academy/CSC Training/`

### 4. Economics
Research actual published figures where available:
- initial investment
- equipment
- setup
- registration
- security/deposit
- software
- monthly expenses
- commission
- transaction economics
- service fees
- distributor margin
- franchise fee
- royalty
- support fee
- marketing fee
- break-even
- payback claims

Every figure must have:
- source
- date
- geography
- programme/version
- whether official or third-party.

Never convert an old figure into a current price.
