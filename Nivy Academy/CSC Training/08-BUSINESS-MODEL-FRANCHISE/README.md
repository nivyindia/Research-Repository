# CSC Business Model, Franchise & Distribution Research

## Purpose
Build a reusable research and implementation library for a CSC-style service-centre business that can operate at VLE/franchisee, district/distributor, state/channel-partner and central levels.

## Important reuse rule
REUSE → ADAPT → INTEGRATE → BUILD ONLY WHAT IS MISSING.

Publicly available material is not automatically licensed for verbatim republication. This repository therefore stores source links, factual notes, structures, screenshots/references where legally permitted, and original Nivy adaptations. Do not copy protected brochures, proposals, manuals, logos, trademarks, or proprietary text/images verbatim unless permission/licence permits it.

## Folder map

- 01-MARKET-AND-MODEL-RESEARCH — CSC ecosystem, history, operating models, economics, stakeholder structure.
- 02-FRANCHISE-CHANNEL-HIERARCHY — VLE/franchisee, district, state and central/channel-partner structures; eligibility and responsibilities.
- 03-SERVICE-PORTFOLIO — services, categories, provider/authorization, revenue opportunities.
- 04-UNIT-ECONOMICS — investment, recurring costs, commissions, revenue assumptions, break-even models; every figure must be dated/source-tagged.
- 05-FRANCHISE-PROGRAM-DESIGN — original Nivy franchise/distributor programme design derived from research.
- 06-OPERATIONS-AND-TRAINING — onboarding, training, SOP, QC, support, audits.
- 07-MARKETING-MATERIAL-BENCHMARKS — brochures, flyers, websites, social posts, ad structures, sales decks; links/provenance and permitted reuse only.
- 08-SALES-PROPOSALS-AND-PITCHES — public proposal/deck benchmarks and original Nivy templates.
- 09-LEGAL-COMPLIANCE — agreements, disclaimers, authorization boundaries, data protection, branding/trademark checks.
- 10-TECH-AND-SYSTEMS — portal, CRM, KYC, payments, ticketing, reporting and franchise dashboards.
- 11-STATE-AND-DISTRICT-MODELS — state-specific partner/CSC structures and local government ecosystem research.
- 12-SOURCE-REGISTER — canonical source register and provenance.
- 13-IMPLEMENTATION — tracker, gap register, decision log and master execution prompt.

## Research standard
Primary official sources first; dated evidence; separate current facts from historical models; never treat third-party franchise claims as official CSC policy.
