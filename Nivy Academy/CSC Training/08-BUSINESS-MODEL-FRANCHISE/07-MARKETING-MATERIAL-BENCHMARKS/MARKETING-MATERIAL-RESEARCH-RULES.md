# Marketing Material Benchmark Research Rules

Research and catalogue:
- franchise brochures
- franchise landing pages
- distributor presentations
- VLE onboarding pages
- service catalogues
- posters/flyers
- WhatsApp creatives
- social media campaigns
- video scripts
- FAQs
- testimonials/case studies
- partner decks
- lead forms
- pricing/offer pages

For each benchmark capture:
1. Brand/company
2. Material type
3. URL
4. Date/access date
5. Target audience
6. Offer structure
7. Claims made
8. CTA
9. Information architecture
10. Visual/creative pattern
11. What can legally be reused
12. What must be recreated originally
13. Nivy adaptation notes

Never reproduce third-party copyrighted brochure copy/design as if it were Nivy material. Use it as a benchmark and create original copy/design.
