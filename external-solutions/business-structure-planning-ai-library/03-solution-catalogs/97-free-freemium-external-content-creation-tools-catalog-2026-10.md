## 6. End-to-end free/freemium starter stack (recommended order)

1. **Research & scripts:** ChatGPT / Claude / Perplexity + vidIQ free
2. **Voice:** ElevenLabs free or CapCut TTS
3. **Video edit & Shorts:** CapCut (primary) + OpusClip for clipping
4. **Thumbnails:** Canva free + Leonardo.ai
5. **SEO & upload:** TubeBuddy / vidIQ free + YouTube Studio scheduler
6. **Avatars (optional):** HeyGen free tier or CapCut talking-avatar features

This stack can produce publishable YouTube + Shorts content at $0 for low-to-medium volume.

---

## 7. Notes & caveats

- Free tiers change frequently — always re-check character/credit/watermark limits before a production batch.
- Commercial rights: ElevenLabs free tier usually forbids commercial use; upgrade or switch to tools that explicitly allow it (TTSMaker, CapCut, Canva free for many cases).
- Watermarks: VEED, some InVideo/Pictory free exports, HeyGen free, Descript free often carry watermarks.
- Pair these with the open-source agents in catalog 23 for full local/self-hosted control when free SaaS limits are hit.
