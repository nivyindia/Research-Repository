## Interpretation and status controls

This matrix normalizes the existing 75 stable gap IDs; it does not create a competing gap register. Existing gap IDs, source states, and priority labels are preserved from the source gap analysis. Candidate paths are internal mapping anchors, not proof that an asset is reusable or integrated.

- **Coverage state** is copied from the source gap analysis, not upgraded by this wave.
- **Candidate source(s)** are likely evidence locations to inspect; exact implementation behavior, license, dependencies and runtime are not inferred from a catalog entry.
- **Acceptance evidence** must be collected before a status can be changed to inspected, adapted, integrated, tested or accepted.
- Where multiple source files exist, compare versions/duplicates and preserve history rather than deleting one.
