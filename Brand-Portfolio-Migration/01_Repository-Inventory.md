## Cross-brand findings from the first audit pass

1. **The existing Nivy Next sales deck describes all four future service areas together.** It includes digital marketing, AI/automation, web/app development, graphic design, video editing and virtual assistance. This is useful as a historical umbrella deck but should not automatically become the approved Next-only sales deck.
2. **The social media package mixes services.** Creative production may belong to Nivy Crafts; campaign management and paid ads belong to Nivy Hype. Any split must preserve package economics, scope and dependencies.
3. **Pricing has multiple repository copies.** The SEO package content appears in both Nivy Next and Notion; social media package content appears in multiple locations. No canonical copy or live approval status is established yet.
4. **Brand-guideline files are inconsistent in completeness and status.** At least one Nivy Next guideline file is only a heading, while the Nivy Digital guideline explicitly leaves some identity choices unconfirmed. Avoid declaring any one of these the definitive guide before comparison.
5. **Some sales claims require evidence review.** The sales deck and video page contain quantitative claims/case examples. Verify their source, permission and substantiation before reuse in public-facing assets.
