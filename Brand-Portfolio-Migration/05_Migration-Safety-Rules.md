# Migration Safety Rules

1. **No unapproved moves:** Do not move, rename, delete or overwrite existing business documents before the source-to-destination batch is reviewed and approved.
2. **Preserve provenance:** Record original path, original link, destination, action and resulting commit.
3. **Inspect before classification:** File names and search snippets are leads, not sufficient evidence.
4. **No premature deletion:** Retain source copies until migration and link verification are complete. Archive candidates require explicit approval.
5. **Handle duplicates carefully:** Similar titles do not prove duplicates. Compare actual content and versions.
6. **Protect pricing and internal material:** Separate public prices from draft, custom proposal prices and internal costing. Do not expose confidential material in public-facing folders.
7. **Mixed-brand content:** Preserve the original; create a brand-specific derivative only when the content can be separated cleanly and the derivative is clearly labelled.
8. **Shared assets:** Maintain one canonical shared copy where practical; reference it from brand-specific documents instead of silently copying multiple editable versions.
9. **External references:** Leave third-party research/reference files in their research location unless there is a specific reason and permission to move them.
10. **Broken links:** After each batch, update internal references and verify relevant paths.
11. **Brand naming:** Use existing `Nivy Crafts` spelling for now; do not rename to `Nivy Craft` without owner confirmation.
12. **Approval gate:** The migration register must show approval before an existing asset is changed.
13. **Traceability:** Every migrated item should have a commit SHA or commit URL recorded when available.
14. **Exceptions:** Ambiguous ownership, conflicting claims, missing source files, or pricing inconsistencies remain `Review Required`; do not guess.
15. **Separate SEO phase:** Do not begin the final keyword/page implementation plan until the actual services, approved offers and existing page assets have been audited.
