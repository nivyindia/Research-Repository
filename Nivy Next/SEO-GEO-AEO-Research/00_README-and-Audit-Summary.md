## Key findings from the prepared documents
1. **The source material is substantial but inconsistent in scope/counts.** One plan refers to 29 current pages and 43+ after additions; another inventory calls itself 28 pages but lists 29 rows. Reconcile the canonical page register before editing copy.
2. **Homepage positioning is broad.** “Scale Smarter. Grow Faster. Go Global.” is memorable but does not directly name the highest-value services or ICP. The copy also claims seven service verticals, while the master plan refers to 18 services. Standardize this.
3. **The prepared copy targets too many service categories at once.** SEO, PPC, social, content, AI automation, web/app development, design, video and virtual assistance can dilute topical focus and sales positioning if all are given equal prominence.
4. **SEO page includes unsupported proof-risk items.** Statistics, percentage improvements, client outcomes, testimonials, “trusted by” statements, and case studies must be verified against evidence before publishing. Remove or label as internal examples until verified.
5. **Market list is inconsistent.** Older copy prioritizes US, UK, Canada, Australia and UAE; the current research brief expands to Europe and New Zealand. UAE should remain a secondary market unless sales evidence says otherwise.
6. **GEO/AEO is not yet a clearly differentiated, measurable offer.** Create a dedicated service page and deliverables that describe AI visibility baseline, prompt-set tracking, entity consistency, crawl/index accessibility, structured data, answer-ready content, source corroboration and monthly reporting—without promising guaranteed citations.
7. **Keyword list is not yet evidence-based.** Existing metadata lists generic terms such as “AI digital agency” and “international digital agency”; these should not be treated as validated target keywords until volume, SERP intent, competition, geography and commercial fit are checked.
8. **Trust and conversion gaps matter as much as keyword use.** Real case studies, transparent scope, real testimonials, clear pricing/engagement terms, founder/team credentials, response expectations and a specific CTA should be addressed before scaling content.

## Recommended strategic focus
**Primary wedge to test:** SEO + AI Search Visibility (GEO/AEO) for B2B service businesses and selected growth-stage SMEs. Keep AI automation and web development as adjacent cross-sells, not equal headline priorities, unless the existing sales pipeline shows they are stronger.

**ICP hypotheses to validate:** small-to-mid-sized B2B service companies, professional services, recruitment, specialist agencies, and selected SaaS/e-commerce firms with a live website, a clear commercial offer, weak organic/AI visibility, and a decision-maker accessible to outbound sales.

## Research status rules
- **Proposed** = reasoned hypothesis; not yet validated by keyword data.
- **Validated** = checked against a current SERP and reliable keyword data for a named country.
- **Approved** = selected after commercial fit, delivery capability and sales evidence are reviewed.
- Never invent search volumes, keyword difficulty, competitor traffic, customer results or client proof.
