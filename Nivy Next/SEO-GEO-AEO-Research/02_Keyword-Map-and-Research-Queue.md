## Country and language notes
- **US:** “SEO agency”, “AI search optimization”, “GEO services”; US spelling “optimization”.
- **UK:** “SEO agency”, “generative engine optimisation” and “optimisation” spelling variants; test SERP differences before deciding copy.
- **Canada:** test both Canadian/US spelling variants; emphasize fit, communication and clear scope, not generic country claims.
- **Australia / New Zealand:** test “SEO agency”, “SEO services”, “AI search optimisation”; do not create location pages without distinct value.
- **Europe:** do not treat Europe as one market. Start with English-language opportunities in the UK, Ireland, Netherlands and other English-compatible segments only after country-specific SERP and language research. Local-language pages require native-quality content and support.
- Avoid country-modified pages that merely swap country names. Add unique market information, proof, terminology, currency/legal notes or local buyer questions.

## Validation workflow for each keyword
Record:
1. Exact query and variant.
2. Country, language and device.
3. Search intent (transactional / commercial investigation / informational / navigational).
4. Current SERP composition and 5–10 competing URLs.
5. Search volume, trend and keyword difficulty from a named tool/source and capture date.
6. Competitor page type, depth, offer, price visibility, proof and CTA.
7. Fit with ICP, service delivery, sales value and close probability.
8. Priority score with rationale; chosen primary keyword and supporting cluster.
9. Page assigned; cannibalization check; title/H1/meta/URL recommendation.
10. Status: Proposed → SERP checked → Metrics validated → Approved → Mapped.

## Scoring rubric (use consistently)
Score 1–5 and document evidence: commercial intent (30%), ICP fit (25%), delivery fit (20%), achievable SERP gap (15%), evidence/proof readiness (10%). Search volume is a sizing input, not a substitute for commercial fit. Do not select a high-volume term if it attracts the wrong buyer.

## Do not do
- No keyword stuffing or repetitive “AI SEO / GEO / AEO / LLM SEO” lists.
- No search-volume or KD claims without a source/date.
- No guarantees of ranking, citations or AI recommendations.
- No one-page-per-synonym strategy.
