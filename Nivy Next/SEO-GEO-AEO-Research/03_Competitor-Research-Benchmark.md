## Cross-competitor patterns
1. **Service definition:** The strongest pages define GEO as improving the chance of being found, cited or recommended by AI answer systems, not a magic replacement for SEO.
2. **Specific deliverables:** Audits, prompt-set baselines, page rewrites, schema, entity consistency, off-site corroboration and recurring measurement are named explicitly.
3. **Buyer clarity:** Several pages publish packages or price ranges; others provide a free audit or entry product to reduce risk.
4. **Measurement:** Competitors describe tracking prompts, citation/share-of-voice, visibility and competitor comparisons. Nivy Next should document a repeatable method and distinguish observed changes from causal proof.
5. **Claims require skepticism:** Large percentages, citation counts and testimonials are marketing claims unless supported by methodology, source data and client evidence.
6. **SEO and GEO overlap:** Technical accessibility, useful content, clear entities, source citations and authority remain central; GEO should be positioned as an integrated extension of SEO, not a guaranteed shortcut.

## Recommended Nivy Next positioning hypothesis
**“SEO + AI Search Visibility for growth-stage B2B service businesses.”**
- Start with a clear primary service: SEO foundations + AI search visibility improvements.
- Offer a defined baseline: agreed query/prompt set, engines, country/language, competitors and date.
- Deliver an implementation queue: technical access, page clarity, schema/entity consistency, useful evidence-backed content, internal links and off-site credibility.
- Re-test the same prompt set periodically and report visibility/citations alongside Search Console, analytics and qualified leads where available.
- State explicitly that no provider can guarantee a particular AI answer, citation, ranking or recommendation.

## Competitor page review checklist
For each competitor and target country, record:
- Target ICP / industry / company size
- Main service and page URL
- Primary keyword and search intent
- Offer packaging, price, minimum term, cancellation and CTA
- Deliverables, exclusions and turnaround
- Case studies with client name, timeframe, baseline and outcome
- Reviews/directories and evidence of reputation
- Page structure, FAQs, schema clues, internal links and supporting content
- AI visibility claims and measurement methodology
- Gaps Nivy Next can address with real capabilities
- Source URL, date checked and confidence level
