## Content changes to apply to every priority service page
1. One explicit H1 that names the service and audience; keep benefit-led copy as supporting text.
2. First 100 words: define the service, who it is for, key outcome, and what is actually delivered.
3. Include a concise “What is included” list with measurable deliverables and boundaries.
4. Show process, expected inputs, reporting cadence, timelines as estimates, and dependencies.
5. Add real evidence: named/permissioned case studies, portfolio samples, staff credentials, reviews. Never fabricate proof.
6. Answer buyer questions directly with specific, quotable passages and FAQs.
7. Add relevant internal links: service hub ↔ service page ↔ case study ↔ FAQ/resource ↔ contact.
8. Add unique title/meta description, one canonical URL, logical headings, descriptive image alt text, and appropriate structured data.
9. Use US/UK spelling variants naturally where relevant; do not create duplicate pages solely for spelling.
10. Remove unsupported statistics, unverified “trusted by” language, placeholder testimonials and invented client outcomes.

## Canonical URL and consolidation rules
- One primary page per commercial intent.
- Do not make separate SEO, AI SEO, LLM SEO, GEO and AEO pages unless the SERPs show distinct intent and each page has unique scope. Start with one strong SEO page plus one dedicated GEO/AEO page.
- Redirect only after confirming an existing URL, backlinks, traffic and replacement page. Never redirect based on this plan alone.
- Preserve existing useful URLs and content; update the register before implementation.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every page has a single primary keyword theme, audience, intent, CTA and owner.
- No orphan commercial pages, duplicate intent pages, placeholder proof or unsupported claims.
- All proposed pages are marked Existing / Drafted / Approved / Published / Redirected with evidence and URL.
