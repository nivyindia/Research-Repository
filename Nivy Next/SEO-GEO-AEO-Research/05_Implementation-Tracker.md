## Status definitions
- **Not started** — no work completed.
- **In progress** — active work, partial evidence exists.
- **Needs decision** — blocked by founder/commercial choice.
- **Ready for review** — draft complete, needs verification.
- **Approved** — evidence and owner sign-off recorded.
- **Implemented** — approved change applied to the intended asset.
- **Validated** — post-change result checked and recorded.

## Definition of done for a page
- Primary keyword and intent validated for target country.
- Search competitors and content gap documented.
- H1, title, meta, URL and section outline approved.
- All factual claims and proof verified.
- Service scope, exclusions, CTA and next step are clear.
- Internal links, schema recommendation and measurement plan documented.
- Cannibalization and redirect risks reviewed.
- Draft version and approval recorded; live implementation tracked separately.
